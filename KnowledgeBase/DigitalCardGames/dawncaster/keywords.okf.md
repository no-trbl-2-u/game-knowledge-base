## Keyword definitions

### Affliction

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Various
- **Definition:** A negative effect such as Poison, Bleeding or Dazed. Most Afflictions are additive. They can be prevented with a Ward, or removed by Cleansing
- **Source:** src-001

### Ambush

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Triggers if this card is the first card played of the round. (Reliable cards also trigger this effect)
- **Source:** src-001

### Ancestral

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Ancestral effects are active while you have 4 or more cards in your Discard Pile
- **Source:** src-001

### Anger

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Buff, Offense
- **Definition:** Increases the damage dealt by Actions by 1 per stack of Anger. (The damage bonus from Anger can, in turn, be doubled by Rampage)
- **Source:** src-001

### Armor

- **Type:** Other
- **Functions:** Defense
- **Definition:** When dealt damage, take 1 less damage per stack of Armor. Armor fades at the start of the turn
- **Source:** src-001

### Ascend

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Various
- **Definition:** Ascend to a higher form, gaining more powerful buffs for each Ascended level. You can Ascend up to 3 levels
- **Source:** src-001

### Ascended

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Various
- **Definition:** Ascended effects are active while you are currently in an Ascended form
- **Source:** src-001

### Attune

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** If you are not Attuned already, attune to a random Element. Attunements empower Actions that share their type
- **Source:** src-001

### Balance

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** The remaining number of cards in your deck and remaining Health are both even numbered
- **Source:** src-001

### Bane

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Debuff
- **Definition:** Whenever you gain an Affliction (except Dazzled, Jinxed or Shocked), gain an additional stack of that Affliction for each point of Bane
- **Source:** src-001

### Barrier

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Defense
- **Definition:** When dealt damage, take 1 less non-Piercing damage per stack of Barrier. Barrier does not fade, and can be Dispelled
- **Source:** src-001

### Basic Attack

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** Play your Basic Weapon attack. You start the game with several copies of your class-specific Basic Weapon, which may be relatively weak compared to later cards. If you are Disarmed, you cannot play Basic Attack cards. You can pay the Blacksmith 50 Gold to Upgrade all of your Basic Attacks, or 30 Gold to permanently Remove a Basic Attack from your deck. Some rare cards allow you to Swap all your Basic Weapon cards for another
- **Source:** src-001

### Beastform

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Various
- **Definition:** 4 possible Beastforms provide various powerful buffs (Bear, Cat, Raven, or Dragon). Only 1 Beastform can be active at a time: activating a new Beastform will replace the old one. As a Channeling card, Beastform can be extended by 1 round by having at least 1 Energy at the end of your turn, and ends when you get Interrupted
- **Source:** src-001

### Bleeding

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** When dealt damage, take 1 additional damage per stack of Bleeding, then reduce Bleeding stacks by 1
- **Source:** src-001

### Blessing

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Various
- **Definition:** A beneficial effect such as Regenerate, Focus or Chain. Most Blessings are additive. They can be removed by Dispelling them
- **Source:** src-001

### Blood

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Blood Ritual, Energy Management
- **Definition:** Sacrifice your own blood instead of Energy in order to gain special powers. Goes hand in hand with Corruption, Darkness, Hexes, and Lifedrain
- **Source:** src-001

### Bloodlust

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** Bloodlust requires the enemy to have the Bleeding Affliction. (Reliable cards also trigger the Bloodlust effect, even when the enemy isn't Bleeding)
- **Source:** src-001

### Brittle

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** When dealt damage, remove Brittle. If the damage was dealt from a Melee Action, take 1 more damage per stack removed
- **Source:** src-001

### Burning

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** At the end of the turn, take 1 damage per stack of Burning
- **Source:** src-001

### Bury

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Removes the top card of your deck from combat. Persistent cards cannot be Buried
- **Source:** src-001

### Cascade

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Energy Management
- **Definition:** Triggers when played after a card that costs 1 more or 1 less Energy. (Reliable cards also trigger this effect)
- **Source:** src-001

### Chain

- **Type:** Blessing, Effect
- **Functions:** Buff, Offense
- **Definition:** Increase the damage of the next action by 1 per stack. Chain fades if a played card did not increase Chain. Activating Chains plays a key role in raising the Tide
- **Source:** src-001

### Challenger

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** -
- **Definition:** A Challenger has to be selected first. Opportunities return to the area deck when a Challenger is selected
- **Source:** src-001

### Channel

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Energy Management
- **Definition:** A channeling effect can be extended by 1 round by having at least 1 Energy at the end of your turn. Channeled cards end when you get Interrupted
- **Source:** src-001

### Chaos

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Requires an odd number of cards remaining in your deck
- **Source:** src-001

### Charges

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Cards with Charges can be played X number of times per combat before they become Depleted and are removed from combat
- **Source:** src-001

### Charmed

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Instakill
- **Definition:** Lose the battle if your Health is less than your Charmed stack. When dealt non-Piercing damage, remove an equal amount of Charmed
- **Source:** src-001

### Cleanse

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Healing
- **Definition:** Removes an Affliction
- **Source:** src-001

### Codes

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Various
- **Definition:** Codes provide a strict rule for your deck. If it is met at the Start of Combat, a code is put directly in play. If not, it gets removed. Codes are Unique and Grounded: only one Code with that name can be played per combat, and Codes can't be altered, copied or Imbued
- **Source:** src-001

### Combat Fatigue

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** Whenever your deck runs out of cards, you increase your Fatigue by 1, then take 10% of your Maximum Health per Fatigue in unpreventable damage
- **Source:** src-001

### Conjure

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Creates a temporary card that is always Removed from your deck after use or after the combat ends, even if it has non-Removable properties such as Cursed
- **Source:** src-001

### Continuity

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Triggers when played after a card of the same type (melee, magic, ranged, divine). (Reliable cards also trigger this effect)
- **Source:** src-001

### Corrupted

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Blood Ritual, Deck Management
- **Definition:** 4 or more Corruptions are present in your deck, hand and/or Discard Pile
- **Source:** src-001

### Corruption

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Blood Ritual
- **Definition:** Corruptions are cards with negative effects to their owner (Corruptions may have a negative effect when drawn, even if they are not played. Some Corruptions are unplayable (such as Envy, Greed, or Lust). If your deck contains 4 or more Corruptions, you become Corrupted, which unlocks special effects on many Blood Ritual cards.)
- **Source:** src-001

### Critical Hit

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** Critical Hits deal double damage. You can't inflict Critical Hits while Afflicted by Weakness. (Critical Hits can be buffed by the Ferocious Blessing)
- **Source:** src-001

### Crushing

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** Crushing requires a card to deal 10 or more damage
- **Source:** src-001

### Cursed

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Cursed cards can't be Transmuted or Removed from your deck. However, they can be transformed into a card of equal rarity at a Shrine of Commerce. Conjured and One Use cards are always Removed from your deck after use or after the combat ends, even if they are Cursed
- **Source:** src-001

### Darkness

- **Type:** Other
- **Functions:** Blood Ritual
- **Definition:** When dealt damage, take 1 less damage per stack of Darkness. When Darkness runs out, gain Stunned. Your Maximum Health is lowered by your total Darkness
- **Source:** src-001

### Dazed

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Defense
- **Definition:** When playing a card, decrease Dazed by 1. When Dazed reaches 0, gain Stunned. Dazed fades at the end of the turn
- **Source:** src-001

### Dazzled

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Defense
- **Definition:** Draw one fewer card per stack of Dazzled, then decrease Dazzled stacks by 1 per skipped draw. Stacks of Dazzled cannot be increased
- **Source:** src-001

### Deep Wound

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense, Instakill
- **Definition:** Inflicts Bleeding every turn. A target who has sustained 5 or more Deep Wounds is instantly slain
- **Source:** src-001

### Delirious

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Chaos, Energy Management
- **Definition:** After playing a card, shuffle the costs of all cards in hand. Lasts 1 round per stack
- **Source:** src-001

### Delve

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Select 1 of 3 randomly selected cards
- **Source:** src-001

### Deplete

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Depleted cards are removed from combat. Deplete triggers when the final Charge of a card with Charges is spent, or when Equipment has used up its Durability
- **Source:** src-001

### Disarmed

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Defense
- **Definition:** While Disarmed, Basic Attack cards can't be played. Lasts 1 round per stack
- **Source:** src-001

### Discard

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Removes a card from the hand
- **Source:** src-001

### Discard Pile

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** After you play a card, it gets moved to the Discard Pile. Once your deck is empty, your Discard Pile is shuffled back into your deck
- **Source:** src-001

### Dispel

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Debuff
- **Definition:** Removes a Blessing from the target
- **Source:** src-001

### Dominated

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Chaos, Deck Management
- **Definition:** While Dominated, you can only play the randomly selected card in your hand. Exception: Reliable cards can still be played
- **Source:** src-001

### Doom

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** At the end of your turn, take 1 damage for each stack of Doom. Increase Doom by 1 each time an Action is played
- **Source:** src-001

### Dragon

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** -
- **Definition:** -
- **Source:** src-001

### Durability

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** The number of times Equipment can be activated before it's Depleted for the remainder of the combat
- **Source:** src-001

### Echo

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Cards with Echo are cast twice when played from your hand
- **Source:** src-001

### Empower

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Energy Management
- **Definition:** Empower is active while you have 5 or more Energy
- **Source:** src-001

### Evasion

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Defense, Immunity
- **Definition:** When dealt Melee damage, reduce the first damage taken to 0, then decrease Evasion by 1. Evasion fades at the start of the turn
- **Source:** src-001

### Execute

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Triggers if the card with Execute is active when you defeat your foe, including if you use this card to defeat your foe
- **Source:** src-001

### Ferocious

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Buff, Healing, Offense
- **Definition:** Your next Critical Hit deals 1 additional damage and heals you for 1 Health per stack of Ferocious
- **Source:** src-001

### Figmented

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Various
- **Definition:** Conjures a copy of the card when played.
- **Source:** src-001

### Finale

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Triggers when played with 2 or fewer cards remaining in hand. (Reliable cards also trigger this effect, regardless of how many cards are left in hand)
- **Source:** src-001

### Firecast

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** A card with Firecast is played and moved into the discard pile whenever it gets Buried on your turn
- **Source:** src-001

### Flanking

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Triggers when this card is played from either the leftmost or the rightmost position in hand. (Reliable cards also trigger this effect, regardless of their position in hand)
- **Source:** src-001

### Flow

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Triggers when played after playing at least X number of cards during the turn. (Reliable cards also trigger this effect, regardless of how many cards have been played during the turn)
- **Source:** src-001

### Focus

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** At the start of the turn, draw an additional card if you have 5 or more Focus
- **Source:** src-001

### Foretell

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Look at the top X cards of your deck. Put 1 on top, and the others at the bottom of your deck
- **Source:** src-001

### Form

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Various
- **Definition:** Forms transform your appearance. Only 1 Form can be active at a time: activating a new Form will replace the old one
- **Source:** src-001

### Frenzy

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Buff
- **Definition:** Triggers when you've taken damage during your turn. (Reliable cards also trigger this effect, regardless of whether you've taken damage on your turn)
- **Source:** src-001

### Frozen

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Look at the top X cards of your deck. Put one on top, and the others at the bottom of your deck
- **Source:** src-001

### Grounded

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Grounded cards can't be altered, copied or Imbued
- **Source:** src-001

### Haste

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Energy Management
- **Definition:** Reduce the cost of the next drawn card by 1 Energy, then decrease Haste by 1
- **Source:** src-001

### Health

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Defense, Healing
- **Definition:** If you lose all your Health, you lose the game! If your enemy loses all their Health, you win the battle! Some cards add or remove Health points, while others increase or decrease Maximum Health
- **Source:** src-001

### Heavy

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Heavy cards do not get Reshuffled after your deck reaches 0 cards
- **Source:** src-001

### Hex

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Blood Ritual, Debuff
- **Definition:** Hexes are powerful effects that debilitate foes in different ways
- **Source:** src-001

### Holy

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Holy Energy, Energy Management
- **Definition:** Holy Energy can be spent on powerful Divine cards. Goes hand in hand with Zeal
- **Source:** src-001

### Imbued

- **Type:** Other
- **Functions:** Buff, Deck Management
- **Definition:** Imbuing an Enchantment Removes an Enchantment from your deck and activates it permanently, from the start of every combat. In order to be Imbued, the Enchantment card must already be present in your deck, and must last for the entire combat when activated. Grounded cards and Codes cannot be Imbued. Imbuing is expensive, often costing 100 Gold
- **Source:** src-001

### Impervious

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Defense, Immunity
- **Definition:** While Impervious, take no non-Piercing damage. When dealt damage on the enemy turn, take no non-Piercing damage and decrease Impervious by 1
- **Source:** src-001

### Imprint

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Add a card to this card's memory
- **Source:** src-001

### Infected

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Offense
- **Definition:** Whenever you discard a card, take 1 damage for every point of Infected. Remove all Infected whenever you gain Health
- **Source:** src-001

### Infuse

- **Type:** Other
- **Functions:** Energy Management, Upgrade, Offense
- **Definition:** When you Infuse a card, you convert its energy cost to Neutral (colorless). Also, if the card is Upgradeable, Infusing it will Upgrade the card as many times as its cost in energy
- **Source:** src-001

### Infusion

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Upgrade, Offense
- **Definition:** When you add a card with Infusion to your deck, Upgrade all other cards with Infusion by 1
- **Source:** src-001

### Insight

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Defense, Immunity
- **Definition:** Whenever you are dealt an amount of non-Piercing damage exactly the same as your current Insight, prevent that damage
- **Source:** src-001

### Interrupt

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Energy Management
- **Definition:** Removes the target's remaining Energy (and ends their Channeling effects, including Beastform). If it targets an enemy, it will force them to replace their Deadly attacks with their regular cards
- **Source:** src-001

### Jinxed

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Debuff, Deck Management
- **Definition:** The next Action card played has no effect and reduces Jinxed by 1. (Exception: Reliable cards still have an effect.) Jinxed stacks fade at the end of the turn, and cannot be increased by Bane.
- **Source:** src-001

### Ki

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Debuff, Deck Management
- **Definition:** If you start the turn with more Ki than Health, remove all Ki and gain Stunned
- **Source:** src-001

### Lasting

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Buff, Deck Management
- **Definition:** The effects of Lasting cards are extended by 1 turn whenever the card is played again
- **Source:** src-001

### Lifedrain

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Blood Ritual, Healing
- **Definition:** Cards with Lifedrain restore Health equal to the damage they deal
- **Source:** src-001

### Memorized

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** This card always starts at the top of your deck
- **Source:** src-001

### Mergecraft

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Upgrade, Offense
- **Definition:** When Mergecraft cards are added to the deck, they Upgrade all copies of that card by their Mergecraft value
- **Source:** src-001

### Momentum

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Buff, Deck Management
- **Definition:** Whenever you have 5 or more Momentum, remove all stacks and draw a card
- **Source:** src-001

### Monster Card

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Various
- **Definition:** All enemy cards are monster cards, and can be recognized by their deep red banner
- **Source:** src-001

### On Draw

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Triggers whenever this card is drawn from your deck
- **Source:** src-001

### On Hit

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Buff
- **Definition:** Triggers when this attack hits the target, and deals damage
- **Source:** src-001

### One Use

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** This card will be permanently Removed from your deck after playing it, even if it has non-Removable properties such as Cursed
- **Source:** src-001

### Order

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Requires an even number of cards remaining in your deck
- **Source:** src-001

### Overkill

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Whenever a foe dies to damage, "Overkill" refers to their negative Health total. (For example, if you do 13 damage to an enemy who has 10 Health left with no defenses, they will die at –3 Health, which is the same as 3 Overkill)
- **Source:** src-001

### Perform

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Buff
- **Definition:** Increase your current Performance by X, or start a new Performance
- **Source:** src-001

### Performance

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Buff
- **Definition:** Performance counts the progress of your currently active song. While no song is active, your Performance is 0. Performance stacks are increased by Performing
- **Source:** src-001

### Persistent

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Cards with Persistent don't get Discarded at the end of the turn and cannot be Buried
- **Source:** src-001

### Piercing

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** Piercing damage ignores all effects that would reduce or prevent its damage (such as Charmed, Impervious, Insight, and Resilient)
- **Source:** src-001

### Pinned

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Chaos, Deck Management
- **Definition:** While Pinned, only the leftmost and rightmost cards in hand can be played. (Exception: Reliable cards can still be played, regardless of their position in your hand.) Lasts 1 round per stack
- **Source:** src-001

### Poison

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** After playing a card, take 1 damage, then decrease Poison by 1. When gaining Health, prevent 1 Health per stack and reduce Poison by 1 for each Health prevented
- **Source:** src-001

### Precision

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** The target ends the attack at an amount of total Health ending in 0 or 5
- **Source:** src-001

### Rally

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Healing
- **Definition:** Whenever your opponent plays a card, gain Health equal to your Rally, then lower your Rally by 1
- **Source:** src-001

### Rampage

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Buff, Offense
- **Definition:** Cards with Rampage gain double the damage bonus from Anger
- **Source:** src-001

### Reaping

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** Reaping lowers the target's Maximum Health equal to the damage it deals. When used against creatures who benefit from Anima, it lowers Anima instead. When used by creatures who benefit from Anima, Reaping damage is added to the Anima instead
- **Source:** src-001

### Rebound

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Rebound counts the number of times you played the card, and triggers its special effect when the card has been played X number of times
- **Source:** src-001

### Reflect

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** Return the next damage dealt to you to the attacker. Increase this damage by 1 for each stack of Reflect. Fades at the start of your turn
- **Source:** src-001

### Regenerate

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Healing
- **Definition:** At the start of your turn, gain 1 Health per stack and decrease Regenerate by 1
- **Source:** src-001

### Reliable

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** The Ambush, Bloodlust, Cascade, Continuity, Finale, Flanking, Flow, and Frenzy conditions are always met. Reliable cards ignore disabling conditions like Crushing, Jinxed, Stunned, Pinned or Dominated
- **Source:** src-001

### Removed

- **Type:** Other
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Cards that are removed from your deck are permanently eliminated. You can Remove cards at Campfires, the Shrine of Potential, and some special events. (You can also pay the Blacksmith 30 Gold to Remove a Basic Attack.) Cursed cards cannot be removed from your deck. Generally, cards that are removed from your hand during combat are only Discarded, not Removed from your deck entirely. However, Conjured or One Use cards are always Removed after they are activated
- **Source:** src-001

### Reshuffle

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Chaos, Deck Management
- **Definition:** Whenever your deck runs out of cards, your Discard Pile and the cards you have played (except for Conjure or One Use cards) return to your deck in a random order
- **Source:** src-001

### Resilience

- **Type:** Blessing
- **Functions:** Defense
- **Definition:** When dealt damage, take 1 less non-Piercing damage per stack of Resilience, then lower your Resilience by 1
- **Source:** src-001

### Revelation

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Holy Energy
- **Definition:** Revelations offer incredibly powerful and Unique effects
- **Source:** src-001

### Scholar

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Triggers if your deck contained 20 or more cards at the start of combat
- **Source:** src-001

### Shift

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** Assume a new Form. Only one Form can be active at a time: activating a new Form will replace the old one
- **Source:** src-001

### Shocked

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Deck Management
- **Definition:** At the start of the turn, reduce Shocked by 1. When Shocked reaches 0, you are Stunned. The stacks of this Affliction cannot be increased
- **Source:** src-001

### Sickened

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Energy Management
- **Definition:** While Sickened, you lose 1 random Energy at the start of your turn. Lasts 1 round per stack
- **Source:** src-001

### Silenced

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Deck Management, Defense
- **Definition:** While Silenced, Magic Actions can't be played. Lasts 1 round per stack
- **Source:** src-001

### Sinister

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Buff, Deck Management
- **Definition:** Sinister cards get more powerful for each other Sinister card you played during the entire combat
- **Source:** src-001

### Slay

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Offense
- **Definition:** Triggers when an enemy is reduced to exactly 0 Health (no Overkill)
- **Source:** src-001

### Slow

- **Type:** Affliction
- **Functions:** Energy Management
- **Definition:** Increase the cost of the next drawn card by 1 Neutral Energy, then decrease Slow by 1
- **Source:** src-001

### Souls

- **Type:** Effect
- **Functions:** Blood Ritual, Healing
- **Definition:** Use the lingering essence of slain foes to empower Actions. When you die with over 100 souls collected, revive and lose all Souls
- **Source:** src-001

### Stagger

- **Type:** Affliction
