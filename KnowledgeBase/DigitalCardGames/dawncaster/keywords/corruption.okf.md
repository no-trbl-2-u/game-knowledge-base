## Definition

Corruptions are cards with negative effects to their owner (Corruptions may have a negative effect when drawn, even if they are not played. Some Corruptions are unplayable (such as Envy, Greed, or Lust). If your deck contains 4 or more Corruptions, you become Corrupted, which unlocks special effects on many Blood Ritual cards.)
