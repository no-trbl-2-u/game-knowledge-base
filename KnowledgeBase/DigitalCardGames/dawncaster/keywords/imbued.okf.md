## Definition

Imbuing an Enchantment Removes an Enchantment from your deck and activates it permanently, from the start of every combat. In order to be Imbued, the Enchantment card must already be present in your deck, and must last for the entire combat when activated. Grounded cards and Codes cannot be Imbued. Imbuing is expensive, often costing 100 Gold
