## Definition

4 possible Beastforms provide various powerful buffs (Bear, Cat, Raven, or Dragon). Only 1 Beastform can be active at a time: activating a new Beastform will replace the old one. As a Channeling card, Beastform can be extended by 1 round by having at least 1 Energy at the end of your turn, and ends when you get Interrupted
