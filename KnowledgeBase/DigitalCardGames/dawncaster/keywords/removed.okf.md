## Definition

Cards that are removed from your deck are permanently eliminated. You can Remove cards at Campfires, the Shrine of Potential, and some special events. (You can also pay the Blacksmith 30 Gold to Remove a Basic Attack.) Cursed cards cannot be removed from your deck. Generally, cards that are removed from your hand during combat are only Discarded, not Removed from your deck entirely. However, Conjured or One Use cards are always Removed after they are activated
