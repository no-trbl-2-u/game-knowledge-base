## Definition

Play your Basic Weapon attack. You start the game with several copies of your class-specific Basic Weapon, which may be relatively weak compared to later cards. If you are Disarmed, you cannot play Basic Attack cards. You can pay the Blacksmith 50 Gold to Upgrade all of your Basic Attacks, or 30 Gold to permanently Remove a Basic Attack from your deck.
