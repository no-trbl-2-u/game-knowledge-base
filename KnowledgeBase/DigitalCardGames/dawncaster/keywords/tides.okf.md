## Definition

Tides shift the effects and power of certain Tidal cards. Generally speaking, Low Tide is the default state, but activating multiple Chains or a card that change the Tide can trigger High Tide, which unlocks more powerful effects. In a combat, only one side can enjoy the High Tide: if your opponent activates High Tide, you will go back to Low Tide.
