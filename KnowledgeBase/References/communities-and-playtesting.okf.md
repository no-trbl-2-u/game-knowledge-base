## Summary

Where board-game (and specifically deckbuilder) designers get structured
feedback. Two venue families: **live playtest networks** (Protospiel events,
Protospiel Online, Break My Game and Virtual Playtesting Discords) and
**asynchronous critique forums** (BGG Board Game Design forum, BGDF,
r/tabletopgamedesign). The common operating norm across all of them is
reciprocity — designers earn playtests by playtesting others' games.

## Source-backed facts

  Protospiel Online runs the same format virtually over Discord voice/video
  plus a virtual tabletop of the designer's choice.

  Confidence: medium

## Implications for designers

- A digital-first deckbuilder can still use these venues: Protospiel Online
  and Break My Game accept virtual-tabletop prototypes, and the mechanism
  vocabulary (thinning, buy rows, engine pacing) is shared across board and
  digital forms.
- Forums reward specific questions ("does this reward-draft rate cause
  bloat?") over show-and-tell; bring a one-page rules summary and a testable
  claim.
- Reciprocity is the entry fee everywhere; budget time to test other
  people's games.
