Board Game Knowledge Base

A daily scout-built corpus for tabletop rules, player reception, and “this game would be better if…” design opportunities.

Project scaffold created · integration deferred · gathering knowledge first

Purpose

Each run studies one board game, locates official rules where possible, breaks those rules into categorized OKF documents, and gathers review/forum reception with special attention to desired improvements.

Source Law

  • Publisher/product page first.
  • Official rulebook, FAQ, and errata next.
  • BoardGameGeek for discovery, metadata, files, forums, and review leads.
  • Third-party reviews as reception evidence, not rules authority.

Daily Cadence

Target schedule: once daily at 06:00 UTC. The scout chooses one game, researches it, and writes a new directory under games/<slug>/.

Better-if Focus

Every game entry should preserve improvement pressure. Look for complaints and proposals around onboarding, setup, downtime, ambiguity, randomness, scoring, combat, solo/co-op automation, campaign progression, balance, and accessibility.

The point is not worship. It is judgment. What worked, what failed, and what should have been sharper.
