## Summary

Every round has a fixed phase order, but the player-turn phase permits a choice between Agent and Reveal turns.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A round has five phases: Round Start, Player Turns, Combat, Makers, and Recall.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 7 lists the five phases in order.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: During Player Turns, players alternate Agent and Reveal turns; after a player takes a Reveal turn, that player's turns are skipped for the remainder of the phase.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 7–8 explains the choice and the skip condition.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The round begins by revealing a Conflict card and drawing five cards.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 7 gives both Round Start steps.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The phase sequence creates a visible clock, while the Reveal decision controls when a player stops claiming spaces and converts their remaining hand into effects, Persuasion, and combat strength.

## Player friction

Revealing early is legal but costly to teach: a player must understand what they surrender in board access before they can judge the timing.
