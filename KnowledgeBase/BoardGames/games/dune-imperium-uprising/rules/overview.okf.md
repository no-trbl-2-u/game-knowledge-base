## Summary

Uprising's core loop combines card-gated worker placement with a five-phase round and a public Conflict race.

## Rules / Mechanics

The game is a race to 10 or more Victory Points, with Conflict cards providing a hard deck-based clock. Cards are not only economic objects: Agent icons determine where an Agent may go, while Reveal effects and Persuasion shape the rest of the round.

## Player friction

The rules ask players to parse icon matching, costs, influence thresholds, hidden Intrigue timing, and combat deployment in a single loop.
