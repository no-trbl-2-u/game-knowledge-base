## Summary

The following are evidence-linked design opportunities, not final mechanics verdicts.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The rulebook makes sandworm access depend on Maker Hooks and the Shield Wall state.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rules pp. 10 and 17 specify both prerequisites.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Ryan Board Games reports added complexity and six-player downtime.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: The review's “What Doesn't” section says the spy, sandworm, Shield Wall, Maker Hooks, and contract rules add cognitive overhead and that six players create too much downtime.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Ryan Board Games reports sandworm reward doubling can make one investment path feel dominant.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: The review says the player investing in sandworms pulled ahead decisively in many of its plays and that ignoring sandworms can feel like a mistake.
  Confidence: medium

## Better-if opportunities

1. **Onboarding** — A first-round teaching script could isolate one Agent turn, one Spy choice, one Reveal, and one Conflict before introducing the CHOAM module.
   Evidence basis: the rulebook's layered prerequisites and the review's added-complexity report.
   Confidence: medium

2. **Combat resolution / balance-faction-asymmetry** — A player aid could foreground Maker Hooks, Shield Wall state, sandworm strength, and reward doubling before Conflict commitment.
   Evidence basis: official combat restrictions plus the review's sandworm-balance testimony.
   Confidence: medium

3. **Downtime** — The six-player team mode could use simultaneous planning prompts and a compact turn-order aid.
   Evidence basis: Ryan Board Games' report of too much six-player downtime.
   Confidence: medium

4. **Solo-coop-automation** — Preserve the explicit Rival difficulty ladder in a quick-reference aid rather than forcing first-time solo players to discover the mode's decision grammar during play.
   Evidence basis: Ryan Board Games' positive report on improved Rivals and the rulebook's separate player-count supplements.
   Confidence: low

## Player friction

The opportunities above are deliberately narrow. They address teachability and information timing, not a claim that the underlying system is mechanically unsound.

## Praised design

Any intervention should preserve the Spy choice between infiltration and card draw, which the review identifies as a genuine improvement.

## Open questions

- The sandworm-balance signal is a single reviewer's testimony and needs broader independent corroboration before being treated as a general balance finding.
