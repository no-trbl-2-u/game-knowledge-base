# Edge Cases, FAQ, and Errata

## Authority boundary

The FAQ covers the base game and all three expansions. Only its base-game errata/FAQ on PDF pages 1-3 and generally applicable secrecy clarification on PDF page 10 are summarized here; expansion-specific rulings are excluded.

## Incorporated printing corrections

- Claim: The corrected online rulebook already uses Laura Roslin, Gaius Baltar, then Tom Zarek for setup presidency priority, matching the FAQ correction.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Corrected rulebook p. 5, "Distribute Title Cards"; compare FAQ v2.1 p. 1, "The Rulebook."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Only one Executive Order may be used per turn.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 1, "The 'Executive Order' Card."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Super Crisis cards are immune to character abilities affecting either Crisis cards or skill checks.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 1, rulebook p. 19 correction.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: When a title is lost, the eligible player highest in its succession takes it; this is not necessarily the next seated player.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 1, rulebook p. 28 correction.
  Confidence: high

## Base-game rulings

- Claim: A reroll retains Strategic Planning's +2 modifier, and a modified d8 result is bounded to 1-8 after all modifiers.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 pp. 1 and 3, "Characters" and "Miscellaneous."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Revealing as a Cylon during the first action of an Executive Order cancels the second action, after which the original current player continues the turn.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 2, "Cylon Players."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A revealed Cylon cannot use unrevealed Loyalty-card actions and cannot be targeted by Executive Order or Quorum cards; any piloted viper returns to Reserves on reveal.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 2, "Cylon Players."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A character in the Brig may take actions, but movement and skill-check participation are restricted; a forced move to Sickbay does not remove that character from the Brig.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 2, "The Brig and Sickbay."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Civilian-ship faces viewed through Communications remain secret, and the current player chooses which of multiple piloted vipers in one area a raider attacks.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 2, "Miscellaneous."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: If all four centurion markers are already on the board, a boarding heavy raider remains because component limits prevent placing another centurion; multiple centurions may share a track space.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 pp. 2-3, "Miscellaneous."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Damaging FTL Control does not stop jump-track advancement or auto-jump, and Command may activate the same unmanned viper repeatedly.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 3, base-game miscellaneous questions.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A nuke's 7-8 result destroys normal raiders, not heavy raiders; a revealed Sympathizer remains in play but is excluded from random Loyalty-card inspection.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 3, base-game miscellaneous questions.
  Confidence: high

## Secrecy clarification

- Claim: Players may describe a contributed Skill card only in polar terms such as high or low, while the number of cards contributed is open information; exact types, colors, strengths, and contributed card abilities are not discloseable.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 10, "Secrecy Golden Rule," "Skill Checks," and "Skill Card Abilities."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Hand sizes and the sizes of Skill decks, Quorum deck, and Destiny deck are open; discard top cards are open but discard piles may not be counted.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 10, "Hand and Deck Size."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A viewed Loyalty card or deck-top card cannot be described specifically, and a viewed civilian ship may only be characterized as containing a lot or a little resources.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: FAQ v2.1 p. 10, "Loyalty Cards," "Top Card of the Deck," and "Civilian Ships."
  Confidence: high
