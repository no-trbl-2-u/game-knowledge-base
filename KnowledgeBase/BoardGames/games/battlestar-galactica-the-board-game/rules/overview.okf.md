# Rules Overview

## Edition and authority

This record covers only the English 2008 base game using Fantasy Flight Games' corrected 32-page online rulebook. Pegasus, Exodus, Daybreak, their modules, and their components are excluded.

- Claim: The game is team-based, but each player's human or Cylon team is secret and team members win or lose together.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 2, "Game Overview": players are secretly assigned to human or Cylon teams and "win or lose with the other members of their team."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Human players pursue the Kobol objective; Cylon players prevent it by exhausting a resource, completing a centurion invasion, or destroying Galactica.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 6, 12, 14, and 18, "Object of the Game," "Human Objective," "Kobol Objective Card," and "Cylon Objective."
  Confidence: high

## System map

- Claim: A normal human turn proceeds through Receive Skills, Movement, Action, Crisis, Cylon Ship Activation when shown, and Jump Preparation when shown.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 9, "Game Turn," lists six ordered steps.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Crisis cards create one of three resolution forms: Cylon attack, skill check, or event.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 10-11, "Crisis Step," identifies Cylon attack crises, skill-check crises, and event crises.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Skill checks mix two random Destiny cards with facedown player contributions; matching skill strengths add, nonmatching strengths subtract, and the resulting total determines pass, partial pass where printed, or fail.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 16-17, "Skill Checks" and "Partial Passes," steps 1-6.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: At four distance the Sleeper Agent phase deals each player, including revealed Cylons, another Loyalty card; the Kobol card governs this threshold.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 14 and 19, "Kobol Objective Card" and "Sleeper Agent Phase."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Combat uses finite ships and tokens; Cylon ship behavior is automated by crisis icons while players command or pilot vipers and may risk raptors outside combat.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 21-28, "Ship Reference," "Combat," "Piloting Vipers," "Raptors and Risking," and "Component Limitations."
  Confidence: high

## Information boundary

- Claim: Players may accuse or lie about Loyalty information, but may not reveal exact Skill-card strengths or identify their post-check contributions; deck-top information also cannot be disclosed specifically.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 20, "Secrecy," especially "Open Accusations," "Skill Cards and Skill Checks," and "Cards in the Decks."
  Confidence: high
