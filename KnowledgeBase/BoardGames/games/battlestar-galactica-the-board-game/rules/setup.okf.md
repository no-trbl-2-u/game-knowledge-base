# Setup and Complete Base-Game Components

## Product scope and component denominator

The finite denominator is **34 leaf component categories** explicitly exposed by the official rulebook's box inventory on page 2: stand-alone listed items plus each disclosed subtype under cardboard tokens, large cards, small cards, and plastic ships. Parent rollups (52 tokens, 110 large cards, 128 small cards, and 32 ships) are checksums, not extra denominator rows. The arithmetic is 3 standalone categories + 11 token subtypes + 6 large-card subtypes + 7 small-card subtypes + 1 die + 4 ship subtypes + 2 plastic accessory types = 34. Expansions, promotional material, replacement parts, and inferred card distributions are excluded.

## Base-game setup sequence

- Claim: Install the four dials once; start food and fuel at 8, morale at 10, and population at 12.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 5, "Installing Resource Dials" and Setup step 1.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Put eight vipers and four raptors in Reserves, the fleet marker at the jump-track start, and all remaining tokens facedown where applicable beside the board.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 5, Setup step 2.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Randomly choose the first player, then select characters clockwise while choosing only from a currently most-plentiful political, military, or pilot type; support characters are exempt.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 5, Setup steps 3-4.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Give President to the first selected eligible character in Roslin/Baltar/Zarek order, Admiral in Adama/Tigh/Helo order, two nukes to the Admiral, and one shuffled Quorum card to the President.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Corrected rulebook p. 5, Setup step 5.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Build the Loyalty deck by player count, add one non-Cylon card for Baltar or Boomer, deal one card each, and add Sympathizer after the deal only at four or six players.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 6, "Creating the Loyalty Deck," steps 1-6.
  Confidence: high
