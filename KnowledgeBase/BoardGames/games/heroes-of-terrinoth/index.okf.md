## Summary

*Heroes of Terrinoth* is a one-to-four-player cooperative adventure card game built around four exhausted/readied action cards, mixed hero-and-enemy dice pools, escalating peril, location travel, and in-quest class upgrades. Eight independent quests compress a dungeon-crawl arc into sessions of roughly an hour.

## Rules / Mechanics

Each hero repeatedly chooses Attack, Explore, Rest, or Aid. The chosen activation card exhausts; Rest and selected effects recover exhausted cards. Actions roll hero dice plus one enemy die per ready engaged enemy, binding action economy and danger into the same throw. Enemy, peril, and travel phases then advance the opposition and scenario clock.

## Player friction

Published reviews repeatedly identify low-agency Rest turns, formulaic administration, shallow persistent progression, absent campaign linkage, and easy introductory quests.

## Praised design

Reviews praise quick setup-to-adventure, meaningful cooperation through Aid and enemy handoffs, asymmetric heroes, class-upgrade choices, variable quests, and strong scaling across player counts.
