## Summary

The strongest improvement case is not “make it larger.” It is to preserve the one-hour adventure while giving recovery, progression, and quest-to-quest continuity more agency.

## Better-if opportunities

- **Turn pacing:** Rest should recover the action row while creating an active tactical choice—stance, preparation, threat manipulation, or team support—rather than feeling like maintenance.
- **Campaign progression:** Link quests into optional short arcs with a small persistent choice, scar, or branch; avoid converting the game into a full legacy campaign.
- **Strategic depth:** Give each action at least one alternate use so the four-verb structure produces more than obvious throughput optimization.
- **Balance/faction asymmetry:** Tune introductory quests for teachable danger rather than near-certain victory, and sharpen hero differentiation beyond one ability plus class access.
- **Player interaction:** Preserve Aid and enemy handoffs while introducing private or simultaneous commitments that limit quarterbacking.
- **Randomness:** Keep dramatic mixed-dice resolution, but give players more post-roll conversion or mitigation so failure creates decisions rather than pure loss.
