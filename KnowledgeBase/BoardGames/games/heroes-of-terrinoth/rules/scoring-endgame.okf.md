## Summary

There is no score track. Each quest sheet defines explicit Victory and Defeat conditions, usually involving location progress, a nemesis, survival, or the peril clock.

## Rules / Mechanics

Victory is scenario-specific rather than points-based. Peril supplies a deterministic round clock, while random enemy/location sets and dice alter the pressure inside that fixed quest frame.
