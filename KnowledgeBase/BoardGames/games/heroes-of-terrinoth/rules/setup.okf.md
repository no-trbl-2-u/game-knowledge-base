## Summary

Setup is quest-driven: choose one quest sheet, assemble its enemy/location/exploration decks, choose heroes and basic activation cards, place peril, and spawn the opening state.

## Rules / Mechanics

Difficulty icons divide enemies and locations into Trivial, Challenging, and Formidable pools. Quest instructions name fixed sets and may request random sets from a tier, giving each quest bounded setup variability without changing its objective spine.
