## Summary

Attack converts successes into wounds, Explore into location progress, Rest into healing, and Aid into banked success tokens for another hero.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: An action rolls the printed hero dice plus one enemy die for each ready enemy engaged with the active hero.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Rulebook pages 6 and 14, Actions clarification.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Critical successes count as successes and reroll, while defense reduces counterattack wounds.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Rulebook page 6, Resolve Hero Dice and Resolve Enemy Dice.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Aid grants up to five held success tokens; at most two can be spent on one action.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Rulebook page 7, Aid, and page 9, Success Tokens.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: A hero whose four action cards are exhausted suffers two wounds and readies all four at activation start.

## Rules / Mechanics

Card exhaustion is the central tempo economy. Rest is the full reset, Aid can selectively ready an ally's card through secondary effects, and total exhaustion forces a painful reset. Enemy dice make every engaged foe both a future phase threat and a present tax on action resolution.
