## Summary

Rounds resolve in a fixed four-phase cadence: heroes act, enemies activate, peril advances, and the party may travel.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Each hero phase contains exactly four hero activations regardless of hero count.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Rulebook page 6; page 12 assigns extra activations with fewer than four heroes.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Readied enemies activate once each, then all enemies ready at phase end.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Rulebook pages 7–8, Enemy Phase and Readying Enemies.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Peril advances one space every round before travel.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Rulebook page 8, Peril Phase.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Travel discards enemies in the shadows but carries engaged enemies forward.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Rulebook page 8, Traveling; engaged enemies are not among the discarded shadow enemies.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The party leader begins the hero phase and breaks many ties. At three heroes, one hero receives a second activation; at two heroes, both activate twice; solo controls two heroes. This preserves four party actions per round while health bonuses compensate smaller parties.
