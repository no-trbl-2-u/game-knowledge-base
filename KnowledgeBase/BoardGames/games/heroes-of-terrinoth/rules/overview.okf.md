## Summary

One to four players choose a quest and a party of heroes. The party must satisfy that quest's objective before wounds and the peril track produce its defeat condition.

## Rules / Mechanics

Heroes exhaust action cards to roll dice and perform one of four verbs. Ready engaged enemies add black dice to the active hero's roll, so danger creates immediate counterattack and threat exposure. Enemies then follow printed behavior bars; peril advances the scenario clock; fully explored locations permit travel. Quests can grant equipment and permanent-for-that-quest class-card upgrades.
