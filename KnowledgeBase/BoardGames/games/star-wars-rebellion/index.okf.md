## Summary

Star Wars: Rebellion is a 2016 Fantasy Flight Games physical tabletop game for two to four players. One side commands the Galactic Empire while the other hides a Rebel base and races to build reputation. The packet is limited to the English base game and does not treat Rise of the Empire as part of the base-game rules scope.

- Claim: The product is a two-to-four-player core game with a 180–240 minute listed duration and Corey Konieczka as designer.

- Claim: The design is asymmetric and centered on a hidden Rebel base versus Imperial search pressure.
  Source: src-002, src-005, src-006
  Evidence: The official rules establish the Rebel-base objective and faction rules; both independent reviews describe the Empire hunting the hidden base and the Rebels surviving through missions and reputation.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Players assign leaders to missions, resolve missions or activate systems, move units, resolve battles, and refresh leaders and production. The Rebel player secretly selects a base location and advances reputation through objectives; the Imperial player narrows the possible locations with probe cards and seeks to destroy the base.

## Player friction

The packet records independent testimony about a large table footprint, long sessions, rules lookup, and combat that can become slow in late-game battles. These are reception findings, not claims that every group will experience the same pace.
