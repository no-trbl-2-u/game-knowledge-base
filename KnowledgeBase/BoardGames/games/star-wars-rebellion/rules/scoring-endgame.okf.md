## Summary

The game is a race between Imperial discovery and Rebel reputation, not a conventional points total.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The Imperial player wins by finding and destroying the Rebel base; the Rebel player wins when reputation reaches the required position on the round track.
  Source: src-002, src-005
  Evidence: The official Learn to Play document defines the two victory conditions, and Ars Technica summarizes the base-destruction and reputation-track race.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Rebel objectives are the primary route for advancing reputation.
  Source: src-002, src-005
  Evidence: The official rules connect objective completion to reputation, while the independent review describes objective-card conditions as the Rebel progress engine.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Track the round and reputation markers together. The Imperial player wins immediately when the Rebel base is destroyed under the applicable rules; otherwise the Rebel player wins when reputation reaches the current round marker. Production and probe-card refreshes keep the race moving between action rounds.
