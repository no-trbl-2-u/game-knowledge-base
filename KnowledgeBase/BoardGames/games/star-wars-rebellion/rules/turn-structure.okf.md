## Summary

Each round moves from assigning leaders to resolving actions, then refreshes the state for the next round.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Leaders are assigned to mission cards before the action-resolution portion of the round.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The official round sequence places leader assignment before players resolve missions or activate systems.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Refresh returns leaders and handles card draws, recruitment, production, probe searches, and tracker movement according to the round marker.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The official rules list these refresh operations and their timing conditions.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Players alternate resolving an assigned mission or placing an unassigned leader to activate a system. When both sides have no remaining action, refresh steps reset leaders, advance production, draw new cards, and progress the game-round state.
