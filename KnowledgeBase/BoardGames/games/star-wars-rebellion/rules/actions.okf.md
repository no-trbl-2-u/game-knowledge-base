## Summary

Actions spend the scarce leader pool on either missions or system activation, forcing each faction to trade immediate operations against future mobility.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A leader assigned to a mission is unavailable for system activation until the round refresh.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The official rules distinguish assigned leaders from leaders remaining in the leader pool and use those pools for different action types.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Activating a system moves units and can initiate battles when opposing forces share a system and theater.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The Rules Reference defines system activation, movement, and the conditions that begin combat.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Mission resolution may be automatic or opposed by an opponent's leader and dice.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The official rules distinguish unopposed mission resolution from opposed tests using leader skill values.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The visible board state is only half the action problem: the Imperial player spends leaders on search, control, and force projection while the Rebel player spends them on loyalty, sabotage, rescue, and objective progress. The same pool cannot perform both jobs in one round.
