## Summary

Reception evidence points to a hard, respected cooperative deckbuilder whose no-shuffle system creates uncommon agency. The same design raises friction: players report punishment for small sequencing errors, setup burden, and limits in the fixed market.

## Player friction

- Setup burden and table-readiness friction.
- Hard difficulty that can repel casual co-op/deckbuilder players.
- Extensive card text and language dependence.
- Fixed market may feel narrow in some plays.

## Praised design

- Deterministic deck control gives losses diagnostic force.
- Nemesis pressure creates a tactical boss-fight frame rather than solitaire market optimization.
- Variable turn order creates memorable tempo crises.
