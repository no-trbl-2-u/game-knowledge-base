## Summary

Setup builds three engines before play: player starting decks and breaches, the turn-order deck, and a tiered Nemesis deck. The supply market has nine piles and may be chosen or randomized.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Each player chooses a player mat and player number token, then builds their starting hand and deck as shown on the mat.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "Each player chooses a player mat and a player number token"; "builds their starting hand and deck as shown on their player mat".
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Starting deck order matters from setup.
  Source: src-003
