## Summary

Turns are governed by a shuffled turn-order deck, not by fixed seating order. This produces cooperative uncertainty: players can plan, but they cannot fully rely on when the Nemesis or a particular player acts.

## Rules / Mechanics

- Tempo is a threat source. A double Nemesis turn can punish delayed defense.
- The wild card in three-player games adds a cooperative agency point.
- Turn order randomness is bounded by the deck composition; players can infer remaining risk as cards are revealed.
