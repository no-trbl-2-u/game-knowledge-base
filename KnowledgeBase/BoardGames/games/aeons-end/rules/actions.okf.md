## Summary

Player actions revolve around casting prepped spells, using resources, acquiring cards, focusing or opening breaches, and planning discard/deck order. The action economy is inseparable from future deck order.

## Rules / Mechanics

- Casting is gated by prior prep to breaches.
- Buying is not merely economy growth; it schedules future hands because the deck is not shuffled.
- Unique mage abilities use charge tokens, creating delayed payoff and role identity.
- Market composition changes the tactical arc of each session.
