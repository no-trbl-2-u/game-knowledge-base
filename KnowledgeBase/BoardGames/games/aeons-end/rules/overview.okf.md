## Summary

*Aeon's End* is a cooperative deckbuilder in which players jointly fight a Nemesis while defending Gravehold. The central mechanical distinction is that player decks are not shuffled; discard order becomes a planned future sequence.

## Source-backed facts

## Rules / Mechanics

- Players buy and use gems, relics, and spells to improve their decks.
- Spells are prepared to breaches and then cast for damage or effects.
- Turn order is not a fixed clockwise loop; it is produced by a turn order deck.
- The Nemesis deck escalates through tiers.
- Player and Gravehold life totals are shared pressure fronts: individual exhaustion and city destruction both matter.
