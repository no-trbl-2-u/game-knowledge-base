## Summary

There is no victory-point scoring frame. The endgame is binary: the mages win by breaking or exhausting the Nemesis threat; they lose if the city or all mages fall, or if a Nemesis-specific condition triggers.

## Rules / Mechanics

- The game uses survival/boss-kill objectives rather than postgame scoring.
- Nemesis depletion creates a second route to victory, but only if persistent threats are cleared.
- Loss conditions split attention between individual health and Gravehold health.
