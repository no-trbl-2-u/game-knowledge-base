## Rules / Mechanics

- Cooperative boss fight against a Nemesis and its minions.
- Player deckbuilding with gems, relics, and spells.
- No-shuffle player deck management: discard order matters.
- Variable turn order produces tempo uncertainty.
- Breaches gate spell preparation and casting.
- Distinct mages and Nemeses create asymmetric setup and tactical priorities.

## Praised design

- Cooperative deckbuilding without hidden-market chaos as the sole engine.
- Boss-specific pressure gives the deckbuilder a direct tactical enemy.
- No-shuffle discard handling turns cleanup into strategy.

## Better-if opportunities

- Setup and market/nemesis construction can be made faster with prepared packs or digital randomizer support.
- Accessibility suffers where large card-text dependence is unavoidable.
