- Claim: Campaign onboarding should make the permanence contract explicit before the first session.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The rulebook says writing, stickers, and destruction are permanent, while the review identifies those changes as central to the experience. This is an actionable interpretation, not a claim that the sources prescribe a new aid.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: A campaign organizer should surface the 12–24 session range and group-commitment requirement before opening the box.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The independent review describes two possible games per month across 12 months and notes the game's limited replay value after permanent alterations.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Teach materials should separate the stable Pandemic action loop from the later legacy rules so the progressive complexity remains legible.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The official rulebook begins with a Pandemic-like action structure and later introduces sealed content and permanent changes; the reviewer praises the progressive addition of nuance. This is Bathcat's design interpretation.
  Confidence: medium
