## Summary

Reception is strongly positive. Reviewers praise accessible tension, replayability, family appeal, and the way the catch-up system keeps damaged players relevant. Criticism centers on luck tolerance, component wear, low interaction, late-game leader certainty, and player-count limits.

## Player friction

- Luck can dominate perceived control for some players.
- Component wear matters because chips are hidden-information tokens handled repeatedly.
- Private simultaneous play lowers downtime but reduces auditability.
- Low direct interaction may leave players unable to affect a late leader.

## Praised design

The game turns failure into spectacle. Busting is memorable, market purchases create hope, and rat tails let the fallen approach the front again without erasing the leader's work.
