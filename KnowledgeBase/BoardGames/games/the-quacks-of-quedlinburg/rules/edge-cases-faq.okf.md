## Summary

No official FAQ/errata was extracted in this run. The rulebook itself clarifies the key edge cases around explosion counting, flask limits, blue-chip timing, simultaneous play, and final conversion.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Only white chip values count toward explosion; colored chip values do not.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "only the values on the white chips are added up"; "The values on the other colored chips are not counted".
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Flask use is prohibited if the drawn white chip caused the explosion.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "If the last chip drawn causes the pot to explode, the flask cannot be used!"
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Blue Set 2 effects do not stack; the better current blue bonus applies instead.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "The actions do not add up" and "you get the better bonus of the blue chips, not both."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Simultaneous private brewing creates an opportunity for cheating according to one reviewer.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: "Because the brewing phase is carried out in a largely solitaire manner, there are many opportunities to cheat".
  Confidence: medium
