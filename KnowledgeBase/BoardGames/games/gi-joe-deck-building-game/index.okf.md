## Summary

G.I. JOE Deck-Building Game is a 1–4 player cooperative deck-builder in which players recruit Joes, gear, and vehicles, then use their growing decks to defeat Cobra missions. It was selected as the highest-scored eligible free-choice target found in this run: Meeple Mountain reports a BGG Geek Rating of 7.67044, and the BGG page classifies it as cooperative deck-building.

## Rules / Mechanics

The system combines shared cooperative objectives, personal deck growth, transport capacity, dice-based mission resolution, escalating Threat, Cobra forces, and a three-Act story structure.

## Player friction

The rulebook separates two-player and solo modifications from the basic setup. The campaign-like mission stack and many card effects create a substantial text and state-tracking burden.

## Praised design

Publisher customer reviews praise the theme, adjustable challenge, card synergy, and short resolution time. These are limited testimonials rather than broad review consensus.

## Better-if opportunities

The strongest improvement pressure is onboarding: a guided first mission could teach Recruit, transport capacity, mission commitment, and Threat escalation without requiring the full story setup.
