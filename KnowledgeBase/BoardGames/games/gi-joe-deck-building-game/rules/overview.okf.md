## Summary

Players collectively assemble Joe teams, improve personal decks, and resolve a sequence of Story Missions against Cobra. Threat, Complications, Cobra units, and Side Missions create the automated opposition.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The game uses two Story Mission packs and a three-Act structure.

  Confidence: high
- Claim: Players win or lose collectively.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 4 states the cooperative objective and collective outcome.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The central loop is deck growth, team assignment to Missions, dice and card resolution, then Cobra and Threat escalation.
