## Summary

The game would be better if its strong theme and compact mission loop were supported by a more explicit first-game teaching route and more visible state management.

## Better-if opportunities

- Better if onboarding used a prescribed first Mission and starter-card script.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The product combines recruit, mission, vehicle, Cobra, Expert Mode, and 36-mission systems.
  Confidence: medium
- Better if setup and story progression were easier to reset between missions.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "36 unique missions" and two Story Mission packs imply repeated scenario assembly.
  Confidence: low
- Better if card synergy had a clearer ceiling for players seeking deeper deck construction.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: "the card synergy is there but not deep enough that it takes too long to resolve."
  Confidence: medium
- Better if FAQ rulings were surfaced in a quick-reference aid.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The publisher exposes a separate FAQ download beside the rulebook.
  Confidence: medium

## Praised design

The improvement path should preserve the short, themed mission resolution and adjustable Expert Mode rather than simply adding complexity.
