## Functional taxonomy

- **Identity/turn aids:** six character boards and draft cards; Help cards/player numbers; first-player marker.
- **Character packages:** one ten-card Action deck, starting weapon and two Quest Items per base character.
- **Objectives:** Corporate, Personal, and Solo/Cooperative objective decks.
- **Ship state:** Coordinates cards; room and exploration tiles/tokens; engine state tokens (not cards).
- **Threat decks:** Event and Intruder Attack cards; hidden Intruder Weakness cards; Intruder bag tokens.
- **Health:** Contamination and Serious Wound cards; physical scanner overlay.
- **Items:** red Military, yellow Technical, green Medical and blue Crafted Item decks.
- **Advanced mode:** Intruder Player Action cards for the optional first-eliminated-player role.

- Claim: Objective, action, item, event, attack, contamination, wound, weakness, coordinates, draft/help and Intruder-player materials have distinct rules functions.
