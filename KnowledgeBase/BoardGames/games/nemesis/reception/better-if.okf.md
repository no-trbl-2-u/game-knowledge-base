# Better-if synthesis

## Evidence-grounded opportunities

- Claim: Better if first play used a short, mode-specific teach that foregrounds action-card costs, noise escalation, encounter hand checks, contamination scanning and four endgame gates.
  Source: src-005, src-008
  Evidence: Reviewers identify a twenty-step setup, comprehensive rules and intimidating amount of material despite eventual intuitiveness.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Better if setup/teardown supported labeled trays or bagged modules keyed to the official sequence.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: Review describes nested setup work and very large table footprint.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Better if every player had a readable personal flow aid for the Event phase, bag development and final engine/coordinate/contamination/objective sequence.
  Source: src-005, src-008
  Evidence: Existing pair of shared quick references must support a large, information-dense table.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Better if elimination mitigation were selected before play—optional Intruder controller, fully cooperative revive, or an explicit agreement that spectating is acceptable.
  Source: src-006, src-007
  Evidence: Reviews pair frequent/punishing death with long-session and downtime concerns.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Better if the teach labels high-variance systems as narrative risk rather than promising deterministic tactical control.
  Source: src-006, src-007, src-009
  Evidence: The same chaos praised for stories is criticized for domino outcomes and overwhelming randomness.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Better if objective/semi-coop expectations are calibrated: direct attacks are prohibited, indirect harm is legal, and cooperation often remains self-preserving even for hostile agendas.
  Source: src-006, src-008, src-009
  Evidence: Reviews repeatedly identify hidden goals as the tension source rather than a conventional revealed-traitor team structure.
  Confidence: high

## Constraint

These opportunities summarize reported friction; they are not unofficial rules or house-rule endorsements.
