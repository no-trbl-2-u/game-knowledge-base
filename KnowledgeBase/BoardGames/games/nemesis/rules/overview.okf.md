# Rules overview

## Core proposition

Each player must survive and satisfy the one objective they retain after the first Intruder appears. Characters cannot directly attack one another, but ship systems, doors, fire, self-destruct, destination and abandoned crewmates permit indirect betrayal.

## Modes and secrecy

- Claim: Standard mode deals one Corporate and one Personal objective privately; all players choose one and discard the other face down when the first Intruder miniature appears.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Crew Setup and First Encounter procedures specify two hidden objectives and simultaneous commitment at first appearance.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Solo draws two solo/coop objectives, chooses one at first encounter, halves room item availability rounded up, and requires survival plus that objective.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Game Modes — Solo Mode.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Fully cooperative play draws one public solo/coop objective per player; all must be fulfilled and at least one character must survive, with a cooperative revive rule.
  Source: src-003, src-002
  Evidence: Game Modes — Fully Cooperative Mode; FAQ clarifies cooperative signal and death/revive handling.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The first eliminated player may optionally control Intruders; this is an advanced continuation role, not the standard solo/cooperative mode.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Game Modes — Playing as an Intruder.
  Confidence: high

## Scope boundary

Only the original Intruder rules, six base characters and base decks/components are covered. Expansion races, characters, campaign/Epilogue content and Lockdown/Retaliation systems are excluded even when the combined FAQ mentions them.
