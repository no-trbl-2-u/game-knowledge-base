# Turn Structure

- Claim: Each round has Player then Event phases; players draw to five, pass the First Player token left except round one, and take clockwise turns of two actions until all pass.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 13, steps 1-3.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Event order is launch CSS, advance Time and resolve Autodestruction/Alert/Power thresholds, remove eligible powered-section Noise, Intruder attacks, Fire damage, Event movement/effect including Darkness text, then Intruder-bag development.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 13, Event steps 4-10.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Passing is an action; a player who takes only one action passes as the second and cannot act again that Player Phase.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 13, Player Turns.
  Confidence: high
