## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The campaign alternates story development with combat-focused play.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: Review discusses world-state development from player actions and a distinct combat mechanism.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Each chapter has a story phase and an encounter phase; the encounter phase runs through a Refresh Phase (regenerate animus, recover cooldown ability cards), an Oathsworn Phase (each character may move and play one ability card per turn), an Encounter Phase proper (stage cards drive enemy movement and attacks), and an End of Round Phase (round-end effect resolution).
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: "each chapter consisting of two distinct phases: a story phase and an encounter phase"; "Refresh Phase: Characters regenerate animus and recover ability cards from cooldown"; "Oathsworn Phase: Players take turns, with each character optionally making one move action and playing one ability card"; "Encounter Phase: Stage cards drive enemy actions, including movement and attacks"; "End of Round Phase: Resolution of round-end effects"
  Confidence: medium

## Turn sequence

At a high level, players resolve a narrative chapter (story phase), then a combat encounter cycling Refresh, Oathsworn, Encounter, and End of Round phases each round [medium][src-007]. This is drawn from a third-party rulebook mirror, not the official Shadowborne Games PDF directly, so exact initiative and enemy-activation wording still needs confirmation against the official rulebook (src-006) once its Google Drive sign-in gate is cleared.
