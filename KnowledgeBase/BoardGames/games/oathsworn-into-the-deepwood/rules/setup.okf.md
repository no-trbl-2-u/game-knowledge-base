## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A campaign begins by forming a Free Company of exactly four player-controlled Oathsworn characters, regardless of how many players (1-4) are at the table; each Oathsworn is played either as a "full character" (fully customizable, complexity rated 1-5 stars) or a simpler two-ability "companion" version, in any mixture.
  Source: src-008
  Evidence: "A Free Company is always made up of four Oathsworn characters regardless of the number of players there are." (Story Rule Book p.6). This confirms and corrects src-006's "four mercenaries" framing — the rulebook's own term is "Oathsworn characters," not mercenaries.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Full-character setup gives each Oathsworn a Player Board, an HP die (6 at the start of a chapter), a Character Sheet recording max Animus/Regen, starting Item Cards by chapter, a Defense Tracker Token sized from equipped items (minimum 1), and Might Track cubes from equipped items; companion setup is the same but uses a Companion Card, starts with 2 Animus gems (capped permanently at 2), and has a minimum Defense of 3.
  Source: src-008
  Evidence: Full Character Setup and Companion Setup steps (Story Rule Book pp.6-7).
  Confidence: high

- Claim: At the start of a campaign, each chosen full character is equipped with a fixed, named starting item loadout (e.g. the Priest starts with a Great Maul and Pig Iron Coat; the Witch with a Gnarled Branch and Tattered Flax Garment) drawn from the Chapter 1 Common Item Cards.
  Source: src-008
  Evidence: Starting Item Cards table, one named loadout per playable Oathsworn (Story Rule Book p.7).
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Setting up an Encounter (the combat half of a chapter) requires, among other steps, building a 7-card starting hand of Ability Cards with a fixed cooldown distribution (one 0-cooldown, two each of 1/2/3-cooldown) and placing the Encounter Board, Stage Card deck (shuffled by Stage, 3 under 2 under 1), and enemies/obstacles per the Encounter Book's setup image.
  Source: src-009
  Evidence: Encounter Setup steps (Encounter Rule Book pp.6-7); starting-hand cooldown rule explicitly stated as "One '0' cooldown card, Two '1' cooldown cards, Two '2' cooldown cards, Two '3' cooldown cards" (Encounter Rule Book p.6).
  Confidence: high

## Setup / teardown

Party selection (Free Company of four Oathsworn) and chapter-specific materials are persistent campaign concerns, now confirmed against the official Story and Encounter Rule Books (see Source-backed facts above). Encounter-specific physical component placement follows the numbered setup lists in the Encounter Rule Book (pp.6-7), cross-referenced against each chapter's Encounter Book page, which is scenario content outside this corpus's scope.
