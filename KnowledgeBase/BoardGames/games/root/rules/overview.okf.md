## Summary

Root’s overview is a shared war map with asymmetric factions. Players fight over clearings and score toward victory, but their faction boards dictate different economies, actions, and failure modes.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Root is described by BGG as a game where 2–4 players battle for control of a wilderness and each player has unique capabilities and a different victory condition.

## Rules / Mechanics

The global rules establish a map-state grammar: pieces occupy clearings, rule determines permissions, cards carry suits and crafting utility, battles remove pieces, and victory is tracked publicly. Factions then bend that grammar by giving each player different phase scripts.
