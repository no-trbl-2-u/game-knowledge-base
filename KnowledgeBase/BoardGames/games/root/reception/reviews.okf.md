## Player friction

- onboarding: each faction is its own lesson.
- balance-faction-asymmetry: faction mix shapes what threats exist and who can police whom.
- expansion-bloat: the base game can feel complete as a design but incomplete as a long-term ecosystem for some players.

## Praised design

Players and reviewers praise the same dangerous thing: faction asymmetry that changes the whole board for everyone, not just the player holding the faction board.
