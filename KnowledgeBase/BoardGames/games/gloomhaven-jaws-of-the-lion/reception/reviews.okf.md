## Summary

Board Game Quest's independent review praises the tutorial, characters, balance, card-combat choices, and faster setup, while warning about mental overhead, long retry costs, and saving strong abilities.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The review calls the five-scenario tutorial a meaningful improvement over being dropped into the larger Gloomhaven system.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The review says the Learn to Play Guide introduces mechanics through the first five scenarios and lists “The tutorial that eases you into the game” as a hit.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The review praises the card-combat system for depth and satisfying choices, and describes scenarios as tightly balanced.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Its Final Thoughts and Hits sections call the card combat deep and satisfying and the scenarios incredibly well balanced.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The review identifies mental overhead, long failed-scenario retries, and holding powerful abilities as friction.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Its Game Experience and Misses sections mention careful planning, a 90-minute loss followed by replay, mental overhead, and hoarding best abilities.
  Confidence: high
