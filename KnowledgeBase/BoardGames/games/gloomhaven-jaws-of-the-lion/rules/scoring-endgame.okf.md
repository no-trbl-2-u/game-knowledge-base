## Summary

Scenario resolution is a race between completing the goal and exhausting the party, with campaign rewards persisting between scenarios.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A character becomes exhausted at zero hit points or when unable to play two cards, and exhaustion removes them from the current scenario.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF pp. 12 and 16 state the hit-point and hand-size exhaustion rules and the party-loss consequence.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Short rests lose a random discarded card; a character may suffer one damage to lose a different random card instead.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF p. 12 describes the short-rest procedure and its one-damage substitution.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A failed scenario resets the scenario, while money, experience, and treasure rewards already earned carry over.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF p. 16 distinguishes reset material from retained money, experience, and treasure rewards.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Successful scenarios award campaign progress, character refresh, gold, experience, perks, items, and level-based ability-card upgrades as the tutorial advances.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF pp. 13, 18-19, 23-25, and 29 describe scenario completion, refresh, rewards, perks, and leveling.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The endgame is not merely victory points: the campaign converts scenario outcomes into persistent character state and future card choices.

## Player friction

The review reports that the scenarios are tightly balanced and that losing after a long session can require a retry; this is reception testimony, not a universal performance claim.
