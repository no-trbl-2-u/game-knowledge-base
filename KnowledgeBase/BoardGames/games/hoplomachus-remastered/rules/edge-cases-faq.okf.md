## Summary

The publisher's FAQ tree was enumerated in the 2026-08-31 librarian pass. Two first-print errata alter Remastered core wording, and two Remastered-specific rulings cover the Skirmish camp-unit count and the Shaghad shard-flip HP shortfall. The rulebook remains the primary source for the baseline distinctions below.

## Errata that alters Remastered core wording

- Claim: The Depthcharger's printed skill name is wrong; the correct skill is Tactical, not Tactician.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: "The Depthcharger should have 'Tactical' rather than 'Tactician' as a skill."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Scheherazade's setup and first turn need one more Health chip than the box supplies, corrected by reducing Ifreet's Head to 3 HP on first deployment.
  Source: src-008
  Evidence: "The setup and first turn of Scheherazade requires 35 Health chips, which is one more than is provided in the game." The correction: "in the first deployment, give Ifreet's Head 1 fewer HP (3 instead of 4)."
  Confidence: high

## Edge-case rulings

- Claim: In Skirmish, an inactive hero in your camp does not count against the four-unit camp limit, but an active hero does.
  Source: src-009
  Evidence: "while the hero is inactive in your camp, it does not count as one of your four units"; "Active heroes in your camp do count towards your unit limit, however."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: A defeated Shaghad shard flips and gains 5 HP of the opposite color; if that color is short the shard takes as much as remains, and if none is available the shard is defeated and reverts to its previous color.
  Source: src-010
  Evidence: "If there are not 5 HP chips of the required color available, give the shard as much HP as possible. If there are no HP chips of the required color available, the shard is considered defeated and reverts back to the previous color it had."
  Confidence: high
