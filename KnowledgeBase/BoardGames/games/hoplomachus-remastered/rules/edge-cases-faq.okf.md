## Summary

The publisher maintains a separate Freshdesk FAQ hub for rulings, clarifications, and errata, organized into per-topic articles. The rulebook remains the primary source for baseline rules; the FAQ resolves specific edge cases and first-print errata for the Remastered edition.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: An official FAQ exists for current rulings, clarifications, and errata.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: support page says the FAQ is kept updated with "rulings, clarifications, and errata."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Cooperative players may decide rival-unit choices together.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: cooperative player-choice guidance in the rulebook.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: A unit is dazed on the turn it is deployed, even if that deployment happens on a different unit's turn; it is then not dazed on its own turn.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "The unit is dazed on the turn it is deployed, even if it is not their turn. And it would then NOT be dazed on their own turn."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Remastered first-print errata: the Depthcharger unit should have the "Tactical" skill rather than "Tactician".
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "The Depthcharger should have \"Tactical\" rather than \"Tactician\" as a skill."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Remastered first-print errata: Scheherazade's setup and first turn require 35 Health chips (one more than provided); the fix is to give Ifreet's Head 3 HP instead of 4 on the first deployment.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "in the first deployment, give Ifreet's Head 1 fewer HP (3 instead of 4)."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Against the titan Shaghad, a defeated shard is flipped and gains 5 HP of the opposite color; if fewer than 5 matching HP chips remain, it gets as many as are available, and if none remain it is considered defeated and reverts to its previous color.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "shards that are defeated are flipped and gain 5 HP of the opposite color. If there are not 5 HP chips of the required color available, give the shard as much HP as possible. If there are no HP chips of the required color available, the shard is considered defeated and reverts back to the previous color it had."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: In Skirmish mode, an inactive hero in camp does not count toward the four-unit camp limit; active heroes do count toward that limit.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "while the hero is inactive in your camp, it does not count as one of your four units that you can have in your camp... Active heroes in your camp do count towards your unit limit"
  Confidence: high
