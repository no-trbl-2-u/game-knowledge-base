## Summary

End conditions are objective-driven. Cooperative players win by completing the selected mode's objective and lose when its failure condition is met.

  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

- Track crowd favor and unit survival as mode-dependent progress pressure.
- Do not import a competitive arena's win condition into Onslaught or Ascension; the mode defines the end state.
