## Summary

*Hoplomachus: Remastered* is a tactical gladiator combat game with solo and cooperative modes, dice-driven unit actions, faction troops, crowd favor, and scenario-specific objectives. It was selected as the strongest available uncovered fit for the Governor's cooperative plus deck/pool-building focus: BGG search classification includes both mechanisms, and Meeple Mountain reports a BGG Geek Rating of 8.03885.

## Rules / Mechanics

- Players command a troop of units from a selected faction.
- Units act through movement, attacks, skills, health, and controller/team relationships.
- Combat uses tactical positioning on an arena grid and attack dice.
- Crowd favor and shard chips create a second economy alongside unit survival.
- Onslaught and Ascension provide cooperative/solo objectives; Skirmish, Pandemonium, Alliance, and other modes structure competitive play.

## Player friction

- The core rules are shared across modes, but each mode adds its own setup and objective rules.
- Large unit and skill vocabularies create reference overhead.
- Tactical combat can demand substantial spatial and status tracking.

## Praised design

- A common tactical rules engine supports materially different modes.
- Unit asymmetry and arena positioning make combat decisions legible and consequential.
