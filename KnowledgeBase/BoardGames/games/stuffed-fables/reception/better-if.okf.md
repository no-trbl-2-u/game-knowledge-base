# Better-if — Stuffed Fables

## Campaign-progression opportunity

- Claim: The campaign is better when persistent state, unlocks, and next-session obligations can be audited quickly.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: The overarching story branches through player choices and outcomes, and independent review reports replayable chapters with branching paths; persistence is primarily narrative and discovery-based rather than heavy character bookkeeping.
  Confidence: medium

## Friction opportunity

- Claim: A targeted improvement should reduce the documented friction without flattening the campaign's consequential choices.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: The review identifies a narrow audience window, rules too complex for very young children, low challenge for experienced adult gamers, and no clean way to save mid-chapter.
  Confidence: high
