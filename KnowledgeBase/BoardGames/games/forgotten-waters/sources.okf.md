# Authority matrix and receipts

## Governing authority
- Claim: The 8-page 2020 rulebook is authoritative for printed components and general procedure, but delegates scenario setup, entries, timing, and branching to the app and rule-bearing physical surfaces.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.2, 4-7 expressly directs app selection/entry use and location/card/sheet resolution.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The player-sheet PDF proves 42 replacement sheet faces/pages, but it does not prove the number of sheets in the retail pad.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF receipt has 42 pages; rulebook inventory says only “1 Pad of Player Sheets.”
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Current app v1.12.0 is inspectable but cannot establish release-era app bytes or release scenario inventory.
  Source: src-004, src-005
  Evidence: 2020 FAQ describes behavior without a build number; current UI/build reports v1.12.0 and contains later content roots.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Entry Book v0.1.0 is a 2024 optional app substitute for five named scenarios, not proof that its prose/data exactly equals the 2020 app build.
  Source: src-006, src-009
  Evidence: Book pp.3-4 names five scenarios and says it is intended in place of the app; PDF creation/copyright is 2024.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: No complete official FAQ/errata/support inventory was established.
  Source: src-003, src-004
  Evidence: Product support links exposed rules, sheets, logs, app, remote assistant, update-log lead, and Entry Book; update-log URL returned 404 and bounded archive retrieval timed out.
  Confidence: medium

  Evidence: FWDLC01 page labels a new scenario and 100+ new events.
  Confidence: high
