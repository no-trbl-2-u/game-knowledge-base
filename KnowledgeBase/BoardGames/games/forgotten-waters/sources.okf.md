# Authority matrix and receipts

## Governing authority
- Claim: The 8-page 2020 rulebook is authoritative for printed components and general procedure, but delegates scenario setup, entries, timing, and branching to the app and rule-bearing physical surfaces.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.2, 4-7 expressly directs app selection/entry use and location/card/sheet resolution.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The player-sheet PDF proves 42 replacement sheet faces/pages, but it does not prove the number of sheets in the retail pad.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF receipt has 42 pages; rulebook inventory says only “1 Pad of Player Sheets.”
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The official release-era scenario denominator is five, while immutable release title/build/data/entry/check/branch/audio manifests remain UNKNOWN.
  Source: src-013, src-016
  Evidence: The 2020-06-10 publisher page says all five app scenarios had full narration; the archived update log names four titles and dated versions but is not the release app bundle.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The recovered app update log records v1.2 (2020-04-21, Witch's Heart audio), v1.2.1 (2020-04-22, Beyond correction), v1.2.1.1 (2020-04-23, Witch correction), v1.3 (2020-05-05, Nature's Splendor audio), v1.4.1 (2020-05-19, Dead or Alive audio), and v1.6.1 (2020-08-19).
  Source: src-014, src-015, src-016
  Evidence: CDX exposes three successful captures and the 2020-10-20 replay supplies the dated structural update history; the retired official locator's current 404 is not an absence claim.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Current app v1.12.0 has six English scenario roots (five base plus later Kraken's Eye), 69 locale/scenario rows across 13 locales, English scenario + generic2 + generic roots, and 7,941 literal audio paths (7,850 MP3 + 91 WAV), but these code-literal counts cannot establish release facts.

  Evidence: Reproduced parsing of the exact current main-JS receipt; the current bundle includes explicitly later content.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Entry Book v0.1.0 is a 2024 optional app substitute for five named scenarios, not proof that its prose/data exactly equals the 2020 app build; the current support link serves a distinct 2025 154-page revision excluded from target authority.
  Source: src-006, src-009, src-018
  Evidence: The 2024 v0.1.0 object is 148 pages and names five base scenarios; the live support asset is a separately hashed 154-page PDF created 2025-09-19.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The official replacement ship-log PDF closes two replacement faces/surfaces, but retail pad multiplicity, collation, and revision identity remain UNKNOWN.
  Source: src-001, src-017
  Evidence: The support PDF has two pages at 648x468 pt, while the p.2 inventory names one pad without an internal sheet count.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: No complete official FAQ/errata/support inventory was established beyond the recovered update log and observed support surface.
  Source: src-003, src-004, src-014, src-015, src-016
  Evidence: Rules, sheets, logs, app, remote assistant, Entry Book, and the archived update history were recovered; no bounded authority proves that list is the complete FAQ/errata/support corpus.
  Confidence: medium

  Source: src-001, src-007
