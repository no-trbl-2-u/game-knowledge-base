# Better-if pressures

- Claim: Under the `strategic-depth` taxonomy, the game would better preserve repeated-play discovery and planning uncertainty if its Crossroads supply felt less visibly finite to repeat players; this is an explicit mapping from one reviewer's finite-narrative-supply concern, not a consensus judgment about strategy quality.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: The reviewer praises the 80-card deck yet identifies knowing that the story supply is limited as one of the few disliked aspects.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Groups sensitive to abrupt agency loss would benefit from choosing variants or expectations that explicitly frame exposure's instant-death result before play.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: The reviewer says a first-turn bite can knock the wind out of a player and group, and qualifies that the consequence is interesting “for the right players.”
  Confidence: medium

These are evidence-grounded pressure statements, not proposed rule changes and not claims of broad player consensus.
