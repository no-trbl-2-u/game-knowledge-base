# Objectives, betrayal, exile, and endgame

- Claim: The game ends immediately when morale reaches zero, the round track reaches zero, or the main objective is completed; reaching either zero preempts a later main-objective check.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 13–14, Move Round Tracker and Winning and Losing the Game.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Victory is individual: each player wins only if that player's secret objective is complete when the game ends, so multiple players may win, some may lose while others win, and everyone may lose.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 14, Winning and Losing the Game.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Betrayal objectives may require colony failure, while ordinary objectives often require the main objective plus private conditions; no global score or tie-break procedure applies in the standard game.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 2 and 15, Secret Objective Cards and Roleplaying, Secret Objectives and Game Balance.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A non-exiled player may once per turn call a vote to exile another player, voting is simultaneous after deliberation, and the first player breaks a tied exile vote.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 11 and 15, Vote to Exile and Voting.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Exile gives the player an exiled secret objective, moves colony survivors to non-colony locations, and removes crisis contribution, voting, colony-food-token spending, and normal morale-loss-on-death privileges while preserving a modified personal game.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 14, Exile.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: If two players are exiled and neither originally held a betrayal objective, morale immediately becomes zero.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 14, Exile important note.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: FAQ v1.1 allows exiled players to request/give requested cards, contribute to the main objective, add colony food by playing food cards, and call another exile vote despite being unable to vote.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: FAQ v1.1 §§5.4.2–5.4.5.
  Confidence: high
