# Round, turn, and actions

- Claim: Every round has a player-turns phase followed by a colony phase; the former reveals a crisis, rolls action dice, and resolves player turns in order.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 8, Round Summary and Player Turns Phase.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A player rolls one action die plus one per survivor controlled, places the results unused, then may take actions in any order and repeat actions unless a rule says otherwise.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 8, Roll Action Dice and Player Turns.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Attack, search, barricade, clean waste, and attract require spending an unused action die; playing/contributing cards, moving, spending food, requesting, handing off, and voting to exile do not.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 8–11 and player reference diagram p. 3.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Action-die thresholds compare the die to the relevant survivor's attack/search value, while fixed-cost actions use any die unless the effect specifies a value.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 8–10, Attack, Search, Barricade, Clean Waste, and Attract.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Food tokens may raise an unused action die by one each, but FAQ v1.1 forbids increasing a six or spending food when no unused die exists.
  Source: src-001, src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 11, Spend Food Tokens; FAQ v1.1 §5.3.1.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A requested hand card must be shown and immediately played by the requester; a hand-off may equip an item onto another survivor at the same location, while equipped cards cease to count as hand cards or contribute to crises.
  Source: src-001, src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 11, Request and Hand Off; FAQ v1.1 §5.2.1.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The retrieved rulebook states no general hand-size limit.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rules for item cards, requests, hand-offs, crises, and endgame contain no hand-cap procedure; absence is limited to the inspected 20-page document.
  Confidence: medium
