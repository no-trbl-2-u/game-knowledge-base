# Movement, search, combat, and exposure

- Claim: Each survivor may move once during its controller's turn between colony and a non-colony location or between two non-colony locations; normal movement requires an exposure roll.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 11, Move a Survivor and Rolling for Exposure.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Exposure can yield nothing, a normal wound, frostbite, or a bite; frostbite counts as a wound and adds another wound at the beginning of that survivor's controller's next turn, while a bite kills immediately and may spread at a shared location.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 11, Rolling for Exposure and Spreading a Bite Effect.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Bite spread proceeds by choosing the lowest-influence survivor at the location and either killing that survivor to stop the spread or rolling exposure; a blank stops it, while any other result kills that survivor and repeats.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 11, Spreading a Bite Effect.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Searching requires a survivor at a non-colony location and a die meeting its search value; the player may take the top item or make noise to reveal another card, repeating noise/reveal before keeping one.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 9–10, Search.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Noise is resolved during colony upkeep by rolling once per token at each non-colony location; low results add zombies, then all noise there is removed.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 12, Add Zombies.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Attacking a zombie requires a die meeting the survivor's attack value and then an exposure roll; attacking another survivor instead rolls an action die and inflicts a wound on a low result, with equipped weapon cards possibly transferred on a high result.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 8–9, Attack.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Three wounds, a bite, a card effect, or some overruns kill survivors; ordinary survivor deaths reduce morale by one, but removal is distinct from killing unless instructed otherwise.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 13–14, Adding Zombies and Killing Survivors.
  Confidence: high
