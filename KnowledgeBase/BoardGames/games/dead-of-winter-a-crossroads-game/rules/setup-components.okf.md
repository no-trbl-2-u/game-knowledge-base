## Standard setup

- Claim: Players place the colony and six locations, choose a standard main objective and follow its scenario-specific setup, then build a hidden secret-objective pool from two non-betrayal objectives per player plus one betrayal objective before dealing one to each player.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 6, setup steps 1–6.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Each player receives five starting items, draws four survivors and keeps two, makes one the group leader, and places both survivors at the colony; highest group-leader influence takes first player.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 6, setup steps 7–14.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Exact morale, round-track, zombie, and other scenario changes are printed on the chosen main objective rather than fixed globally.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 2 and 6 describe each main objective's setup field and direct players to follow it.
  Confidence: high
