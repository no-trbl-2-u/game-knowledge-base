## Summary

The game proceeds in generations. Each generation has a player-turn phase followed by production and research cleanup.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A generation begins with players drawing project cards and deciding which cards to keep by paying their costs.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 6–7 describe the research phase and the cost of keeping selected cards.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: After all players have acted, production and resource collection advance the game into the next generation.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 10–11 place production after player actions and before the next generation.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The generation rhythm alternates private planning with public parameter change. Drafting or research decisions shape the hand, and production determines which engines can fund the next round's project choices.

## Player friction

The hand-management decision arrives before players know exactly which global parameter opportunities will remain. The packet records the timing, not a claim about optimal strategy.
