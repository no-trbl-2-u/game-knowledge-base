## Strongest design lessons

Persistent roles become legible when campaign rewards change the next tactical mission, while the opposing player role gives cooperative play a concrete antagonist rather than a purely automated clock.

## Strongest player complaints

Review evidence points to campaign balance sensitivity and the practical difficulty of assembling the larger group associated with the best Rebel-side experience.

## This game would be better if...

...its onboarding and player-count scaling made the campaign's intended group shape explicit before the first long session.
