## Summary

Star Wars: Imperial Assault is a scenario-driven tactical campaign in which one Imperial player opposes one to four Rebel heroes. Its role-play identity comes from persistent heroes, class and equipment development, narrative mission objectives, and a campaign that changes future play; its combat layer uses modular maps, dice, line of sight, and asymmetric forces.

## Rules / Mechanics

The core loop is campaign mission selection, tactical activation on a modular map, combat resolution, and between-mission advancement. It is not a deckbuilder: the core rule reference describes actions, movement, dice, abilities, deployment cards, and campaign resources rather than deck construction as the central mechanism.
