## Summary

Attack, Move, Interact, Rest, and Special actions are the principal verbs. Movement points may be spent around actions, while attacks combine attack dice, defense dice, range, accuracy, and surge effects.

## Source-backed facts

## Rules / Mechanics

The action menu is deliberately broad but bounded. Character build is expressed through special actions, weapons, class cards, and equipment rather than through deck construction.
