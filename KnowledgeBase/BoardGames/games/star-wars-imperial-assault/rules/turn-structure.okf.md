## Summary

Players alternate activating one ready friendly group at a time until all groups are exhausted. A round ends after every group has activated; the next round readies the relevant activation markers.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A figure can perform up to two actions during its activation.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rules Reference, “Actions,” states that during activation a figure may perform two actions.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Unspent actions and movement points do not carry over.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rules Reference, “Activations,” explicitly denies carry-over after an activation ends.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Activation order is a tactical resource. Heroes can coordinate timing against Imperial deployments, but readiness and exhaustion prevent a figure from banking unused capacity.
