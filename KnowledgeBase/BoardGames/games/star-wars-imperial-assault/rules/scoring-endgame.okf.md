## Summary

Missions end when their stated objective or defeat condition is met. Campaign results award experience, credits, rewards, influence, or future mission consequences rather than reducing the entire game to a single victory score.

## Rules / Mechanics

The endgame is objective-driven and episodic. The durable score is campaign state: who won, what rewards were earned, and which later choices become available.
