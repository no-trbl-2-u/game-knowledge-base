Setup is scenario-specific: players choose a campaign and mission, assemble the indicated map tiles and tokens, prepare hero and deployment materials, and place figures as directed by the mission.

## Rules / Mechanics

The setup burden is front-loaded into a mission packet: map assembly, deployment, objective markers, doors or terminals, and scenario-specific rules. Campaign state determines available rewards and future choices.
