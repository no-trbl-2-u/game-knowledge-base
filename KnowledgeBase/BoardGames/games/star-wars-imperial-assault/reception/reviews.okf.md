## Summary

Reception is strong overall, with BGG showing an 8.0 average from roughly 26,000 ratings. Praise concentrates on Star Wars embodiment, tactical missions, asymmetric roles, and campaign progression. Friction concentrates on campaign balance, group-size demands, and the learning/setup burden.

## Praised design

- Claim: The game successfully gives one player a distinct opposing role while other players inhabit individual heroes.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Review frames the game around one Imperial player against 1–4 Rebel heroes.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Campaign missions provide a cinematic adventure structure.
  Source: src-001, src-004
  Evidence: BGG calls the campaign a cinematic tale; review describes scenario-by-scenario progression.
  Confidence: high

## Player friction

- Claim: The game can demand a stable large group for its best campaign experience.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Review discussion argues that four Rebel characters may produce the strongest experience and therefore requires five participants.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Imperial/Rebel balance and campaign outcomes can be sensitive to mission design and player count.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Review reports uneven campaign outcomes and discusses balance concerns.
