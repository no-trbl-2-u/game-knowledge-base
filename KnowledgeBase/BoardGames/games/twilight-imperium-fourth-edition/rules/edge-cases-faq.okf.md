## Edge cases and FAQ

- Claim: A player may pass during the action phase only after performing that player's strategic action.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 10 says a player cannot pass unless their strategy card is exhausted, meaning the player has performed the strategic action.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A player who passes has their turn skipped for the remainder of the action phase.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 9 states that after a player passes, that player's turn is skipped for the rest of the action phase.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Exhausting a strategy card removes its abilities from further resolution while preserving its initiative number.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 10 explains that exhausted strategy cards cannot resolve abilities, but their numbers still determine initiative order.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Negotiation is permitted during the active player's turn, subject to the timing and trade restrictions in the core rules.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 17 introduces negotiation during the active player's turn and distinguishes trade goods, commodities, and promissory notes.
  Confidence: high
