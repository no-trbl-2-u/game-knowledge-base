## Summary

Twilight Imperium Fourth Edition is a 2017 Fantasy Flight Games science-fiction strategy game for 3–6 players. Its core loop combines drafted strategy cards, command-token actions, modular galaxy movement, negotiation, technology development, and dice-resolved conflict.

## Rules / Mechanics

Players draft one strategy card per round, then take action-phase turns in initiative order. A tactical action activates a system, moves units, resolves any combat and invasion, and can produce units. The modular galaxy makes area movement and control the board-level structure; technology, trade goods, secret objectives, and faction powers create asymmetric development paths.
