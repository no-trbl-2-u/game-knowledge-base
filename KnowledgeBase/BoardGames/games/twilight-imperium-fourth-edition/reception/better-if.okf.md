## Better-if opportunities

- Claim: Onboarding would improve if strategy cards, command pools, and the first tactical action were taught as separate linked concepts.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The reviewer describes strategy cards and commands as the main practical grammar after noting that the rules are straightforward.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: A session aid should set duration expectations before teaching begins.
  Source: src-001, src-003
  Evidence: The publisher lists 240–360 minutes and the reviewer reports a five-hour first game.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Player aids should expose secondary strategy effects and negotiation windows.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The review explains that each strategy card has a primary ability for its picker and a secondary effect available to other players, while its first game featured alliances and betrayals.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Strategic-depth teaching should distinguish rules comprehension from the larger challenge of choosing among the game's strategic options.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The review treats the rules as fairly straightforward but emphasizes the breadth of strategy cards, commands, and long-form play.
  Confidence: medium
