## Core loop

Each knight must first advance evil, then travel or act on a quest by playing sets, fighting, healing, or accusing. Completed quests add white or black swords, and the Round Table tally decides the realm.

- **Source:** src-002
- **Evidence:** Publisher rules/product framing establishes the shared threat, player-role structure, and individual or traitor incentives summarized above.
- **Confidence:** medium-high

## Design signal

**Praise:** A foundational hidden-traitor cooperative design whose simple quest grammar keeps suspicion visible and teachable.

**Friction:** Early elimination, dated balance, quarterbacking, and a traitor role that may be absent or weak can flatten later turns.
