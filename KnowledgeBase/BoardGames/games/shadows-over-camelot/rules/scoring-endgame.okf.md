# Quest Resolution and Endgame

- Claim: Quest resolution immediately moves present Knights to Camelot, applies the printed victory/defeat package, discards played cards, and returns Pict/Saxon figures; a lone winner takes all awarded White cards privately, while multiple present Knights share face-up by agreement or random deal.
  Source: src-001, src-002
  Evidence: Book of Quests pp. 4-12; FAQ section III.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Black Knight and both Wars are repeatable; Grail, Excalibur, Lancelot, and Dragon are one-time states, with Lancelot flipping into Dragon after either outcome and completed Grail/Excalibur/Dragon-family Black cards converting to Siege pressure as printed.
  Source: src-001, src-002
  Evidence: Book of Quests pp. 5-10; FAQ section III.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A Quest may be won with no Knight present only on Dragon or Excalibur. Its White swords are still placed, no Life is gained, award cards are drawn and discarded, and the remote Excalibur completer receives the relic.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: FAQ section III no-Knight Quest-completion ruling.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Loyal Knights lose immediately at the twelfth Siege Engine, seventh Black sword, or death of the last Loyal Knight. These triggers outrank simultaneously arriving sword totals.
  Source: src-001, src-002
  Evidence: Book of Quests p. 12 and Appendix I; FAQ section VIII gives twelfth-Siege and last-Loyal simultaneous cases.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Otherwise the first action placing the twelfth or later sword ends play; an alive undetected Traitor then flips two White swords to Black, and Loyal wins only if White strictly exceeds Black. A tie favors Evil; a dead Traitor may still win but does not flip two swords if never revealed before death.
  Source: src-001, src-002
  Evidence: Book of Quests p. 4 and Appendix I pp. 13-14; FAQ sections VI and VIII.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Heroism can cause more than twelve total swords, but a seventh Black sword ends play at once before later swords in that resolution can rescue the loyal side.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: FAQ section VIII, four-White/seven-Black plus Heroism example.
  Confidence: high
