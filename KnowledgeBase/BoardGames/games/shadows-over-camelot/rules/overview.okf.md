# Rules Overview

- Claim: Loyal Knights seek a strict majority of White swords when the twelfth sword is placed, while a possibly dealt Traitor wins with Evil; Quests award White swords on victory and Black swords on defeat.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Book of Quests pp. 2-12 and Appendix I pp. 13-14.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The operative framework is shared hand management and set construction across simultaneous Quest pressures, plus hidden Loyalty and variable Knight powers; it is not deck-building because no action purchases cards into a personal deck or discard cycle.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Book of Quests pp. 2-12 specifies Fight/Grail placement, shared White-card awards, common draws/discards, and Knight powers without personal deck acquisition.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The later official rulebook organizes every turn as mandatory Progression of Evil followed, if alive and play has not ended, by mandatory Heroic Action; this is later comparison pending recovery of the release Rules.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Later rulebook pp. 9-17.
  Confidence: medium
