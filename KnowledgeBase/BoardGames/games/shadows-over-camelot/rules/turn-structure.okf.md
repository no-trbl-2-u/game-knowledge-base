# Turn Structure and Communication

- Claim: Later comparison requires a Progression of Evil choice—draw/apply one Black card, add one Siege Engine, or lose one Life—then an immediate end check; if play continues, the Knight must take a Heroic Action and check end conditions again.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Later rulebook pp. 9-12.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: The five Heroic Action types are move, Quest-specific action, play one Special White, heal, or accuse; passing is forbidden. A once-per-turn one-Life sacrifice may buy a second action only if it is a different type, and Knight powers do not waive that duplicate-action restriction except where their own timing says otherwise.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: FAQ section I; later rulebook pp. 12-17.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Players may discuss intent/resources/capability only generally, never explicit card values or other concealed specifics; advice is nonbinding and lying is allowed but rule-breaking is not.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Later rulebook p. 8, A Word on Collaborating.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: A Special Black can be cancelled only when revealed by collectively spending three Merlin cards; Standard Black cards are not cancelled this way, and named persistent Special effects cannot be retroactively removed.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: FAQ section II Merlin/Morgan rulings; later rulebook pp. 10-11.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Arthur may exchange only during his own turn after Progression of Evil. The exchange is face-down and simultaneous, may be forced, and cannot target a Knight who has no card to return.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: FAQ section IV King Arthur ruling.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Percival with Lancelot's Armor inspects two Black cards before choosing his Progression of Evil action, puts one beneath the Black pile and the other on top, then decides whether to draw that top card or choose another Evil action.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: FAQ section IV Percival ruling.
  Confidence: high
