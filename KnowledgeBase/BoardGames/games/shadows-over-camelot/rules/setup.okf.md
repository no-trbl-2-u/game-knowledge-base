# Setup, Scaling, and Components

- Claim: Later comparison setup places the master board and three Quest boards with Lancelot face up; relics on their Quests; siege, warrior, and sword reserves; one random coat and matching Knight at the Round Table per player; Life d6 at 4; separate Black/White/Loyalty piles; one Merlin plus five other White cards per player; and one secret Loyalty card from all eight.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Later rulebook pp. 6-8.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Beginning play contributes one White card face up per Knight and redistributes the offered pool by agreement or randomly; King Arthur starts, otherwise the youngest player's Knight does.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Later rulebook pp. 8-9.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: In a three-player game with the Traitor included, FAQ 1.0 recommends dealing Loyalty at the start but waiting until the sixth sword or Siege Engine appears before looking; playing without the Traitor is separately recommended for initial play, not a universal no-traitor rule.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: FAQ section IX and later rulebook p. 19 comparison.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: No seven-player exception changes the eight-card Loyalty procedure; unused Loyalty remains unknown, so a Traitor is possible but not guaranteed at every count.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Later rulebook p. 7 deals one of eight Loyalty cards per player and boxes unused cards unseen.
  Confidence: medium
