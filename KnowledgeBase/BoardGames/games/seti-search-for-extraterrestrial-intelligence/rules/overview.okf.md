## Summary

SETI is an exploration and discovery game in which each player runs a space exploration agency and uses limited resources to launch probes, explore planets, listen to stars, and advance exobiology.

## Source-backed facts

## Rules / Mechanics

The game is built around a repeated choice among exploration, scanning, analysis, cards, research, and passing. Its board is not static: the solar-system discs rotate, changing probe positions and the cost/value of future routes.
