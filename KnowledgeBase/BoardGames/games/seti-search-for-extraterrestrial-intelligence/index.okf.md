## Summary

SETI is a 2024 space-exploration strategy game for 1–4 players, with a solo rival mode. Players launch probes, scan nearby stars, analyze data, research technologies, and pursue alien-life traces across a five-round game.

## Rules / Mechanics

The central loop couples spatial probe movement with resource management, signal marking, data collection, card effects, technology purchases, and alien discovery. A player can pursue planetary exploration, star scanning, or data analysis, but the systems cross-feed: traces, publicity, income, technologies, and cards make later actions more efficient.

## Player friction

The rulebook presents a broad action menu, multiple resource types, card modes, rotating-board movement, and a hidden-information alien layer. The packet does not claim a complete card or technology inventory.
