# Hunter's Dream, death, and progression

- Claim: A Hunter who played Hunter's Dream does not attack, takes half Monster damage rounded down, then banks all collected Echoes, reclaims all used Actions, gains one Available Upgrade, and returns to full health.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 12, Hunter's Dream steps 1–4."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Upgrades are chosen in First Player order, enter the hand immediately, and are not replenished until every eligible Hunter has chosen.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 13 under Hunter Upgrade Cards."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Available Upgrades are refilled to the player count when possible; if the deck or row is exhausted, missing cards are not replaced.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 13: refill after eligible choices; no new card is added when the deck runs out."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A Hunter may never own more than seven total Actions across hand and used pile; taking an eighth requires immediately removing one card from the game, but Hunter's Dream can never be removed.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 13 under Upgrade Limit."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Death discards all collected, unbanked Echoes but preserves banked Echoes; the Hunter returns at full health and gains an Upgrade during that round's Dream step.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 14 under Hunter Death."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A dead Hunter normally does not reclaim used Actions; they do reclaim them if their selected card that round was Hunter's Dream.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 12 and 14 under Dead Hunters and Hunter Death."
  Confidence: high
