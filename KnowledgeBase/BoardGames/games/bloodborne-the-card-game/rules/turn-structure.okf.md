# Round structure

- Claim: Every round resolves eight ordered steps: choose and reveal Actions; transform weapons; resolve Instant effects; Monster attack; Hunters attack; Monster escape; Hunter's Dream; end of round.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 9 round sequence."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Initial Action selection is secret and simultaneous; discussion and promises are allowed but not binding.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 9: each player secretly chooses one card, then all reveal; its playing hint says no one is required to play a promised card."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Transform players choose a Melee or Ranged Weapon only after seeing the first reveal, then make and reveal those replacement choices simultaneously.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 9, step 2."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: When multiple ordered player effects resolve, First Player acts first and play continues left; the First Player token passes left at round end.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 10, 13–14: Instant effects, upgrade choice, and Hunter attacks use First Player order; end of round passes the token."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A surviving Boss remains the active encounter without revealing a new dungeon card; otherwise the next card is revealed at round end.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 11 and 14 under Fighting Bosses and Reveal the Next Monster."
  Confidence: high
