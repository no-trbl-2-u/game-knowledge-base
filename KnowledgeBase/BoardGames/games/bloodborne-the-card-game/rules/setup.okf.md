# Components and setup

## Physical inventory

- Claim: The box contains 87 cards, 5 Hunter Boards, 5 Hunter Health Dials, 75 Blood Echo plastic tokens, 16 cardboard tokens, 3 custom Monster dice, and 1 rulebook.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: "CMON live product-page component manifest."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The 87 cards break down into 25 Hunter Starter, 32 Hunter Upgrade, 5 Final Boss, 18 Chalice Dungeon Monster, and 7 Chalice Dungeon Boss cards.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 4 component spread; 25 + 32 + 5 + 18 + 7 = 87."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The 75 Blood Echoes are fifteen value-5 tokens and sixty value-1 tokens; the sixteen cardboard tokens are fifteen Trophies and one First Player token.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 4 component spread."
  Confidence: high

## Setup

- Claim: Shuffle five Final Bosses and reveal one, or let all players agree on one; its ability applies for the entire game.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 5, setup step 1."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Build the Chalice Dungeon by separately shuffling Monsters and Bosses, selecting seven Monsters and three Bosses, shuffling those ten together, and returning unused cards to the box.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 5, setup step 2."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Shuffle Upgrades, reveal one Available Upgrade per player, and leave the remainder face down.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 5, setup step 3."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Each Hunter takes one board, one Trophy token of each of the three types, one of each of the five Starter cards, and a Health Dial set to eight; one player receives the First Player token.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 5–6, setup steps 4, 5, 7, and 8."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Reveal the first dungeon card and place Blood Echoes equal to its printed health, adding one Echo at four players or two at five players.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 6 under Monster Health and setup step 9."
  Confidence: high
