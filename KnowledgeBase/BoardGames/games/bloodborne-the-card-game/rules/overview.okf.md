# Rules overview

- Claim: Three to five Hunters face one encounter at a time from a ten-card Chalice Dungeon, then fight one Final Boss.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 3, 5, 15: 3–5 Hunters; seven Monsters and three Bosses form the dungeon; the Final Boss follows that deck."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Hunters need collective damage to defeat encounters, but victory is individual and determined by personal Blood Echoes plus Trophy bonuses.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 3, 10–11, 15: each damaging Hunter removes Blood Echoes for themself, trophies reward participants in a kill, and only the highest Blood Score wins."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Every player begins with the same five Action cards and can permanently add Upgrades while maintaining no more than seven total cards.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 5, 8, 12–13: one of each Starter card is dealt; Upgrades enter the hand immediately; hand plus used pile may never exceed seven."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: This hand-development system is not a conventional draw-and-reshuffle deck-building loop.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 12–14: played cards enter a visible used pile and return together only through Hunter's Dream; no personal draw pile, hand draw, or reshuffle procedure is specified."
  Confidence: high
