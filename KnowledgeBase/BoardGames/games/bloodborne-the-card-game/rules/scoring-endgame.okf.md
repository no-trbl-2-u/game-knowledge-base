# Trophies, Final Boss, scoring, and ties

- Claim: A Hunter advances a matching Trophy track once for each type on an encounter only if that Hunter dealt damage in the round the encounter was killed.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 11 and 14–15 under Killing a Monster, Trophies, and Defeating a Boss."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Trophy tracks award their printed Blood Echo bonuses at game end; Bosses can award two or three types.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 14–15 under Trophies and Defeating a Boss."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: After all ten dungeon cards resolve, the Final Boss is fought like a persistent Boss; its special ability has applied for the entire game.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 7 and 15 under Final Boss Cards and The Final Boss."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Each Hunter who damages the Final Boss in the round it dies gains one Trophy of all three types.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 15 under The Final Boss."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Defeating the Final Boss ends the game; every Hunter first banks all remaining collected Echoes, then totals banked Echoes plus all three Trophy-track bonuses.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 15 under The End of the Game."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Highest Blood Score wins; a tie goes to the tied Hunter with more banked Blood, and a continuing tie is a shared victory.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 15, final two paragraphs of The End of the Game."
  Confidence: high
