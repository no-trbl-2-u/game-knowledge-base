# Actions, combat, and damage

- Claim: Action cards comprise Melee Weapons, Ranged Weapons, and Utility cards; card-local text supplies Instant and other special effects.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 8 card-type overview and pp. 9–10 timing steps."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Resolve all Instant effects before the Monster attacks, beginning with First Player and proceeding left; if an Instant kills the Monster, remove it and skip Monster attack, Hunters attack, and Monster escape.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 10, step 3."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: First Player rolls the encounter's indicated die; its result damages every Hunter, and a combo symbol requires another roll whose value is added, repeating for further combo symbols.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 10, step 4 and Monster Combos."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A Hunter reduced to zero or less dies immediately and does not attack in the following Hunters Attack step.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 10 under Hunter Death."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: In First Player order, each eligible Weapon user removes Blood Echoes from the encounter equal to damage dealt, limited by the tokens still present; removed tokens become that Hunter's collected Echoes.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 10, step 5."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: When the last Echo is removed, the encounter is killed; each Hunter who dealt damage during that round gains each applicable Trophy type.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 11 and 14–15 under Killing a Monster, Boss Trophies, and Defeating a Boss."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A non-Boss with Echoes remaining after attacks escapes and yields no Trophies; a Boss instead persists with its remaining Echoes.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook p. 11, step 6 and Fighting Bosses."
  Confidence: high

## Authority caution

- Claim: No general printed-text precedence rule was located in the inspected rulebook; local effects are instead assigned to named timing windows, while Final Boss abilities change rules for the whole game.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: "Rulebook pp. 6–10 and 15 describe reveal, escape, ongoing, Instant, and Final Boss effects but do not state a universal card-over-rulebook hierarchy."
  Confidence: medium
