## Summary

Scoring comes from public tracks, conflict outcomes, card acquisition, and hidden endgame Intrigues. Endgame triggers are simple; final valuation can include surprise cards.

## Rules / Mechanics

Dune: Imperium uses a race threshold rather than fixed rounds alone, but the Conflict Deck creates a hard clock. Combat is not pure combat: it can award resources, board control, and VP.

## Player friction

Hidden Endgame Intrigue cards and combat timing can make the true score state opaque.

## Better-if opportunities

- scoring/endgame: provide a public endgame checklist and hidden-score warning icon.
- combat resolution: make tie consequences visible because tied first place produces no winner.

## Open questions

- How often endgame Intrigue changes table perception from dramatic to unfair.
