## Summary

Reception is strongly positive: BGG rank/rating signals are high, reviewers praise the mechanism fusion, and digital adaptation feedback suggests the system translates well. Complaints cluster around learning curve, hidden randomness/uncertainty, component/rulebook usability, and some balance perceptions.

## Player friction

- Learning overhead from mixed mechanisms and icon grammar.
- Hidden combat and Intrigue cards can feel swingy if players read uncertainty as unfairness rather than the game.
- Social friction is high because blocking, combat, and alliance races directly deny opponents.

## Praised design

- Deck-building and worker placement are not parallel systems; they are fused.
- Hidden combat inputs create dramatic uncertainty.
- 10-point scoring race makes each round consequential.
- Digital adaptation reception suggests the system benefits from automation and rules enforcement.
