## Summary

The strongest improvements would preserve the combinatorial core while externalizing Action state, shortening low-impact chains, and making Pact challenge easier to compare.

## Better-if opportunities

- **Turn pacing:** Include a small Action tracker or rotate a marker whenever an effect adds or spends an Action; the visible state should survive long gain-to-hand chains.
- **Component clarity:** Visually separate permissions (extra Actions), resources (Gold/Insight), and numeric multiplication so players can audit a turn without replaying it verbally.
- **Strategic depth:** Compress routine combo links into fewer resolutions while preserving consequential multiplier choices.
- **Balance/faction asymmetry:** Give Pacts a difficulty indicator or recommended pairing/draft method, based on playtesting rather than assuming thirteen distinct thresholds are equivalent.
