## Summary

Players cycle ten-card starting decks, acquire cards from a ten-card display, manipulate printed numerals with multipliers, and race to meet a private Dark Pact.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Each player normally takes one Action, then plays Treasures and buys cards.

  Evidence: Official rules turn procedure.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: A gained card goes directly to hand rather than discard.
  Source: src-002, src-004

- Claim: A player can win by fulfilling a Dark Pact; if the supply exhausts first, compare Insight after subtracting Curse points.

## Rules / Mechanics

The direct-to-hand market makes purchase timing tactical: a newly gained Action or Treasure can extend the same turn. Multipliers change printed numbers rather than providing a separate resource, allowing one card to amplify costs, Insight, thresholds, or other numeric effects as permitted by the rules.
