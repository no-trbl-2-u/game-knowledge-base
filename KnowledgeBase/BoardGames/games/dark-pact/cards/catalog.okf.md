## Taxonomy

- **Starting deck:** ten cards per player; exact full composition is not cataloged here.
- **Grimoire reserve:** Silver, second 2x, and Inscription.
- **Common supply / market:** ten visible cards available for acquisition; gained cards go to hand.
- **Multipliers:** 2x and 3x cards operating on printed numerals.
- **Pacts:** thirteen alternate victory conditions, divided into persistent **Keeper** and Action-timed **Instant** behavior.
- **Solo materials:** bot-facing cards/rules exist, but the three exact victory routes remain an explicit verification gap.
