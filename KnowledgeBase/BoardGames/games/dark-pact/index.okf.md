## Summary

*Dark Pact* is Tom Lehmann's competitive fantasy deck-building game for one to four players. Players improve decks from a shared market while pursuing one of thirteen hidden alternate victory conditions.

## Rules / Mechanics

A ten-card central display supplies immediate hand gains. A private three-card grimoire provides Silver, a second 2x multiplier, and Inscription. Printed numerals can be multiplied by 2x or 3x cards, and a hidden Pact can end the game immediately when its requirement is achieved.
