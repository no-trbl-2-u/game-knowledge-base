## Summary

Reception is strongly positive. The game is praised for depth, replayability, theme-mechanic integration, asymmetric spirits, solo/small-group strength, and cooperative puzzle quality. Criticisms cluster around rules load, brain burn, fiddliness, high-player-count length, base-game predictability/limited fear-blight variety, and anticlimactic or procedural endings.

## Rules / Mechanics

Reception sources consistently understand the game as a cooperative tactical puzzle with asymmetric roles, power timing, invader automation, fear, and blight.

## Player friction

- High cognitive load and extensive planning.
- Table discussion can expand playtime at 4 players.
- Base-game fear/blight variety may repeat.
- Predictability can make late turns feel solved.
- True solo can be swingy; multi-handed solo burdens the player.

## Praised design

- Asymmetric spirits with strong identities.
- Cooperative puzzle gives players agency and table voice.
- Theme and mechanics reinforce each other.
- Replay value is high without mandatory expansion purchase.
