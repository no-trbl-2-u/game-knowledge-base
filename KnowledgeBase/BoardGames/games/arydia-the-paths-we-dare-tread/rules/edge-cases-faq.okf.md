## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Arydia is built around a cooperative fantasy campaign rather than a standalone score race.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Publisher description emphasizes hostile locations, weapons, skills, cooperation, and turn-based combat.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Returning to a previously cleared map does not respawn foes, Event tokens, or Chest tokens.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: "When returning to a map that has already been cleared ... Foes, Event tokens, and Chest tokens do not spawn again."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: During combat, squares containing Chests, NPCs, and Points of Interest cannot be interacted with via the Explore action, though creatures may move through or end their movement on a Chest square; moving adjacent to an Event token still triggers it immediately.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: "Pointer Interactions in Combat: During combat, squares with Chests, NPCs, and POIs cannot be interacted with ... Players cannot take the explore action while in combat. If you move adjacent to an Event token, it will trigger immediately."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Combat is confined to a single floor of a location; exiles and foes cannot change floors while combat is active.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: "Combat only takes place on a single floor of a location (a floor can consist of one or more maps). Exiles and foes cannot move to another floor while in combat."
  Confidence: high
