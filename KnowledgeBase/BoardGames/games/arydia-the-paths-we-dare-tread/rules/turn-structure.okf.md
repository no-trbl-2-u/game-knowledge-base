## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Foes activate through threat-driven AI rather than a conventional fixed player-facing initiative sequence.
  Source: src-005, src-007
  Evidence: "Threat is a shared resource spent by foes to activate AI behaviors."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Combat proceeds over rounds; each round has two parts — a Foe Turn (draw one Threat card, possibly activating foes) followed by an Exile Turn in which one exile takes actions. Play then passes to the next player clockwise for the following round.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: "Combat proceeds in rounds, and each round has two parts: 1. Foe Turn (draw one Threat card, possibly activating foes) 2. Exile Turn (one exile takes actions) ... play passes to the player on the left in clockwise order, and another round begins."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The player who triggers combat (e.g. by exploring into revealed foes) resolves the first Foe Turn and then takes the first Exile Turn.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: "For the first round, the player who triggered combat goes first. They take a turn for the foes, followed by taking their exile turn."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Combat ends when the exiles defeat all foes (victory), when all exiles are knocked out at the end of an Exile Turn (defeat), when the exiles flee, or by another combat-specific ending condition.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: "Combat ends in victory if all foes are defeated. If all the players are KO'd at the end of an Exile Turn, combat ends in defeat. Exiles can flee the battle."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: When shared Threat or Combo reaches 7, a "Limit Break" triggers, causing all foes to activate as if an All Foes Threat card had been drawn.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: "When either Threat or Combo reaches 7, a Limit Break! may occur ... All Foes: Then, all foes activate as if an All Foes Threat card were drawn, spending Threat normally."
  Confidence: high
