## Setup status

- Claim: The rulebook's printed "Setup" section (p.4) covers resuming a saved campaign, not first-time play; first-time players are directed to a separate Quick Start Guide instead.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: p.4, "(These rules are for continuing your campaign after Saving. If this is your first time playing, use the Quick Start Guide.)"
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Continuing-campaign setup places the Worldmap with the Party token and Mode token (set to Adventure mode), the Threat/Combo Track and cubes (set to 0), the Threat deck/discard pile and Hit Dice, map boxes, the various component tokens (XP, Armor, Kae, Wound, Wear, Chest/Event/Cart) and the Pathless Chest, the Travel deck/tokens/discard pile, the Job deck, and the Day deck/discard pile with the current Day card face up.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: p.4, "Worldmap... Threat / Combo Track and cubes (set to 0)... Day deck and the Day discard pile... with the current Day face up."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The Day discard pile always begins with card DAY-1 as its first card.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: p.4, "(Day discard pile always includes DAY-1 as the first card in it)."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: After placing components, the saved Hex is retrieved from the Party section of the card Index, returned to the Worldmap, and the Party token is placed on it.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: p.4, "Retrieve saved Hex from Party section of Index, return it to the Worldmap and place Party Token on it."
  Confidence: high

## Open questions
