# Scoring and endgame

## Win/loss
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** Victory occurs if players defeat the final Boss.
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** Which Boss counts as final depends on how many Acts the group wants to play; players may stop after any Act.
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** If a player's HP is reduced to 0, that player dies and the party loses, except under optional variants.
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** The game is divided into Acts; each Act takes about 60-90 minutes, with stop/save/continue options at Act end.

## Progression beyond victory
- **Source-backed fact [medium][official-rulebook]:** Rulebook sections include Unlocks & Ascension, Achievements & Daily Climb, Act IV, Quick Start Rules, and Optional Rules.
- **Source-backed fact [medium][bgg-rulebook-file]:** The v2.27 rulebook included an A7 sticker fix; v2.30 reprint added teardown rules and removed optional sequential-turn rules.

## Player friction
- **Player friction [campaign/progression][high][coopgestalt-review]:** A reviewer who had never played the video game found post-Act III progression unclear and said the rulebook seemed to assume video-game knowledge.

## Design implications for SomberSoft
- Modular victory length is strong: one Act can satisfy a table, while three Acts or Act IV offer mastery depth.
- If a game has unlocks/ascension, the post-win reset/progression procedure must be explicit. The absence of a save-file metaphor on tabletop makes reset rules mission-critical.
