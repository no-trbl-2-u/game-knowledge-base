# Rules overview

## Core loop
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** The rulebook describes the game as a cooperative roguelike deckbuilding adventure where players craft decks, fight enemies, gain relics, and defeat bosses.
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** Players proceed through Acts. At the end of an Act they can continue, stop and end the adventure, or save and continue later.
- **Source-backed fact [high][bgg-main]:** If players defeat the final Boss, they win; which Boss is final depends on how many Acts they choose to play.
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** If any player HP reaches 0, that player dies and the party loses, except where optional rules alter that.

## Player state
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** Player boards track HP, Block, Energy, character ability, and resource/status cubes.
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** Damage can be prevented by Block; "lose HP" effects cannot be blocked.
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** Power cards stay in play until combat ends; exhausted cards leave the combat until it ends.

## Progression
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** Each player starts with a starter deck and improves it through card rewards, removal, upgrade, and transformation.
- **Source-backed fact [medium][meeple-mountain-review]:** The map includes normal encounters, elites, shops, campfires, and bosses; randomized tokens preserve a procedural feeling on a physical board.

## System lesson
The rules preserve roguelike pressure by making defeat reset the run, but preserve table sanity through Act stopping/saving. This makes a long campaign-shaped session divisible without dissolving stakes.
