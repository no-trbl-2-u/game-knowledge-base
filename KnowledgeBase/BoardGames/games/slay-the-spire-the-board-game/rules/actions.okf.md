# Actions

## Player actions and card types
- **Source-backed fact [high][src-002]:** Main card types are Attack, Skill, Power, Curse, and Status. Attacks deal damage; Skills provide block/utility; Powers persist for the combat.
- **Source-backed fact [high][src-006]:** Players start a combat round with limited Energy, commonly 3, and choose which cards from a hand of 5 to play.
- **Source-backed fact [high][src-006]:** Attack cards can target monsters, Skill cards can add Block/draw/apply effects, and Power cards grant lasting combat bonuses.
- **Source-backed fact [high][src-006]:** Players can attack monsters outside their own row, but only some skill cards let players give Block to other players.
- **Source-backed fact [high][src-002]:** Potions and relics are item-area resources with effects that modify play; Power cards remain in the power area until combat ends.

## Rewards and deck manipulation
- **Source-backed fact [high][src-002]:** The game includes rules for rewards, remove, upgrade, and transform.
- **Source-backed fact [medium][src-008]:** The reviewer praises the Merchant/card-culling system as satisfying the "Andrew rule": deckbuilders need systematic card culling.
- **Source-backed fact [medium][src-006]:** Shops let players buy potions, relics, cards, and pay to remove cards; campfires present Rest vs Upgrade decisions.

## Praised design
- **Praised design [strategic depth][high][src-007]:** Reviewer praises cooperative play as the main value beyond the video game and calls it a revelation.
- **Praised design [player interaction][high][src-006]:** The multiplayer element shines because players coordinate attacks, block, and shared survival.
