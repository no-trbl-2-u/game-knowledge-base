# Edge cases and FAQ

## Official FAQ locations
- **Source-backed fact [high][official-rulebook]:** The rulebook table of contents includes FAQ pages 18-19, Triggered Abilities page 19, Unlocks & Ascension page 20, Achievements & Daily Climb page 21, Act IV & Quick Start Rules page 22, Optional Rules page 23, and Abilities & Keywords page 24.
- **Source-backed fact [high][bgg-rulebook-file]:** BGG file entry lists official rulebook v2.30 and v2.27; v2.27 is said to include the A7 sticker fix.

## Known clarifications / errata leads
- **Source-backed fact [medium][bgg-rulebook-file]:** A BGG comment identifies the A7 sticker fix as a misprint in the rules for Ascension 7; a designer/publisher comment says if a player lacks the sticker fix then they likely have wave 2 or later edition.
- **Source-backed fact [medium][bgg-rulebook-file]:** The v2.30 change note says teardown rules were added to page 23 and optional rules for sequential turns were cut.
- **Source-backed fact [medium][kickstarter-downfall-faq]:** Downfall/reprint FAQ says later reprints added a rulebook section on how to reset the game and clarification to Gremlin Leader that Gremlins are in Act 1 summon deck.

## Player-discovered ambiguity
- **Player friction [rules ambiguity][high][coopgestalt-review]:** Rulebook may assume video-game knowledge, especially around how to continue after Act III and how to handle summon deck organization.
- **Player friction [solo/co-op automation][medium][rolling-meep-review]:** Solo play is considered weaker by Rolling In The Meep because it feels too close to the video game; cooperative play provides the tabletop justification.
