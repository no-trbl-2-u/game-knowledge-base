## Strongest rule lesson
Flexible co-op action order is the design engine. Players can interleave cards, potions, and abilities in chosen order, preserving tactical precision while allowing simultaneous play to reduce downtime.

## Strongest reception complaint
The rulebook and physical handling assume too much tolerance: unfamiliar players report ambiguity around progression/reset and summon-deck handling, while reviewers cite fiddly tracking, large footprint, and setup burden.

## Top 3 better-if opportunities
1. Explain abnormal setup steps and searchable deck handling. Categories: onboarding, setup/teardown, rules ambiguity, component clarity.
2. Clarify reset/unlock/post-Act progression for players without video-game knowledge. Categories: campaign/progression, scoring/endgame, onboarding.
3. Reduce or scaffold component handling and effect tracking. Categories: setup/teardown, component clarity, accessibility.
