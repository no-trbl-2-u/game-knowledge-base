## Rules / Mechanics

- Cooperative deckbuilding with recruitment and combat currencies.
- Scenario construction around movie locations, objective decks, and hive decks.
- A central complex of hidden/revealed threats creates information pressure before enemies reach combat.
- Character cards recruited from the barracks improve player decks over time.
- The system mixes survival tension with familiar open-market deckbuilder tempo.

## Praised design

- Strong Alien theme through hidden hive movement, Facehugger/Chestburster pressure, and scenario arcs.
- Cooperative deckbuilding gives every purchase tactical consequence against a shared clock.
- The central complex adds reveal/scan tension that ordinary market deckbuilders often lack.
