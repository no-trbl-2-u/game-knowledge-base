## Player friction

- Sorting/setup burden from a large card corpus.
- Timing ambiguity around objectives and hive spawning.
- Random/high-pressure hazards can swing outcomes hard.
- Heavy card text and Alien IP specificity affect accessibility.

## Praised design

- Theme is carried by mechanisms, not only art.
- Hidden hive/complex pressure makes deckbuilding feel dangerous.
- Simple choices remain meaningful because the enemy clock is legible and severe.
