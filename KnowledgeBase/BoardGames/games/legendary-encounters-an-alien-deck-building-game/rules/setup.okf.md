## Summary

Setup is scenario-driven. The available secondary walkthrough says the group chooses a movie/location scenario, pairs it with objectives and hive decks, and assembles the central card areas for recruitment and threat movement.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Initial setup includes selecting a location/scenario, with different locations corresponding to Alien films.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "select a location card which is different for each of the four scenarios"; examples include "Nostromo" and "Hadley's Hope".
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Each scenario has three objectives and corresponding hive decks.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "Each scenario has its own set of objectives (three for each) as well as three corresponding hive decks".
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Product/component summaries describe a 600-card set, board/mat, rulebook, and organization inserts.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: "600 total Original Art Card Set"; "Full Color Game Board (mat)"; "Full Color Rule Book".
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Each player takes a personal starting deck of Specialist and Grunt cards and one of ten Roles, and the Barracks/HQ recruitment row is dealt out before the first hand is drawn. The official rulebook's own printed deck size is internally inconsistent — one passage says the personal deck has 7 Specialists + 5 Grunts (12 cards), while the shuffle/draw instruction later refers to "13 cards" — quoted as printed rather than resolved.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "Give each player their own personal 12-card deck made up of these cards: 7 Specialists, 5 Grunts. Each player then selects one of the ten Roles and takes the appropriate Role Avatar and Role Character Card... Put the top five cards of the Barracks into the five spaces of the Headquarters (HQ). Each player shuffles their own personal deck of 13 cards and draws a hand of 6 cards from it."
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

- Sort scenario material before play; setup burden is part of the system.
- Build a hive deck/stack from scenario-specific cards.
- Prepare the barracks/open recruitment row for player deck growth.
- Prepare player starting decks and avatar/role elements; exact card counts require official rulebook verification.
