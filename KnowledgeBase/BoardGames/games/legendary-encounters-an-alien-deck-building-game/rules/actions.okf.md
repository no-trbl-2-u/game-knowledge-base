## Summary

Player actions revolve around two spendable pressures: recruit to improve the deck and attack to scan rooms or fight threats. This creates the deckbuilder dilemma in hostile form: strengthen the future or survive the present.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Players use attack points and recruitment points from hand cards.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "You have a hand of cards with either attack points or recruitment points".
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Attack can be used to scan rooms and reveal cards in the complex.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "You may 'reveal' a card in the complex by scanning the appropriate room by paying the attack cost".
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Recruitment buys cards from the barracks into the player's deck/hand system.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "recruit additional characters/ cards from the barracks".
  Confidence: medium

## Rules / Mechanics

- Recruit: buy cards from the barracks/open row to improve future hands.
- Scan: spend attack to reveal hidden complex cards and reduce uncertainty.
- Fight: spend attack to defeat exposed enemies or solve combat demands.
- Objective work: certain scenario cards require specific resolution; details need official text.
- Emergency response: Facehugger and Chestburster pressure forces immediate team triage.
