## Summary

The game is a cooperative survival deckbuilder. Players recruit cards into their decks, confront a scenario hive, and complete objectives before the Alien system overwhelms the team.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: BGG describes the game as fully cooperative and based on the Alien films.

  Confidence: high
- Claim: The review describes deckbuilding around attack points, recruitment points, scanning rooms, and recruiting cards from the barracks.

## Rules / Mechanics

- Cooperative objective completion against a scenario threat deck.
- Recruit economy builds the player decks.
- Attack economy handles scanning, fighting, and threat removal.
- Hidden cards in the complex create a reveal-risk layer before full combat.
- Player elimination/transformation pressure is part of the Alien theme according to review testimony.
