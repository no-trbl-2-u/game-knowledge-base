## Summary

This is a win/loss cooperative game, not a point-scored contest. The secondary source describes victory as completing all three objectives; loss pressure comes from lethal Alien effects and the scenario system overwhelming players.

- Loss pressure: enemy movement, high-damage effects, deck depletion/tempo loss, and lethal special cards.
- Scoring: no point scoring identified in this scout; outcome is cooperative success/failure.
