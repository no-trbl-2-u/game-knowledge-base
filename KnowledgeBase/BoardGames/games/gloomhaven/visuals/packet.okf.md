# Gloomhaven visual reference packet

## Curation notes

Open the contact sheet first. Its four labeled panels intentionally move from pre-play burden to in-play reasoning: the complete component taxonomy, a representative scenario table layout, one ability-card anatomy explanation, and one monster-automation example. Together they expose Gloomhaven's information architecture without retaining contiguous pages, a complete rules sequence, source-resolution artwork, or enough material to reproduce a component set.

The four references are noncontiguous PDF pages 2, 4, 7, and 29 from a 52-page rulebook. Each local reference is a 760 × 978 WebP derivative; the 920 × 1248 contact sheet is a further reduced overview. The packet totals approximately 505 KiB. Visual inspection on 2026-07-17 confirmed that all four panels are present, correctly labeled, legible for structural analysis, and free of clipping or rendering corruption.
