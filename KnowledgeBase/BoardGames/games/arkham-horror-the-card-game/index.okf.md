## Summary

*Arkham Horror: The Card Game* is a cooperative Living Card Game about investigators resolving linked Lovecraftian scenarios through custom investigator decks, action-point turns, skill tests, and campaign consequences.

## Rules / Mechanics

- Cooperative scenario resolution with shared progress pressure.
- Player-specific deck construction and campaign upgrades.
- Three-action investigator turns inside a round structure.
- Skill tests using investigator stats, committed cards, and chaos-token uncertainty.
- Act and agenda decks create competing progress clocks.

## Player friction

- The core/revised entry point teaches a large rules engine and a campaign product line.
- Full deck-construction depth depends on card-pool growth beyond a starter set.

## Praised design

- Deck construction is bound to scenario identity, investigator asymmetry, and campaign advancement.
- Losing or partial success can still feed narrative consequence rather than simple reset.

## Better-if opportunities

- Onboarding improves when preconstructed decks, first-scenario guidance, and upgrade advice are treated as core teaching instruments.
- Campaign card pools need strong organization and upgrade-path tools to reduce collection-management friction.
