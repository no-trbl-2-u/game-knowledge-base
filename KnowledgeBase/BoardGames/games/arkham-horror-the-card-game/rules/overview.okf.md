## Summary

The core rules frame Arkham as a cooperative campaign card game: investigators build decks, enter scenarios, explore locations, collect clues, resist the Mythos, and carry consequences forward.

- Investigator identity determines stats, deckbuilding constraints, signature cards, and weaknesses.
- The scenario supplies locations, an encounter deck, agenda pressure, and act objectives.
- Campaign play turns experience and trauma into persistent state.
