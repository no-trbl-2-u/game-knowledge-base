## Summary

Actions are scarce currency. Investigators spend them to move, investigate, fight, evade, draw, gain resources, and play cards while the scenario converts delay into doom and encounter pressure.

## Source-backed facts

## Rules / Mechanics

- The action system forces triage: progress, survival, economy, and positioning compete.
- Player cards can be played for effects or committed to improve tests.
- Enemy engagement taxes action efficiency because fight/evade actions interrupt clue progress.
