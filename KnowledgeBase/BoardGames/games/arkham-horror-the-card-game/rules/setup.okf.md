## Summary

Setup is scenario-driven. The introductory material tells new players to begin with a guided scenario and preconstructed starter decks before using full deck customization.

## Rules / Mechanics

- Choose investigator identities and decks.
- Prepare the scenario's act, agenda, locations, encounter materials, tokens, and starting hands.
- Starter decks are part of the onboarding route; custom deckbuilding appears after basic play is understood.
