## Summary

The playable round is a pressure cycle: Mythos pressure arrives, investigators spend three actions each, enemies resolve, then upkeep refreshes hands and resources.

## Rules / Mechanics

- Mythos phase advances doom pressure and encounter complications.
- Investigation phase is the main player agency window; turn order is cooperative and action-limited.
- Enemy phase punishes unresolved enemy presence.
- Upkeep resets exhausted cards and replenishes cards/resources.
