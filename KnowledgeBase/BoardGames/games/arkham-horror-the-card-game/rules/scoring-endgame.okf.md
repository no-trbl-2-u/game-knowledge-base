## Summary

The game treats outcomes as campaign state, not merely win/loss. Experience strengthens decks; trauma, scenario resolutions, and advancing agenda/act states alter future play.

- Claim: Campaign progress gives investigators experience and lets them improve decks.

  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

- Scenario resolution can advance a campaign even when the result is compromised.
- Experience purchases convert performance into future deck strength.
- Trauma and narrative consequences give failure teeth beyond a single session.
