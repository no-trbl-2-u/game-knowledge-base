## Summary

Survivors use differentiated equipment and abilities to move on a tactical grid, attack, and manage danger during a showdown. Monster behavior and hit locations make target selection consequential.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Tactical combat, dice rolling, grid movement, and role-playing are central mechanisms identified by the review.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Review mechanism list names dice rolling, grid movement, role playing, and scenario / mission / campaign game.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Each monster's showdown uses its own AI, hit-location, and resource decks; the AI deck is assembled for the encounter and its card count functions as monster health.
  Source: src-007, src-009
  Evidence: WayTooManyGames describes monster-specific AI, Hit Location, and Resource decks, says the AI deck is assembled at showdown by difficulty, and says its card count determines health; Meeple Mountain likewise says the AI deck doubles as hit points.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A successful accuracy roll draws a hit-location card, after which the attacker tests whether the chosen location is wounded; wound results can remove an AI card from the monster's remaining pool.
  Source: src-007, src-008
  Evidence: WayTooManyGames describes one hit-location draw for each successful accuracy roll and a separate wound test; Always Board Never Boring describes successful wounding as removing the top AI card from play.
  Confidence: high
