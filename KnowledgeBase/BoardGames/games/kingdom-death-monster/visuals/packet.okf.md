# Kingdom Death: Monster visual reference packet

## Curation notes

Open the contact sheet first. The packet deliberately moves from physical vocabulary to three nonredundant campaign contexts: the broad component image establishes what kinds of objects recur; the settlement view shows campaign administration across multiple surfaces; the hunt view shows a narrow, linear transition state; and the showdown view shows spatial tactical play on a grid. Decorative miniature lineups and character illustrations available on the same product page were excluded because they add little rules-state information.

Each reference is a 640-pixel-wide, lossy WebP reduction of one official product-page image. The contact sheet presents labeled 560-pixel-wide previews in a 2x2 layout. Visual inspection confirmed that all four cells render without distortion or clipping and that their labels match the referenced files.

## Copyright and provenance

The publisher product locator and exact CDN asset URL are preserved separately for every reference. The official page does not identify the individual image creator, declare the image rights holder, or provide an image license, so this packet does not infer those facts or claim that official provenance grants reuse rights.

Local files are materially reduced, metadata-stripped editorial excerpts retained for private visual research and design analysis. They are not source-resolution promotional assets and do not reproduce a rulebook, card library, player aid, or component at replacement quality. The contact sheet inherits the restrictions of all four source images. Attribute Kingdom Death and the linked official product page when discussing these references; do not redistribute the local derivatives as standalone artwork. Remove them on rights-holder request.
