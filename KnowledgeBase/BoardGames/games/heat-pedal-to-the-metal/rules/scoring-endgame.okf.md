## Summary

Corners are the judgment points. Movement is not enough; a car must also survive the speed limit of any corner line it crosses.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: If multiple cars finish in the same round, the car furthest ahead wins, with Race Line position breaking side-by-side ties.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The rulebook states that if multiple cars finish in the same round, the car furthest ahead wins, and the car closest to the Race Line wins side-by-side ties.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Passing through a corner above its speed limit requires paying Heat equal to the excess.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The corner rules compare total speed to the speed limit and require Heat for the excess.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Failing to pay required Heat after exceeding a corner speed limit causes a spin out.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The core rules describe spin out as the consequence when the player cannot pay enough Heat.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Cars do not block movement through occupied spaces, but a car cannot end in a fully occupied space.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The track rules say cars do not block movement through spaces, but a blocked ending space moves the car to the first available space behind.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Scoring and endgame are spatial rather than arithmetic. Victory asks who crosses first and how far beyond the line they can carry momentum. This makes the final hand a speed puzzle under corner and deck constraints.
