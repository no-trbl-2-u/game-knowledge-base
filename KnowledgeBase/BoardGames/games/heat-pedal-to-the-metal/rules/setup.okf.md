## Summary

Setup gives every player a symmetrical car deck, then uses track-specific Heat and Stress counts and a random starting grid to seed the first tactical state.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The recommended first game uses the USA track and may be reduced to one lap as a learning game.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "For the first game, use the USA track" and "consider racing only 1 lap as a learning game."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Each player takes a car, player mat, gear pawn, 12 basic cards, and 3 starting upgrade cards in their color.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The setup list assigns those components to each player.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Typical starting car preparation uses 6 Heat cards on the Engine spot and 3 Stress cards shuffled into the deck, with exact counts checked on the board.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "Typically: 6 Heat and 3 Stress" and place "6 Heat cards faceup" on the Engine spot.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Cars are randomly placed on the starting grid and that order determines player order for the first round.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "Randomly place participating race cars on the starting grid" and "This determines player order for the first round."
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The setup is fast for the core game because advanced decks and module mats can remain boxed. The first hand is seven cards, making early gear choice legible but not perfectly deterministic.
