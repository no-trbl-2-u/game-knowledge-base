## Summary

A round contains nine named steps, but only four are mandatory every round: Shift Gears, Play Cards, Reveal & Move, and Replenish Hand.

## Rules / Mechanics

The design separates commitment from resolution. Everyone plans under imperfect information at once, then the board state changes in leader order. This keeps downtime low while still allowing position to matter.
