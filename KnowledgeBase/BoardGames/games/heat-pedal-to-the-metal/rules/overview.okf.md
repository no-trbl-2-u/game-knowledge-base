## Summary

The core game is a race for position and tempo. Each round players choose gears, play cards equal to gear, move by total speed, manage Heat and Stress, and test corner speed limits.

## Rules / Mechanics

Gear sets the size of the committed hand. Corners enforce speed discipline. Heat is both a resource and a future clog. Stress injects uncertainty when played. Slipstream and adrenaline create pack compression so the race remains contested.
