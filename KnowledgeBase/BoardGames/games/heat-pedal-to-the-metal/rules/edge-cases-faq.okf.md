## Summary

Important edge cases cluster around track position, full spaces, Heat payment, spin outs, and module boundaries. No separate official FAQ/errata source was found in this run.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Side-by-side cars are ranked by closeness to the Race Line.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "The car closest to the Race Line is considered ahead for all purposes."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: If a car would end movement in a full space, it is placed in the first available space behind the blocking cars.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The blocking rule instructs placement in the first available space behind and in the spot closest to the Race Line.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The advanced booklet adds Garage, Legends, Championship, Weather, Road Conditions, and Tournament content that may be used after learning the core game.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: The advanced rules list Garage Module, Legends Module, Championship System, Weather & Road Conditions Module, and Tournament Mode content.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Core edge cases reinforce the race line as the final arbiter of position. Modules are real content, but they are not required to understand the base race and should not be taught before the core loop is stable.
