## Summary

The better-if signal is not that Heat is broken. It is that its strengths have known shadows: rubber-banding can feel artificial, speed can become opaque under randomness, iconography can slow onboarding, and production grandeur demands table space.

## Better-if opportunities

- Claim: The game would better serve simulation-minded players if catch-up effects felt less artificial or more earned.
  Source: src-005, src-006, src-007
  Evidence: Roll to Review says the "adrenaline catch-up mechanic can feel too forgiving or artificial"; Co-op Board Games says "Catch-up mechanics may frustrate some"; Shelf Gamer says slipstreaming "doesn't feel earned or satisfying."
  Confidence: medium
  Labels: randomness, strategic-depth
- Claim: The game would onboard faster if iconography and phase resolution were easier for first-time players.
  Source: src-006, src-007
  Evidence: Co-op Board Games says "Player board iconography requires learning"; Shelf Gamer says multi-phase turns can be tricky for non-gamers.
  Confidence: medium
  Labels: onboarding, component-clarity
- Claim: The core game would hold experienced players longer if module depth arrived before the early races feel too simple.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: Roll to Review lists as a con that "Early games without modules may feel overly simplistic for seasoned players."
  Confidence: medium
  Labels: strategic-depth
- Claim: The table experience would improve with lower footprint or stronger setup/table management aids.
  Source: src-007
  Evidence: Shelf Gamer calls the game a "table hog" and cites large map boards, player mats, and module mats.
  Confidence: medium
  Labels: setup-teardown, component-clarity

## Player friction

Friction is concentrated in perception of agency. Catch-up mechanisms are useful because racing needs packs; they are dangerous because players notice when the hand of the system moves them.

## Praised design

Even the critical reviews praise the core hand-management engine, snappy simultaneous card play, and strong production. The complaint profile is refinement pressure, not rejection.

## Open questions

- Larger-scale BGG comment extraction could quantify whether catch-up complaints are a minority taste issue or a dominant friction.
