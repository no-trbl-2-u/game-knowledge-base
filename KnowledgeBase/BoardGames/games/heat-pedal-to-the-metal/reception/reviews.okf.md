## Summary

Reception is broadly positive. Reviewers praise speed, tension, simultaneous play, production, heat management, and content volume. Complaints are narrower: catch-up tools can feel artificial, iconography and modules add learning cost, slipstreaming may feel unearned, and the physical footprint is large.

## Player friction

- onboarding: icons and multi-phase turns need learning.
- setup-teardown: the base game is manageable, but large boards and module mats create table demands.
- randomness: Stress, hand draws, and slipstream opportunity can obscure agency for some players.
- strategic-depth: the core race can feel too simple before modules are added.

## Praised design

Simultaneous selection reduces downtime. Heat turns speed into a debt. Corners make spatial tempo matter. Modules extend the shelf life without changing the core identity.
