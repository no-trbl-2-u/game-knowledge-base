## Summary

Heat: Pedal to the Metal is a 1–6 player racing game about timing speed, gear, heat, and corners. It uses hand management and deck pressure to create racing tempo: higher gears demand more cards, heat clogs future hands, and catch-up tools keep the pack contested.

## Rules / Mechanics

The race is governed by a compact round skeleton: shift, commit cards, reveal, move, resolve position/risk, and refill. The notable pressure is that immediate speed purchases future disorder: Heat and Stress change the quality of later hands.

## Player friction

Reception sources identify friction around catch-up permissiveness, icon learning, table footprint, and whether early non-module races feel too simple for experienced players.

## Praised design

Praise concentrates on simultaneous planning, tense heat management, strong production, meaningful pack dynamics, and the ability to support solo, low-count, and full-table races with modules.
