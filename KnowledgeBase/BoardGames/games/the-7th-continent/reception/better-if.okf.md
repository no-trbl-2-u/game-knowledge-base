# Better-if — The 7th Continent

## Campaign-progression opportunity

- Claim: The campaign is better when persistent state, unlocks, and next-session obligations can be audited quickly.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: The save system allows the group to interrupt and resume later, while the card-based continent, inventory, experience, and curse progress form the persistent adventure state.
  Confidence: medium

## Friction opportunity

- Claim: A targeted improvement should reduce the documented friction without flattening the campaign's consequential choices.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The action deck doubles as the group's life force, so repeated failure and long exploration can create attrition pressure, while the enormous card system demands careful sorting, storage, and bookkeeping.
  Confidence: high
