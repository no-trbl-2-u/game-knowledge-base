# Better-if — Middara: Unintentional Malum – Act 1

## Campaign-progression opportunity

- Claim: The campaign is better when persistent state, unlocks, and next-session obligations can be audited quickly.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Campaign persistence is explicit: encounters are not repeated, win/loss outcomes alter the continuing story, hidden decks unlock campaign content, and XP advances characters.
  Confidence: medium

## Friction opportunity

- Claim: A targeted improvement should reduce the documented friction without flattening the campaign's consequential choices.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The main operational friction is campaign commitment: 3–4 hour weekly sessions over six to eight months, a 500-page Adventure Book, and a large reading/component footprint.
  Confidence: high
