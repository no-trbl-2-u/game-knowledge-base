## Summary

Setup builds a central game board, display, association board, conservation project row, final scoring deck, and individual zoo maps with action cards, workers, money, counters, tokens, and starting hand selection.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Global setup places the game board centrally and puts the Break token on the start space corresponding to player count.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 4: "Place the game board in the center"; "place it on the start space of the Break track that corresponds to your player count."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The Zoo card deck is shuffled and six cards are dealt face down to the display, turned face up at the end of setup.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 4: "Shuffle the Zoo cards"; "Deal 6 face-down cards... This is the display."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Nine Bonus tiles are shuffled, four are placed on blank yellow Bonus spaces, and five are returned to the box.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 4: "place a random Bonus tile face up on each... 4 blank yellow Bonus spaces"; "Return the remaining 5 tiles to the box."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The Association board receives one partner zoo of each continent and one university of each kind.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 5: "Place 1 partner zoo of each continent and 1 university of each kind".
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Base Conservation Project cards are shuffled; four are revealed in a four-player game and three otherwise.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 5: "In a 4-player game, place the 4 topmost cards face up... otherwise the 3 topmost."
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Player setup summary from extracted rulebook and search snippets: each player chooses a color, takes matching components, places appeal/conservation/reputation counters, keeps seven player tokens on the zoo map's conservation bonus spaces, places association workers with one active, takes 25 money, draws eight Zoo cards, keeps four, draws final scoring cards, and reveals the display after hand selection.

## Player friction

Setup is broad: central boards, card market, multiple project rows, many tiles, individual maps, and hidden final scoring. The box includes organizers, and the rulebook recommends organizing enclosures and reserves.

## Praised design

The setup makes long-term goals visible early: base conservation projects and final scoring cards orient opening choices.

## Better-if opportunities

- setup/teardown: tray-driven setup order and player-count packs.
- onboarding: first-game starting decks should be integrated into setup rather than optional lookup.
- component clarity: display when project slots are blocked in two-player games.
