## Summary

War of the Ring sets the Free Peoples against the Shadow in an asymmetric Middle-earth campaign. The game is primarily a two-player contest, with a team configuration for four players.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The two sides have different victory routes: the Free Peoples can destroy the Ring or achieve a smaller military target, while the Shadow can corrupt the Ring or achieve a larger military target.
  Source: src-018, src-003
  Evidence: Official victory rules and the independent review both describe Ring-based and military victory conditions for the two factions.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The game uses a map of regions, armies, action dice, event cards, and the hidden Fellowship as its main state-bearing systems.
  Source: src-011, src-012, src-014, src-002
  Evidence: Official page images for the turn, action, and Fellowship sections, together with the independent rules overview, describe map movement, dice actions, cards, and Fellowship movement as the core loop.
  Confidence: high
