# Turn structure

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Each turn has three ordered phases: play or discard one card, fill the hand, then shuffle the Limbo pile.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 6, Game turn, enumerates the three phases.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: During the fill phase, the player draws until the hand returns to five cards and resolves each Door or Dream before continuing.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed pages 8-9 explain Location, Door, and Dream draw handling and continuing until five cards are held.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: At turn end, every card placed in Limbo that turn is shuffled back into the deck; if Limbo is empty, that phase is skipped.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 10, Shuffle the Limbo pile, states both outcomes.
  Confidence: high
