# Objectives, exile, and endgame

- Claim: A non-exiled player's secret objective can require main-objective completion plus private conditions; betrayal objectives can instead reward colony failure.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.2, 6, 14-15.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Exile immediately adds an exiled objective and forces colony survivors controlled by that player to non-colony locations; the exiled player then loses crisis contribution and voting privileges among other changes.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p.14, "Exile."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Two exiled non-betrayers cause morale to drop to zero immediately.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p.14 important note.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Multiple players may win, and everyone may lose.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p.14.
  Confidence: high

The exact victory semantics of all 8 dual-sided main objectives and 40 secret/betrayal/exiled objectives remain an open card-text authority class.
