# Actions and hazards

- Claim: Die-requiring actions include attack, search, barricade, clean waste, attract, and some survivor abilities; attack/search require a result meeting the survivor stat.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.8-10.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Card play, crisis contribution, movement, food spending, requesting, handing off, and exile voting do not inherently consume an action die.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.10-11.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Moving a survivor normally triggers exposure; killing a zombie also triggers exposure for its killer.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.11 and 13.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Exposure can produce a wound, frostbite, or bite; bites kill and may spread through occupied locations.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p.11, "Rolling for Exposure" and "Spreading a Bite Effect."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Search draws from the current location deck and may add noise to see additional cards.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.9-10.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A search begins with one card; each noise token placed in an empty noise space permits one additional draw, after which the player keeps one drawn card and returns the rest to the bottom of that location deck.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.9-10, "Search."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A barricade action spends any action-die result to place a barricade in an empty entrance space at the acting survivor's location; during zombie addition, a would-be zombie destroys a barricade at a full entrance and is not placed.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.10 and 13, "Barricade" and "Adding Zombies."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Zombie attacks spend a die meeting the attacker's stat, kill the target, and expose the attacker; survivor attacks instead reroll the spent die, wound the target and take a random card only on a result at or below the target's attack value, and do not use exposure.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.8-9, "Attack."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A bite kills the exposed survivor and passes to the lowest-influence survivor sharing that location; each recipient either dies deliberately to stop the spread or rerolls exposure, where only a blank saves that survivor and stops the chain.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p.11, "Spreading a Bite Effect."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: At the beginning of a player's turn, the player on the right secretly draws a Crossroads card for that active player; it resolves only if its trigger occurs during the turn, and action-based triggers normally check after the action resolves.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp.8 and 12, "Resolving Crossroads Cards."
  Confidence: high
