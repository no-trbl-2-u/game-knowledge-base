## Why it qualifies

The strongest campaign-RPG fit in the set: inherited characters, persistent scars and objects, branching narrative residue, and role reversal at the haunt.

- **Source:** src-001, src-002
- **Evidence:** Families repeatedly explore the same cursed house across generations until each chapter turns cooperation into a bespoke haunt.
- **Confidence:** high

## Core loop

Players reveal room tiles, gain items and omens, then trigger a haunt that assigns asymmetric objectives. Campaign chapters permanently alter cards, rooms, families, and the house chronology.

- **Source:** src-002
- **Evidence:** Publisher rules/product framing establishes the shared threat, player-role structure, and individual or traitor incentives summarized above.
- **Confidence:** medium-high

## Design signal

**Praise:** Legacy continuity gives familiar Betrayal chaos context and makes the house itself feel authored by the group.

**Friction:** Scenario ambiguity, swingy dice, uneven haunt balance, and irreversible campaign mistakes can injure trust in the rules.
