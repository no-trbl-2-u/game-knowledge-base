# Endgame and Post-Campaign Play

- Claim: The first side to complete its haunt's victory condition wins; there is no separate score track for normal game victory.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "Winning the Game," printed Rules p.21 (PDF p.12).
  Confidence: high

- Claim: During the campaign, chapter end proceeds by reading the directed Bleak Journal ending, recording each character's fate, returning cards as directed, and recording the house history.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "During the Campaign," printed Rules p.21 (PDF p.12).
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The official bundle includes the Bleak Journal entries needed by book-directed endings, but the ordered campaign cards that select and modify those transitions were not acquired.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: PDF pp.68-84 contain numbered Bleak Journal entries; printed Rules pp.4-5 assign campaign sequencing to the Legacy decks.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: After the campaign, play uses a free-play setup, a fixed haunt threshold and chart, no Bleak Journal game-end step, and the Free Play ending in the selected haunt.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "After the Campaign: Free Play," printed Rules foldout pp.2 and 23 (PDF p.2).
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The post-campaign game remains replayable with the campaign-created house, families, history, and a larger haunt pool.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "At the end of the campaign, you will still be able to play Betrayal Legacy" and "you will not have seen most of the haunts," printed Rules foldout (PDF p.2).
  Confidence: high
