# Haunt and Campaign Transitions

- Claim: Before a haunt, drawing an Omen after discovering an Omen tile can require a haunt roll; during the campaign, the current PAUSE/Legacy instruction determines the trigger threshold or alternative trigger.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "Making a Haunt Roll," printed Rules p.16 (PDF p.10).
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Once triggered, the haunt uses one or both haunt books, identifies the traitor or other role structure, gives each side public framing plus private setup/objectives, and then resumes play under the haunt's rules.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "Starting the Haunt," "Public Information," and "Splitting Up," printed Rules pp.16-18 (PDF pp.10-11).
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The two haunt books are complete in the official PDF, but scenario prose and solutions are intentionally not reproduced here.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: PDF pp.14-45 are Secrets of Survival and pp.46-67 are Traitor's Tome.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The campaign's ordered Legacy cards tell players when to draw, pause, stop, alter materials, open concealed content, and apply persistent changes.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "The Legacy Decks" and "Adding Materials," printed Rules pp.4-5 (PDF p.4).
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The Folium Infernum is a governing source because its thirteen concealed doors hide stickers, including rules stickers that can immediately modify the rulebook.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: "Folium Infernum" and "Adding Materials," printed Rules pp.4-5 (PDF p.4).
  Confidence: high

## Coverage limit

The books are acquired; the ordered Legacy/Purgatory decks, Folium contents, and sealed box/envelope are not. No campaign chapter is represented as fully governed without those materials.
