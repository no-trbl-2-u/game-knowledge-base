# Independent Reviews

## Praise

- Claim: Shut Up & Sit Down considered the Legacy layer strong, especially its continuing story, changing rooms/cards, heirlooms, and post-campaign replay value.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: "The game's legacy elements are as good as any I've ever seen" and "the first legacy game I'm actually excited to continue owning after I'm done with it."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The same review found the revised rules formatting and haunt implementation clearer than the original Betrayal.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: "better rules formatting and layout" and "Haunts are much easier to actually implement."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Polygon praised the Legacy deck's narrative role and the campaign's ability to build player-family identities through persistent changes.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "focusing more than ever on the narrative" and "does an exceptional job of building identities for the players at the table."
  Confidence: high

## Friction

- Claim: Shut Up & Sit Down found the experience highly variable: chance can deny meaningful participation, haunts can overrun their welcome or finish abruptly, and player elimination remains uncomfortable for some groups.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: "sometimes end in victory before a single full round passes" and "a series of card draws just pushes you out of the game."
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The review also criticized loose component clips and swingy dice.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: "clips ... will inevitably slip around" and "the most dickheaded dice."
  Confidence: high

## Review boundary

Reception is not rules authority. These observations describe two identified reviewers' experiences and are not generalized into universal player sentiment.
