## Summary

The reception signal is strong enough to explain the game's persistence: campaign continuity and hero growth are repeatedly identified as the strongest appeal. The main counterweight is scenario sensitivity—quest goals and map presentation can determine whether the Overlord-versus-heroes contest feels fair and exploratory.

## Praised design

- Claim: Campaign play is widely treated as the game's strongest element.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: Review search result: “The campaign’s the best bit of Descent.”
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: The game offers a clear role split between an Overlord and heroic adventurers.
  Source: src-001, src-003
  Evidence: BGG and publisher descriptions both foreground the Overlord-versus-heroes campaign.
  Confidence: high

## Player friction

- Claim: Quest balance can vary substantially because outcomes depend on quest goals.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: Review search result: “balance ultimately falls down to the goals in the quests” and is “all over the place.”
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Revealing the full board at setup can reduce the feeling of dungeon discovery.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: Review search result says the full board is set up from the start and gives “no real feeling of mystery.”
  Confidence: medium
