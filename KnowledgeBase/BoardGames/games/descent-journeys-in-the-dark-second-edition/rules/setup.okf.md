## Summary

Setup has three layers: general quest preparation, hero preparation, and Overlord preparation. The chosen quest determines map tiles, tokens, figures, cards, and special instructions.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Players select a quest and build its map from the quest guide before play.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook, “General Setup,” pp. 5–6.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Heroes choose a hero, receive starting equipment and class material, and place figures as directed by the quest.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook, “Hero Setup,” pp. 5–6.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The Overlord prepares monster groups and Overlord cards according to the quest.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook, “Overlord Setup,” p. 5.
  Confidence: high

## Player friction

The full quest map is prepared before play. A review source specifically identifies this as reducing the feeling of discovery in an unexplored dungeon.
