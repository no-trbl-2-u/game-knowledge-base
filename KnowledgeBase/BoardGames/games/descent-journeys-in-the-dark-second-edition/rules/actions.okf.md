## Summary

Hero actions include movement, attacks, resting, searching, opening doors, and special abilities. Movement and attacks are constrained by spaces, range, line of sight, and the relevant figure or card text.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A hero's two actions can include movement, attack, search, opening a door, resting, or an ability, subject to quest and component text.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook, “Hero Turn in Detail,” pp. 8–10.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Attacks use the attacker’s listed dice and resolve range, damage, surges, and defense.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook, “Attack,” pp. 9 and 12–14.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Searching a space with a search token is an action available to an adjacent hero or a hero occupying that space.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook, “Search,” p. 10.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Two-action turns make opportunity cost visible. The same hero may reposition, attack, investigate, or convert tempo into recovery, but cannot bank unused action capacity.
