## Summary

A quest ends when its specific victory condition is met. Campaign mode then converts the result into future state: players resolve rewards and consequences, spend experience, shop, and continue or begin another quest.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Quest victory and defeat are determined by the individual quest’s objective and outcome text.

## Rules / Mechanics

The endgame is not a single score track. It is a state transition: quest outcome changes the campaign position, and the campaign phase turns earned resources into new character capability.
