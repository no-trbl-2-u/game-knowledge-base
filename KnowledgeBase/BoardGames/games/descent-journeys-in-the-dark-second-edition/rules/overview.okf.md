## Summary

The Overlord opposes a party of heroes across quests in dangerous locations. Heroes seek quest objectives while fighting monsters, acquiring riches, and surviving the Overlord's plot.

## Rules / Mechanics

The central structure is a scenario engine wrapped around persistent character roles. Tactical play is local and measurable; campaign play supplies continuity through experience, skills, items, and quest outcomes.
