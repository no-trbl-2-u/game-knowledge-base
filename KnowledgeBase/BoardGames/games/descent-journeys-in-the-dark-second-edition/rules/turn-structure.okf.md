## Summary

Each round proceeds through hero turns and the Overlord turn. On a hero turn, one hero performs actions; after all heroes have acted, the Overlord activates monster groups. The round then resets for the next cycle.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A hero may perform two actions during the hero turn.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook, “Hero Turn Summary,” p. 7, and “Hero Turn in Detail,” p. 8.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Monsters activate by group, with all monsters of one type activated before the next group.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook, “Overlord Turn,” p. 11.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Campaign play adds an inter-quest phase after a quest ends.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook, “The Campaign Phase,” p. 20.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The alternating structure gives heroes a shared planning window and gives the Overlord a concentrated response window. Group activation reduces bookkeeping while preserving tactical sequencing.
