## Strongest design lessons

Persistent hero growth is strongest when campaign resources alter future tactical choices rather than merely recording victory points. The Overlord role makes the opposition legible and human.

## Strongest player complaints

Quest-specific balance can dominate the experience, and revealing the full map can trade away discovery for setup speed.

## This game would be better if...

...scenario objectives and information reveal were tuned together: the quest should remain legible enough to teach while retaining enough uncertainty to make exploration meaningful.
