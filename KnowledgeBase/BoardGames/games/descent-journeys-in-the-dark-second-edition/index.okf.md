## Summary

Descent Second Edition is a competitive-cooperative dungeon-campaign game: one player is the Overlord and up to four others play persistent heroes. Quests combine modular-map movement, dice combat, class skills, equipment, fatigue, experience, and campaign consequences.

## Rules / Mechanics

The game is not a deckbuilder: its core loop is quest setup, alternating hero and monster activations, movement, dice-based attacks, abilities, objectives, and campaign advancement. Cards represent classes, skills, items, and Overlord effects, but deck construction is not the central mechanism.
