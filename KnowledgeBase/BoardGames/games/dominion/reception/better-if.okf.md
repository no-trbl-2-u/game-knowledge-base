# Better-if opportunities

## Source-backed improvement inferences

- Claim: Dominion would be better for groups seeking interaction if the base game offered one more consequential interaction channel without slowing its short turns.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Board Game Quest explicitly lists lower player interaction as a minor flaw while praising quick turns and accessibility.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Dominion would be easier to onboard if its first-game guidance made the action/economy balance more explicit for new players.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The rulebook presents many Kingdom-card choices, while the review says new players can choose how much strategy to apply; this supports an onboarding opportunity, not a claim that the rules are unclear.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Expansion breadth can create maintenance or selection fatigue for players who want the base game’s compact identity.
  Source: src-003, src-007
  Evidence: The review lists expansion fatigue as a minor flaw, while the community errata article demonstrates a broader ongoing rules ecosystem; neither is treated as a measured consensus.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: The base game’s quick turns are a strength to preserve when considering any interaction or onboarding improvement.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The review praises quick turns and the short session length in its Final Thoughts.
  Confidence: high
