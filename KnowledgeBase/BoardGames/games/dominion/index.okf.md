# Dominion

## Scope

Current Rio Grande Games English 2nd Edition base game only. Expansions, promos, online play, digital adaptations, and card-text completeness are excluded.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Dominion is a physical 2–4 player deck-building game published by Rio Grande Games under stock RIO531.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: The official product page lists Dominion®, “Type: Deck Building,” “Players: 2-4 Players,” and “Stock #: RIO531.”
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The base box rulebook describes a 500-card inventory plus a trash mat, organizer tray, organizing inlay, and rule booklet.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official rulebook printed page 2, Components, lists those objects and counts 500 cards.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Each player begins with 3 Estates and 7 Coppers, while the shared Supply uses seven always-present base piles plus ten variable Kingdom piles.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official rulebook printed page 3, setup paragraph.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: A game ends when the Province pile is empty or at least three Supply piles are empty, after which players total Victory Points and the highest total wins.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official rulebook printed pages 4 and 6, game-end and scoring paragraphs.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Board Game Quest’s independent review treats Dominion as a short, accessible foundational deck-builder while noting lower player interaction and a tacked-on theme as minor flaws.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Review dated Feb 26, 2013, Final Thoughts and rating sections.
  Confidence: high
