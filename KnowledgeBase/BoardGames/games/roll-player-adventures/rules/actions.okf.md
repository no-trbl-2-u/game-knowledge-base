## Rules / Mechanics

- Claim: Skill checks are cooperative dice-placement tests: players build a pool, manipulate dice with hand cards and class abilities, then cover matching slots.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Skill Checks, pp. 12–13.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Combat lasts up to three rounds and uses the same shared-pool and slot-covering structure, with counterattacks for uncovered slots.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Combat, pp. 14–15.
  Confidence: high

  Source: src-003
