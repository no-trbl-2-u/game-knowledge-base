## Better-if opportunities

- Claim: The campaign would be easier to re-enter if its persistent state were made faster to audit between sessions.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Saving Campaign Progress requires preserving hands, titles, gold, XP, faction marks, and campaign-track state.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: The branching story could provide stronger replay differentiation if more downstream content visibly depended on earlier choices.
  Source: src-005
  Evidence: Review reports alternate choices but a broadly similar narrative arc.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: The shared dice pool makes randomness a party resource problem rather than a private roll, but stamina costs and card limits create a meaningful preparation tradeoff.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Skill Checks, pp. 12–13, and Combat, pp. 14–15.
  Confidence: high
