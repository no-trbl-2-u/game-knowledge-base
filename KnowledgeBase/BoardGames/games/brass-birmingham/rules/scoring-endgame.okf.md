## Scoring and endgame

- Claim: At the end of each era, players score Link tiles by adjacent locations and score flipped Industry tiles by the VP value printed on each tile.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The end-of-era scoring steps award Link points for adjacent locations and then score the bottom-left VP values of flipped Industry tiles.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: At the end of the Canal Era, all level-1 Industry tiles are removed from the board, while level-2 and higher tiles remain.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The Canal Era maintenance procedure removes all level-1 Industry tiles from the board and leaves higher-level tiles in place.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: After Rail Era scoring, the player with the most Victory Points wins; ties are broken by highest income, then most money, and then a draw.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The winning section lists the winner and the three tie-break steps in that order.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Income is not collected at the end of the final round of the game.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The end-of-round income procedure explicitly gives the final-round exception.
  Confidence: high
