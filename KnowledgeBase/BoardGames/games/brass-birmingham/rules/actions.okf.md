## Action findings

- Claim: The six listed actions are Build, Network, Develop, Sell, Loan, and Scout; Passing is also allowed instead of an action.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The official actions list names those six actions and describes Passing as an available choice that still requires a discard.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Build places an Industry tile, requires a matching Location or Industry card unless a Wild card is used, and may require coal, iron, or beer according to the tile and era.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The Build procedure specifies the matching card, tile placement restrictions, cost payment, and required resources.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Network places Canal Links during the Canal Era or Rail Links during the Rail Era and expands the player's network.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The Network procedure limits the link type by era and defines the placement, cost, and connection requirements.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Develop removes one or two lowest-level Industry tiles from the Player Mat by paying one iron per removed tile, exposing higher-level industries.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The Develop procedure specifies removing one or two tiles, consuming one iron for each, and gaining access to higher-level tiles.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Sell flips connected Cotton Mill, Manufacturer, or Pottery tiles when the player can consume the required beer.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The Sell procedure requires a Merchant connection and beer, then flips each selected unflipped tile and advances income.
  Confidence: high
