## Summary

Each of the six rounds proceeds through income, Gaia, actions, and round-end phases.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Income is collected first, then power in the Gaia area advances, then players take actions until all have passed.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 10–17 labels the four phases and states that the action phase continues until everyone has passed.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Passing sets a player's next-round order and exchanges the current booster for one of the available boosters, except in the last round.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 16 explains pass order, booster exchange, and the sixth-round exception.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

During the action phase, players alternate one main action at a time. Free conversions and passive power gains can happen around turns, but passing removes that player from the remaining action sequence.

## Open questions

The optional variable turn-order card is noted by the rulebook but is not treated as part of the core turn sequence here.
