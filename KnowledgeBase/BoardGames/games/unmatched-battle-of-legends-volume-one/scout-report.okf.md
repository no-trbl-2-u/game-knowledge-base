## Strongest design lesson

A small shared combat grammar can carry asymmetric depth when each fighter's custom card set changes how movement, attack timing, defense, and hand pressure are valued.

## Strongest player friction

The independent review's specific friction is player-count dependent: four-player team play can bog down as more fighters occupy the battlefield, while the reviewer recommends approaching the set primarily as a duel.

## This game would be better if...

The team mode made its spatial congestion and fighter-count burden easier to manage without diluting the duel's bluffing and asymmetric matchup texture.
