## Turn structure

- Claim: A player must take two actions on each turn and may choose the same action twice.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The rules state, “On your turn, you must take 2 actions,” and permit two different actions or the same action twice.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The available actions are maneuver, scheme, and attack.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The turn rules list MANEUVER, SCHEME, and ATTACK as the possible actions.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A maneuver first draws the top card and may then move fighters.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The maneuver procedure makes drawing the top card mandatory before optional movement.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A hand may exceed seven cards during a turn but must be reduced to seven at the end of that turn.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The draw and hand-limit rules allow more than seven during the turn and require discard down to seven at its end.
  Confidence: high
