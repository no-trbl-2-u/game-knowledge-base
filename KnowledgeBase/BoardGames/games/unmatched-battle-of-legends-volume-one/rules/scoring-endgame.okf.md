  Source: src-002

  Confidence: high

- Claim: In a standard game, defeating the opponent’s hero ends the game and wins it.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The winning-the-game rules say the game ends immediately when the opponent’s hero is reduced to zero health.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: In team play, both opposing heroes must be defeated for the opposing team to win.
  Source: src-002
