## Summary

Cthulhu: Death May Die is a cooperative horror dungeon-crawl in which investigators disrupt a ritual and then fight a summoned Elder One. Episode and Elder One boxes combine into a bounded scenario, while investigator stress, sanity, skills, discoveries, and wounds create character growth during play.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The game supports 1–5 players who control investigators and win or lose together.

## Rules / Mechanics

The game is an RPG/role-playing fit through investigator roles, skill leveling, insanity, inventory-like Discovery cards, scenario objectives, and boss-stage progression. It is not a deckbuilder: cards provide scenario, enemy, mythos, discovery, and stage content, but players do not construct or grow a personal draw deck.
