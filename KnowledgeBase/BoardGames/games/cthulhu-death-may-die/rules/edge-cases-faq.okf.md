## Summary

The rulebook resolves several timing boundaries that matter to play: enemy contact during the investigate/fight phase, sanity thresholds, and Elder One attack restrictions.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: If an investigator enters an enemy space, or an enemy enters the investigator's space during the phase, that enemy attacks even if it was not there at phase start.

  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 8.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The Elder One may attack after summoning but cannot be attacked until the ritual is disrupted.
