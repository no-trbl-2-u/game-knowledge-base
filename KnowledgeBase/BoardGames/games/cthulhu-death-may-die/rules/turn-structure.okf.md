## Summary

A turn has four phases: three investigator actions, Mythos resolution, investigation or enemy attacks, and end-of-turn resolution.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The active investigator takes three actions, draws and resolves a Mythos card, investigates or fights, then resolves end-of-turn effects.
  Source: src-002

  Confidence: high

  Source: src-002
