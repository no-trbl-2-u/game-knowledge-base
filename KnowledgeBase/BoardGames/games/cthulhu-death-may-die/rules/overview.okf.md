## Summary

Each game combines one Episode box with one Elder One box. The Episode supplies map, ritual, monsters, Mythos and Discovery content; the Elder One supplies its figure, minions, Mythos cards, and four Stage cards.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Episode and Elder One boxes are combined at setup and their components are not mixed with other boxes.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 4.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Investigators have wound, stress, sanity, and three skill tracks.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 8.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The scenario layer supplies a modular board and objective; the character layer supplies persistent investigator identity within the session. Sanity thresholds activate insanity and raise skills, making risk both a liability and a progression engine.
