## Setup

- Claim: Each player chooses a Gearloc and prepares that character's mat, chip, starting health, initiative die, and stat dice.
  Source: src-003, src-006
  Evidence: (src-003) The accessible rules summary states that each player chooses a Gearloc and places the Gearloc mat, chip, starting health chips, initiative die, and stat dice. (src-006) "Each player chooses a Gearloc and places their selected Gearloc Mat, Gearloc Chip (with # of Starting Health Chips underneath it), Initiative (Ini) Die, and Stat Dice as shown."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The party selects (or randomly draws) a Tyrant, places its Card/Chip/Die beside the Battle Mat, and sets the Day Counter to 1; the number of Days on the Tyrant Card sets the encounter-deck size and game length.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "Select a Tyrant or draw one at random... Place the Tyrant's Card, Chip, and Die to the left of the Battle Mat. Place the Day Counter Card and Chip (set to 1) directly below your Tyrant Card."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The Encounter Deck is built by drawing (Days − 3) cards from the shuffled General Encounter Deck (or the Solo Encounter Deck for one Gearloc), combining them with the chosen Tyrant's 1-3 Tyrant Encounter cards, then placing Special Encounters 001-003 on top in order.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "Draw Encounter Cards equal to the # of Days ( # ) shown on your Tyrant Card minus 3 cards. Shuffle these together with the Tyrant Encounter Card(s)... find Special Encounters 001-003... and place them, in order, on top of your newly created Encounter Deck."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Baddies are set up per-Tyrant: using the Baddie Type icons shown on the Tyrant Card, matching Baddie chips are flipped face-down, shuffled, and split into separate 1Pt/5Pt/20Pt "Active Stacks"; defeated Baddies replenish an emptied Active Stack by reshuffling.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "find all Baddies of these types. Flip them face down, shuffle, and create separate 1Pt, 5Pt, and 20Pt Baddie 'Active Stacks'... If you ever run out of Baddies in an Active Stack, reshuffle defeated #Pt Baddies to replenish that Active Stack."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The group collectively chooses a Party Leader (who makes tie-breaking decisions) and a game mode during setup; Adventurer Mode is the recommended starting mode.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "Collectively choose a Party Leader, who is responsible for making tie-breaking decisions. Choose a game mode to play on as well... Adventurer Mode is recommended to start."
  Confidence: high
