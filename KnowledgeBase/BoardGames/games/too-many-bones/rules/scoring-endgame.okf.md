## Scoring / endgame

- Claim: The primary victory condition is defeating the selected Tyrant within its printed day allotment, which the party earns the right to challenge by accumulating enough Progress Points from daily Encounters.
  Source: src-001, src-003, src-006
  Evidence: "Reach and defeat the selected Tyrant within its day allotment to win"; the Encounter Phase lets the party "challenge the Tyrant" once enough Progress Points are earned.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: A battle Encounter choice succeeds if at least one Gearloc remains on the Battle Mat after all Baddies are defeated; a peaceful choice succeeds automatically unless the card states otherwise. An unsuccessful Encounter (peaceful or battle) grants no rewards and the party proceeds straight to the Recovery Phase.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "A battle Encounter choice is successful if at least one Gearloc remains on the Battle Mat after all Baddies are defeated"; "If an Encounter is unsuccessful ... your party does not gain any rewards."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: If every Gearloc is KO'd by the end of a battle (a full party wipe), the party earns no Progress or Rewards for that day.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: Rulebook — "no surviving Gearlocs at the end of battle (all Gearlocs KO'd), there will be no Progress or Rewards for that day."
  Confidence: high

No conventional victory-point scoring system exists; Progress Points gate the Tyrant challenge rather than accumulating toward a final score.
