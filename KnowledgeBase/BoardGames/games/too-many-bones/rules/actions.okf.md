## Rules / Mechanics

- Claim: A Gearloc's Dexterity Stat is the maximum number of dice (any combination of Attack, Defense, and Skill dice) it may roll each turn; each Attack or Defense die costs 1 Dex to roll in battle, and Dex may also be spent to move on the Battle Mat at 1 Dex per position.
  Source: src-003, src-006
  Evidence: "Your Dex is the max # of dice you may roll each turn (any combination of Atk, Def & Skill Dice)"; "Each Atk Die costs 1 Dex to roll in battle"; "Each Def Die costs 1 Dex to roll in battle."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Attack and Defense dice are never Exhausted by use and are available every turn, unlike Skill dice, which must be Exhausted when used from Active or Locked slots (unless a card states otherwise).
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "Unlike Skill Dice, Def and Atk Dice are never Exhausted and are available to you every turn"; "Skill Dice in these slots must be Exhausted when used (unless otherwise stated)."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Rolled Defense dice are placed in Active slots; their total reduces incoming damage, and all incoming damage (except True Damage, which applies directly to HP) must be absorbed by Active Defense dice before any HP is lost. Defense dice currently sitting in Active slots also reduce how many Defense dice can be rolled that turn.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "Def Dice must be used for all incoming Dmg first before removing HP, except True Dmg, which is applied directly to HP"; "The total Def Dice currently in Active slots will reduce available Def Dice for rolling this turn."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Rolled Bones results from Attack or Defense dice may instead be placed in a Gearloc's Backup Plan without reducing that die type's future availability; up to 5 Bones total may be stored in the Backup Plan at once, used left to right in the order earned.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "Each rolled [Bones] may be used in your Backup Plan (does not reduce # of Atk/Def Dice available to you in future turns)"; "Up to 5 total [Bones] may be stored within the BP from turn to turn."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Position on the Battle Mat matters: attack form (Melee, Ranged, or Melee-Ranged) determines which units a Gearloc may target — Melee only adjacent units, Ranged any unit — and also determines starting position options.
  Source: src-003, src-006
  Evidence: "Melee Gearlocs can only target adjacent units. Ranged Gearlocs can target any unit."
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Loot and Training Point rewards earned from successful Encounters carry forward and strengthen the party for later encounters and the Tyrant fight.
  Source: src-003, src-006
  Evidence: Rulebook Reward Phase grants Loot, Trove Loot, and Training Points from a successful Encounter choice, which are then usable in later Recovery Phases and battles.
  Confidence: high
