## Summary

Players control Gearlocs across a short adventure arc. Each day cycles through New Day, Encounter, Reward, and Recovery phases; successful encounters improve the party with Progress Points and Training Points before the party challenges a selected Tyrant.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The base game is built around Gearloc characters, dice, encounter cards, loot, Tyrants, and a battle mat.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official component list names Gearloc chips and mats, skill/stat/attack/defense/effect/initiative dice, encounter and loot cards, Tyrant cards, and a battle mat.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Dice are developed as character equipment rather than merely rolled from a fixed pool.
  Source: src-003, src-006
  Evidence: "Find your Gearloc's 16 Skill Dice and place them in a Storage Tray" (rulebook setup); skill dice are added to a Gearloc's mat through Training and Recovery rather than drawn from a shared pool.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The game objective is to reach and defeat a selected Tyrant within its day allotment; the party earns Progress Points and Training Points through daily Encounters to prepare for that fight.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: "Reach and defeat the selected Tyrant within its day allotment to win." Rulebook, Game Objective.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Each day of the adventure runs four phases in order — New Day, Encounter, Reward, Recovery — then repeats until the Tyrant is challenged or the party is defeated.
  Source: src-006
  Evidence: Rulebook lists "1. New Day Phase / 2. Encounter Phase / 3. Reward Phase / 4. Recovery Phase" and states the day ends and restarts at New Day Phase.
  Confidence: high
