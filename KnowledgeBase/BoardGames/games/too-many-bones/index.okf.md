## Summary

*Too Many Bones* is a cooperative dice-driven RPG board game in which players develop distinct Gearloc characters through encounters, skill dice, stat growth, loot, and a final Tyrant confrontation.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The game is explicitly sold as a dice-builder RPG and includes Gearloc mats, skill dice, stat dice, encounter cards, loot cards, Tyrant cards, and a battle mat.

## Rules / Mechanics

Gearlocs allocate dice to attack, defense, and skills; encounters grant progression and loot; battle resolution uses a lane-based mat, initiative, baddies, and Tyrant-specific objectives. The campaign is a sequence of encounters culminating in a selected Tyrant.
