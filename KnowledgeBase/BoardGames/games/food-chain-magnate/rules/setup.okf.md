## Summary

Setup creates the employee and milestone supply, builds a randomly selected city map, funds the bank, establishes player order, and places one starting restaurant per player.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The core set includes map, house, garden, marketing, restaurant, turn-order, employee, milestone, and bank-reserve materials.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The component inventory on pages 2–3 lists 20 map tiles, 8 houses, 8 gardens, 16 marketing tiles, three restaurants per player, 222 employee cards, 84 milestone cards, and 18 bank-reserve cards.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The map is created by randomly selecting map tiles, with map size varying by player count.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Page 5 provides a player-count table with 3x3, 3x4, 4x4, and 5x4 map sizes and begins the map setup instruction with random tile selection.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The initial bank receives $50 multiplied by the number of players.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Page 7 instructs players to place $50 times the player count into the bank pool.
  Confidence: high
