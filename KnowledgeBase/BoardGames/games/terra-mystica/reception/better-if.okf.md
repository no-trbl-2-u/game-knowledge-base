## Better-if opportunities

- Claim: Terra Mystica would be better if first-game onboarding exposed the action economy and faction differences with less front-loaded explanation.
  Source: src-003, src-004
  Evidence: Board Game Quest calls the game heavy with a steep learning curve; SPACE-BIFF! says the interlocking systems take a game or two to click.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Terra Mystica would be better if its cult track were integrated more clearly into the main development story.
  Source: src-003, src-002
  Evidence: Board Game Quest calls the cultist track disconnected; the rules make cult progress a separate board and scoring lane alongside building and area development.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Terra Mystica would be better if counterplay against a leader offered more avenues than blocking scarce land.
  Source: src-004, src-002
  Evidence: SPACE-BIFF! says an ahead player is answered by blocking them “and that's about it”; the rules center expansion, upgrades, and majority scoring rather than a named catch-up action.
  Confidence: medium

## Open questions

The last claim is an interpretation of the bounded core rules and one review, not proof that every edition or variant lacks catch-up effects.
