## Summary

Terra Mystica is a physical competitive area-development and engine-building game for 2–5 players. Players terraform terrain, expand connected settlements, upgrade buildings for income and powers, and compete on cult tracks and end-game majorities. This packet is bounded to the base game and does not claim expansion or variant coverage.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The official product page describes 14 peoples, terrain transformation, building upgrades, resource income, four cults, 2–5 players, and 30 minutes per person.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: The publisher page states that players choose one of 14 peoples, transform landscapes, upgrade buildings for workers, money, power, and priests, develop four cults, and lists “2 – 5” and “30 Minuten pro Person.”
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The official rules define the base game as a six-round contest ending with cult, connected-area, and resource scoring.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The rules label six rounds and three phases, then state on printed page 15 that the game ends after round 6 and awards cult, area, and resource points.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Independent reception places the game at the heavy, planning-intensive end of the Euro spectrum.
  Source: src-003, src-004
  Evidence: Board Game Quest calls it a heavy Euro with a steep learning curve; SPACE-BIFF! describes deep contemplation, interlocking systems, and drawn-out mathy turns.
  Confidence: high
