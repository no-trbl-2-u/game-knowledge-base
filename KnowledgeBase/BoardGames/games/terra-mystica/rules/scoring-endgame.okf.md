## Summary

Round tiles score actions during play; the final round ends with cult, connected-area, and resource scoring.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The game ends after every player passes in the action phase of round six.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 15 states that the game ends when all players have passed in round six and that no cult bonus is awarded then.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Final cult scoring awards 8, 4, and 2 points to the first three ranks on each of four cult tracks, with ties sharing the relevant points.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 15 gives the three rank values for each cult and explains tied-rank sharing.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Final area scoring ranks players by connected buildings for 18, 12, and 6 points, then remaining resources convert to points.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 15 lists the connected-building values and says every three coins' worth of remaining resources is one point in the standard case.
  Confidence: high
