## Summary

The rulebook separates a recommended first-game setup from the variable main setup.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The first-game setup uses prescribed peoples based on player count.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 4 lists recommended peoples for two through five players, including Witches and Nomads for two players.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Players prepare a main board, cult board, faction tableau, starting resources, power bowls, and markers before play.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed pages 3–5 list the board, cult board, faction materials, starting tracks, starting resources, power chips, and setup placement.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The variable main setup randomizes round scoring tiles and bonus cards, then allows free faction selection and starting-house placement.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 6 says the main game randomly determines round bonus cards and scoring tiles and differs from the beginner version by free faction and first-house choices.
  Confidence: high
