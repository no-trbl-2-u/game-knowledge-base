## Summary

A game lasts six rounds. Each round has income, actions, and cult-bonus/next-round preparation phases.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Each round begins with income from buildings, the round bonus card, and favor tiles.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 8 labels Phase I as income and explains that workers, coins, priests, and power come from visible tableau income plus cards and tiles.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: During the action phase, players take exactly one action at a time in clockwise order until all have passed.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 9 states that players act one at a time around the table and continue until nobody can or wants to act.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Phase III awards the current cult bonus and prepares the next round; round six omits the cult bonus.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 15 says rounds one through five proceed to preparation after the action phase and that round six has no cult bonus.
  Confidence: high
