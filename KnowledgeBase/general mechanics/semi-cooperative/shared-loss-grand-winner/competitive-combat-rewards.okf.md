## Type definition

Players need one another to survive a common battle, dungeon, or series of enemies, but compete over killing blows, loot, prestige, or another reward stream. Cooperation preserves the party; tactical obstruction determines who profits from its survival.

Representative implementation identified in the sweep:

- *Cutthroat Caverns*: the party must survive its monsters, but players steal killing blows and undermine rivals because only one adventurer wins.

## Distinct strengths

- Every hostile action has a survival cost, so attacks require timing and restraint.
- Rivalry occurs inside a clear social contract: assistance, theft, defection, and reconciliation are all expected.
- The subtype naturally generates stories of stolen kills, last-minute rescue, and betrayal delayed one round too long.

## Distinct dangers

- A player eliminated early loses access to both the cooperative fight and the competitive reward race.
- Unequal ability to steal or deny rewards can turn the endgame into kingmaking.
- Groups that do not enjoy direct aggression may experience the intended social contract as personal hostility rather than play.
