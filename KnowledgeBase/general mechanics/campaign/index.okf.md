# Campaign mechanics

This directory owns **mechanic-level** evidence about campaigns: persistent state, linked-session structure, progression, branching consequences, reset and re-entry rituals, and the cost of carrying a game across nights.

Canonical game identity, setup, turn rules, endgame, reception, provenance, and visuals belong under `KnowledgeBase/BoardGames/games/<slug>/`. Games may be cited here only as evidence for a campaign mechanism.

## Mechanic files

- [Campaign structure](campaign-structure.okf.md) — session cadence, scenario linkage, setup/teardown, and commitment legibility.
- [Campaign progression](campaign-progression.okf.md) — what persists, resets, unlocks, branches, or becomes irreversible.
- [Twenty-game evidence pass](top-20-evidence.okf.md) — persistence models and friction extracted from Bathcat’s ranked campaign-RPG intake.

## Filing law

1. A game earns `campaign-game` only when play is linked across sessions or scenarios by persistent state, authored progression, legacy change, or a formal save/continuation procedure.
2. A scenario anthology without consequential continuity is not automatically a campaign.
3. RPG theme alone proves nothing. Character growth, party/world state, branching narrative, or durable loadout development must be evidenced.
4. Mechanics analysis remains here. Everything else about an individual game remains in its canonical game directory.
5. Deck-building neither qualifies nor disqualifies a campaign game; persistence is the gate.
