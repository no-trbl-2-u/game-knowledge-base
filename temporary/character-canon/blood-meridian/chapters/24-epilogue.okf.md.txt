## Plot
- **Observed:** At dawn, an unnamed man advances across the plain, making a sequence of holes with a two-handled implement and striking fire from stone in each one. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Observed:** Bone seekers and other wanderers follow behind and cross the line of holes one by one. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Inference:** The action resembles installation of a surveyed boundary or fence line, but the epilogue does not explicitly name the work. [PDF pp. 243–244]

## Character Evidence
- **Observed:** The worker proceeds methodically, repeating a physical operation over visible ground. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Inference:** Unlike the judge's totalizing speech, this figure's authority is procedural and materially testable: a hole either exists in sequence or does not. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Observed:** The followers are differentiated only by whether they seek bones; their movement is described as mechanism-like and apparently reflective without confirmed inner reflection. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Inference:** They should not be treated as a unified moral constituency merely because they cross the same trace. [PDF pp. 243–244]

## Voice & Narrative Techniques
- **Observed:** The epilogue uses present tense, one paragraph, repeated sequence language, and no dialogue. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Observed:** Technical imagery of mechanism, verification, causality, and sequence coexists with fire attributed to God in the rock. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Inference:** The paragraph permits opposed readings—progress, enclosure, measurement, recurrence—without selecting one. [PDF pp. 243–244]

## SDLC Translation Candidates
- **Inference:** **Sequence verifier:** execute one observable step at a time, ensure each result has a traceable predecessor, and make the chain independently inspectable. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Inference:** **Infrastructure ambiguity reviewer:** document intended function, beneficiaries, exclusions, and downstream land or access effects before celebrating linear progress. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Inference:** **Mechanization skeptic:** do not infer prudence or judgment from orderly motion alone; verify feedback, agency, and internal state. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Inference:** **Evidence-preserving builder:** leave reproducible markers while distinguishing causal validation from mere adjacency in a sequence. [PDF pp. 243–244]

## Open Questions
- **Observed:** The worker, implement, holes, and intended completed structure are not named. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Observed:** The destination and relationship of the wanderers to the worker remain unspecified. [PDF pp. 243–244]
- **Inference:** Identifying the work definitively as fencing, telegraphy, or a singular historical project would exceed this chapter's evidence. [PDF pp. 243–244]
