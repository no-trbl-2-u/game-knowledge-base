## Plot

- The kid escapes the massacre on foot, finds the wounded Sproule, and travels with him across the desert (PDF pp. 44–45).
- They pass murdered infants and a destroyed village, discover mass killing inside its church, and continue along the attackers' route (pp. 45–48).
- Dehydrated, they see a mirage and encounter mounted Mexican bandits who provide only limited water while displaying Captain White's horse (pp. 48–51).
- A vampire bat wounds Sproule; the kid digs for seep water while Sproule's infected arm deteriorates (pp. 51–52).
- A family in a cart carries them to a town; Sproule dies overnight and soldiers arrest the kid (pp. 52–54).
- The kid identifies Captain White's preserved head, meets other survivors, and is transported under guard to Chihuahua City (pp. 54–57).
- In the prison, he recognizes Toadvine (p. 57).

## Character Evidence

- **The kid:** Uses stars, terrain, tracks, excavation, and coercive initiative to survive; despite harsh speech, he repeatedly waits for or travels with the failing Sproule (pp. 44–53).
- **Sproule:** Is wounded, consumptive, frightened, and increasingly passive; he alternates between asking the kid to leave and following him (pp. 44–53).
- **Mexican bandit leader:** Controls water and the captain's captured horse, mixing limited aid with intimidation and a warning about predators (pp. 49–51).
- **Captain White:** Appears only as a severed head and through a survivor's report, completing the collapse of his promised conquest (pp. 53–55).
- **The woman who feeds the prisoners:** Smuggles sweets and adds household meat to the captives' food despite the surrounding hostility (pp. 54–55).
- **Toadvine:** Reappears in the Chihuahua prison and tests whether the kid recognizes him (pp. 56–57).

## Voice & Narrative Techniques

- The survivor narrative narrows attention to water, shade, direction, wounds, and movement.
- Repeated false or ambiguous signs—seep, mirage, riders, crossroads—make interpretation a survival task.
- Religious spaces and objects are placed beside massacre without offering protection or explanation.
- Recurring animal scavengers register bodily vulnerability and indifferent continuation.
- White's preserved head acts as a material reversal of his expansive rhetoric in Chapter III.
- Small acts of aid are presented without converting the environment into a moral order.

## SDLC Translation Candidates

> The following are design inferences, not claims about software in the novel.

- **Inference — the kid:** **Operational behavior:** prioritize essentials, read weak signals, and improvise recovery under severe resource constraints. **Danger:** survival mode normalizes coercion and suppresses collaboration. **Guardrail:** define emergency authority, minimum humane constraints, and a post-incident review.
- **Inference — Sproule:** **Operational behavior:** report degradation and identify inability to continue. **Danger:** fatalism delays treatment and creates unclear handoff decisions. **Guardrail:** establish health checks, escalation thresholds, evacuation ownership, and explicit consent.
- **Inference — bandit leader:** **Operational behavior:** ration a scarce resource and communicate environmental risk. **Danger:** arbitrary control makes dependency exploitable. **Guardrail:** use transparent allocation rules and prohibit intimidation by resource owners.
- **Inference — woman aiding prisoners:** **Operational behavior:** preserve human welfare through a small exception inside a hostile system. **Danger:** covert aid is fragile and may expose the helper. **Guardrail:** create protected, auditable channels for humanitarian intervention.
