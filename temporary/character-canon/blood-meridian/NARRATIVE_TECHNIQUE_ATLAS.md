# Narrative Technique Atlas

These are analytical abstractions, not prose recipes. Never reproduce the novel's syntax, cadence, diction, imagery, or narrator stance.

| Technique | Observed basis | Abstract mechanism | Safe SDLC use | Misuse / anti-pastiche rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operational detail against grand claims | Ch. IV, PDF pp. 35–43; Ch. XVII, pp. 175–185 | test rhetoric against logistics and constraints | pair strategy with dependency and failure evidence | do not decorate reports with landscape scale |
| Warning before consequence | Ch. III, pp. 33–34; Ch. IV, pp. 40–43 | preserve a dissent signal before outcome | risk register with owner and trigger | no prophetic voice or ominous setup |
| Unstable witness | Ch. IX, pp. 88–90; Ch. X, pp. 93–101 | distinguish event, testimony, and interpretation | confidence grading and corroboration | do not manufacture mystery where logs resolve it |
| Artifact and provenance conflict | Ch. XI, pp. 103–105; Ch. XIII, p. 127 | representation can monopolize or erase originals | retain source, hash, custody, variants | never destroy originals after summarizing |
| Ceremony laundering harm | Ch. XII, pp. 120–122; Ch. XIII, pp. 122–126 | institutional approval can conceal invalid output | audit incentives, target validity, externalities | no baroque juxtaposition; state mismatch plainly |
| Silence as failed governance | Ch. VIII, pp. 80–82; Ch. XIII, pp. 127–128 | nonintervention is operationally consequential | named escalation owner and stop-work path | do not infer motive from silence without label |
| Material care versus abstraction | Ch. XVIII, pp. 185–188; Ch. XXI, pp. 217–218 | concrete aid can outperform doctrine | name need, assign tasks, restore safe minimum | avoid sentimentalizing affected people |
| Elliptical outcome | Ch. XXIII, pp. 240–243 | reactions imply an omitted result | report certainty as implied, not observed | never use omission when operational logs are available |
| Repeated sequence | Epilogue, pp. 243–244 | observable ordered steps create inspectable traces | reproducible build/deploy checkpoints | orderly motion is not proof of judgment |
| Scale shift | Ch. XVIII, pp. 185–188; Ch. XXII, pp. 225–228 | alternate system context and local body/state | connect aggregate metrics to individual impact | no cosmic or biblical ornament |

## Selection rule
Use a technique only when it improves evidence or decisions. If it merely makes prose feel literary, remove it.
