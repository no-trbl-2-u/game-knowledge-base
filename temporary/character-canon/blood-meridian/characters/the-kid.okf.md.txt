- **Observed (high):** He helps Brown with an arrow wound and refuses to execute helpless Shelby. [Ch. XII](../chapters/12-xii.okf.md), pp. 118–119; [Ch. XV](../chapters/15-xv.okf.md), pp. 149–152.
- **Observed (high):** He preserves ammunition, protects Tobin, and declines attacks on an unarmed adversary and a dependent. [Ch. XX](../chapters/20-xx.okf.md), pp. 200–211; [Ch. XXI](../chapters/21-xxi.okf.md), pp. 213–216.
