## Summary

The improvement opportunities below are interpretations of repeated structural signals in the independent reviews, not claims that the rules are defective for every group.

## Better-if opportunities

- Claim: Onboarding could be improved by separating the first exposure to factions, research, planetary reach, and endgame scoring before asking players to optimize their interaction.
  Source: src-003, src-004
  Evidence: Brettspielbox describes an expert game with a dense inherited system; H@LL9000 says the rules become easier for Terra Mystica players because the systems are familiar and newly interlinked.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Accessibility would improve if the game offered a concise aid that maps each research track and building upgrade to the resources and future actions it unlocks.
  Source: src-003, src-004
  Evidence: Both reviews explain the game through its many linked systems rather than a single central action; the need is an interpretation of that structure.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Strategic-depth presentation could be improved by making the changing endgame tiles and variable board consequences visible earlier in teaching materials.
  Source: src-003, src-004
  Evidence: The reviews emphasize variable board, faction, technology, and final-scoring combinations as central to replayability.
  Confidence: medium

- Claim: Turn pacing could be improved for new groups by giving players a compact planning reference for the repeated one-action cycle and pass-order consequences.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: H@LL9000 describes the game as a strategy/economic development title with a long 60–150 minute play window; the proposed aid is an interpretation, not a reported complaint.
  Confidence: low

## Open questions

The sources do not provide controlled playtime comparisons by player count, so no quantitative downtime claim is published.
