## Summary

Independent reception is strongly favorable but describes a demanding, rules-heavy strategy game whose appeal depends on accepting the long planning horizon and coupled systems.

## Praised design

The reviewers' praise centers on replayable variability: faction asymmetry, changing board layout, research choices, and changing endgame objectives.

## Player friction

The same interlocking structure that produces depth creates a steep onboarding burden. The packet treats “deep” and “complex” as reception descriptions, not as universal player facts.
