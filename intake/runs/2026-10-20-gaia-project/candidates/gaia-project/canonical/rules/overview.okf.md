## Summary

Gaia Project is a six-round expansion-and-development game. Each faction colonizes planets, builds an income engine, forms alliances, and advances six research areas; the player with the most points after final scoring wins.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Each player controls one of 14 factions that spreads peacefully through a galaxy.

  Confidence: high

- Claim: The game scores both during play and at the end, including research levels, alliances, two endgame tiles, and remaining resources.

## Rules / Mechanics

The core loop is spatial economic development rather than direct combat. Planet type, reach, and resource costs determine where expansion is possible; upgraded buildings expose income and abilities that make later actions more efficient.
