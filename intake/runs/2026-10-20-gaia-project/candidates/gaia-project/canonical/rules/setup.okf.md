## Summary

Setup assembles the galaxy from space sectors, prepares the research and scoring boards, gives each player a faction tableau and building supply, and places round boosters and technologies.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The board uses ten numbered space sectors, with some sectors double-sided for player-count setup.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 2 lists ten sectors and notes that sectors 05–07 are double-sided; pp. 19–20 give alternate layouts.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Players begin with faction-specific buildings, power stones, research markers, resources, and a starting booster.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 4–7 describes faction selection, starting resources, research markers, starting buildings, and booster selection.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The variable setup is structural, not cosmetic: sector arrangement, player count, faction selection, boosters, technology tiles, round scoring, and endgame tiles alter the opening incentives and available routes.

## Open questions

The packet summarizes setup procedure but does not reproduce the component inventory or every faction's starting exception.
