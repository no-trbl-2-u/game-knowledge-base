## Summary

The main actions are mine construction, Gaia projects, building upgrades, alliance formation, research advancement, power/Q.I.C. actions, special actions, and passing.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A mine requires an unoccupied reachable planet, habitability or terraforming, an available mine, and the printed resource costs.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 10–12 lists the mine prerequisites, reach, planet habitability, and cost payment.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Building upgrades exchange existing buildings for higher-tier buildings and may grant technologies and research advancement.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 13–14 describes Mine → Trading Station, Trading Station → Research Lab/Planetary Institute, and Research Lab → Academy upgrades.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Alliances require connected own buildings with sufficient power value and reward an alliance marker with points/resources and a later technology or level-five research opportunity.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 14–15 states the power-value, satellite, connection, and marker benefits.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Actions trade current resources and spatial position for future income, reach, research, or scoring leverage. Because the action set is shared and order-sensitive, occupying a planet or taking a technology can change the value of later choices.
