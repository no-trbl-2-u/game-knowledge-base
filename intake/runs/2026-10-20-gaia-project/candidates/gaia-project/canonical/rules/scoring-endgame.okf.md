## Summary

The game ends after all players pass in the action phase of round six. Final scoring combines endgame tiles, research levels, and leftover resources.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Six round-scoring tiles award points during the six rounds, while two endgame tiles are scored after round six.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 8 and 17–18 describe one round tile per round and two endgame tiles selected during setup.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Each achieved research level 3, 4, or 5 gives four endgame points, and sets of three credits, knowledge, or ore convert to one point.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 18 states the research and resource scoring conversions.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Endgame scoring rewards both broad development and the specific randomized objectives. The winner is the player with the highest total; ties are shared.

## Open questions

The packet does not enumerate all six possible endgame categories beyond the rulebook's summarized categories.
