## Praised design

- Claim: Reviewers praise Terra Mystica's deterministic planning, faction variety, and interconnected economic systems.
  Source: src-003, src-004
  Evidence: Board Game Quest calls it low-luck and high-strategy with 14 factions; SPACE-BIFF! praises the interlocking systems and fully deterministic victory parameters.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The proximity system creates a meaningful tradeoff between access to opponents' power and competition for land.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Board Game Quest says adjacent opponents provide power but make land more competitive and expensive to reach.
  Confidence: high

## Player friction

- Claim: The reviews report a steep onboarding burden and cognitive load.
  Source: src-003, src-004
  Evidence: Board Game Quest reports a steep learning curve and says a full game may be needed before players feel comfortable; SPACE-BIFF! says it takes a game or two for the interconnections to click.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: SPACE-BIFF! characterizes direct counterplay against an ahead player as blocking their expansion, not as a broad catch-up system.
  Source: src-004
  Evidence: The review says, “If someone is getting ahead, you build in such a way that it blocks them, and that's about it.”
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Board Game Quest reports lower player interaction and a cultist track that can feel disconnected.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Its Misses list includes “Lower amount of player interaction” and “Cultist track feels a little disconnected.”
  Confidence: high
