## Summary

Actions convert terrain, expand reach, improve conversion efficiency, upgrade buildings, advance cults, use power or special actions, and pass.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A dwelling may be built after a connected unbuilt field is converted to the player's home terrain, for one worker and two coins in the standard case.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 9 lists home terrain, an unbuilt field, connection to an own building, and the standard worker/coin cost.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Shipping and conversion-track actions extend reach or reduce the worker cost of spades, and each advance grants points.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed pages 10–11 describe advancing shipping for one priest and four coins and advancing conversion for two workers, five coins, and one priest, with printed point rewards.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Building upgrades exchange the old building back to the tableau and can produce favor tiles, faction powers, or new income.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed pages 11–12 describe dwelling-to-trading-house, trading-house-to-fortress/temple, and temple-to-sanctuary upgrades with their returned building and rewards.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Power actions are one-per-round spaces, while each special action can be used once per round.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed pages 13–14 say board power actions are taken first-come-first-served and that each special action is usable once per round.
  Confidence: high
