## Summary

Players develop one of 14 asymmetric peoples by converting terrain to their home terrain, placing buildings, upgrading those buildings, and pursuing cult influence.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Each people is tied to a home terrain and can build on that terrain after transformation.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 2 says each people is bound to one landscape type and can erect buildings only on its home landscape; terraforming exists to meet that constraint.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Building upgrades convert a dwelling into a trading house and then into a fortress, temple, or sanctuary, with each upgrade changing income or powers.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 2 describes the upgrade chain and states that buildings provide workers, money, power, or priests.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Four cult tracks provide additional power and rewards.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Printed page 2 names the earth, water, fire, and air cults and says progress is recorded on the cult board for additional power and other rewards.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The core loop is a deterministic conversion-and-development engine: resources fund terrain conversion and building upgrades; proximity creates both competition for land and optional power income.
