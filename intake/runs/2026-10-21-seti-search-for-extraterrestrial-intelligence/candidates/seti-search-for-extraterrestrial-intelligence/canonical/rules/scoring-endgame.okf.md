## Summary

The game ends after round five. Players score end-of-game cards, gold scoring tiles, and any alien-specific final scoring; the highest score wins, with tied scores remaining tied.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The game ends after five rounds and final scoring includes end-of-game cards, gold scoring tiles, and possible alien-specific scoring.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 21 lists the five-round end and those final-scoring categories.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Gold scoring tiles can reward sets of technologies, missions, income cards, life traces, or combinations of sector wins with orbiters or landers.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 21–22 describes the technology, mission, income, other, and sector/figure scoring sides of the tiles.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The solo game uses a rival research institution, five difficulty levels, rival action cards, and a progress track.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 22–26 describes the rival board, action deck, difficulty stars, progress, objectives, and end condition.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Scoring rewards breadth across the same systems used during play: technologies, missions, income, traces, and exploration footprints. The endgame therefore reinforces the game's conversion engine rather than replacing it with a separate final puzzle.

## Open questions

The packet does not reproduce every gold-tile side or solo objective tile.
