## Summary

Main actions cover probe launch, orbit, landing, star scans, data analysis, card play, technology research, and passing. Credits, energy, publicity, cards, data, and technologies are deliberately scarce.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Launching a probe costs 2 credits and places a probe at Earth, subject to the normal one-probe-in-space limit.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 8 states the launch cost, starting space, and default probe limit.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Orbiting costs 1 credit and 1 energy, while landing costs 3 energy or 2 energy when an orbiter is already at the planet.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 10–11 gives the orbit and land costs and the orbiter discount.

- Claim: Scanning costs 1 credit and 2 energy and marks a signal in Earth's sector plus a card-matched sector, with telescope technologies adding options.

  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Cards can be played for a main effect or discarded for a free-action benefit, while tucked cards become recurring income. That makes hand management a conversion problem: the same card may be an action, a movement/resource effect, or future income.
