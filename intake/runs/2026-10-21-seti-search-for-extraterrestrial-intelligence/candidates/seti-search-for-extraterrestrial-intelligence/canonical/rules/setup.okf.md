## Summary

Setup randomizes the solar system, selects two hidden alien species, prepares a card row and four end-of-round stacks, and gives each player a starting income card and resources.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The solar-system board begins in a random configuration, with three discs and four surrounding sector boards forming eight sectors.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 4 says the three discs and four sector boards start in a random configuration and must line up into eight sectors.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Two of five alien species are placed face down during setup, with their cards and other components kept out of play until discovery.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 4 instructs players to mix five species, place two face down, and leave the remaining alien components in the box.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Each player starts with 4 publicity, 4 credits, 3 energy, and 5 cards, then tucks one card as income.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 6 lists the starting resources and the initial income-card step.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Variable setup is load-bearing: disc alignment changes movement, the hidden species alter later rules, and random end-of-round cards change the value of passing. The packet records the bounded setup procedure without claiming every possible random layout.

## Open questions

The official page also advertises a randomizer companion app; this packet does not treat the app as required for setup.
