## Summary

The game lasts five rounds. Players take turns clockwise, taking one main action plus any available free actions; a player who passes is finished for that round while others continue.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A turn consists of one main action, with free actions available before, after, or during that main action.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 7 says “On your turn, you take only 1 main action” and explains that free actions can occur at any time during the turn.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: After all players pass, players collect income, the starting-player marker moves, and the next round begins.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 7 and 19–20 describe passing, income, starting-player rotation, and between-round steps.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The pass timing creates a shared tempo decision: passing locks a player out of further main actions but provides earlier access to the end-of-round card stack. The first pass also rotates the solar system.
