## Summary

The two independent reviews are strongly favorable but both describe a complex, choice-rich eurogame. They praise the theme, variety, and interlocking routes while flagging the possibility of analysis paralysis or a heavy first teach.

## Praised design

Reception centers on thematic integration, multiple routes to points, card variety, and the physical rotation of the solar system. These are reviewer judgments, not universal player outcomes.

## Player friction

Choice density and the number of interacting systems can make the opening teach and turn planning demanding. The reviews support this as reception testimony; they do not establish a measured downtime average.
