# Better-if opportunities

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The official rules use a visible action limit, a shared object economy, variable monster selection, and a monster deck that can trigger repeated movement/attacks through the Frenzied Monster symbol.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official instructions printed pp. 4–8 and p. 10.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Independent testimony praises the game’s approachable core loop while describing the monster-specific puzzles and terror pressure as the main source of challenge.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Lee Leonardo review overview, items, and final assessment.
  Confidence: medium

## Interpretations

- Claim: Onboarding could improve by separating the common turn skeleton from the monster-specific exceptions in a single quick-reference sequence.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The official rules split common phases across pp. 5–9 and the reviewer notes that later pages contain component pictures and monster-specific setup/reference.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Randomness is a productive but visible pressure point: the item bag and monster deck create uncertainty, while each selected monster supplies a bounded objective puzzle.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official instructions pp. 4, 7–10 and monster procedures pp. 10–14.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Solo/co-op automation and pacing are improvement surfaces because the solo appendix changes terror and removes specific cards rather than supplying a separate automated opponent.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official instructions p. 15.
  Confidence: medium
