# Rules overview

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Horrified is cooperative: all players win by defeating every selected monster, and all players lose together when terror reaches its maximum or the monster deck is exhausted when a draw is required.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official instructions printed pp. 5 and 9; “«Horrified» es un juego cooperativo” and the three ending conditions.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Difficulty is adjusted by selecting different numbers of monsters, with a first-game recommendation and standard/challenging bands.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official instructions printed p. 5, “Dificultad.”
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The core loop alternates a Hero Phase and Monster Phase, with the next player beginning after both phases resolve.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official instructions printed p. 5, “En tu turno.”
  Confidence: high
