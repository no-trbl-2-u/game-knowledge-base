# Victory, defeat, and endgame

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The game ends immediately in a hero victory when every selected monster is defeated.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official instructions printed p. 9, “Los héroes triunfan.”
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The game ends in a shared loss if terror reaches the skull maximum. Each defeated hero or villager increases terror by one.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official instructions printed pp. 5 and 9, “Terror.”
  Confidence: high
- Claim: If a monster card must be drawn but the deck is empty, the players lose; after the last available card resolves, one final Hero Phase is allowed.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Official instructions printed p. 9, “Se acabó el tiempo.”
  Confidence: high
