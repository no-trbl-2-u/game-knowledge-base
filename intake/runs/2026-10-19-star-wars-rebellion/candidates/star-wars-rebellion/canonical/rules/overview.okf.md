## Summary

Star Wars: Rebellion models a hidden-base insurgency against an expanding galactic empire. The factions share the same map but pursue different information, production, and victory pressures.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The Empire wins by finding and destroying the secret Rebel base, while the Rebels win by advancing reputation until the round tracker meets it.
  Source: src-002, src-005
  Evidence: The official Learn to Play rules define the hidden base and reputation objectives; Ars Technica summarizes the same two-sided victory race.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The game uses leader pools, mission cards, system activation, unit movement, combat, production, and probe-card searches.
  Source: src-002, src-005
  Evidence: The official rules describe the round sequence and the independent review describes leaders, missions, movement, refresh, production, and probe cards.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The central loop alternates leader assignment and action resolution. Unassigned leaders activate systems and move forces; assigned leaders resolve missions. Battles use unit dice, leader tactics, and combat cards, while refresh steps return leaders, draw cards, build units, and narrow or advance the victory race.
