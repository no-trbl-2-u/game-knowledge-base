# Source registry

## Retrieval notes

The official core rulebook was retrieved directly from the Fantasy Flight CDN. Publisher product pages returned HTTP 403 during bounded inspection and are not used as evidence. Both independent reviews were retrieved HTTP 200 as full article text and their prose was read directly; every reception claim quotes retrieved wording rather than page metadata. No unsupported FAQ-dependent claim is published.

Two sources were withdrawn during repair and are deliberately absent:

- A Geek and Sundry TableTop YouTube episode. Its watch page returned HTTP 429/CAPTCHA under independent audit, so only video identity — never the claimed gameplay or reception content — could be confirmed. Video-timestamp claims are not published on identity evidence alone.
- A Boards of Madness image asset. The hosting site asserts "All Rights Reserved" and no reuse basis was established, so it cannot underwrite a canonical visual. Both retained visuals now derive from the official rulebook instead.

Independent reception therefore rests on two written reviews from distinct non-BGG organizational domains (`rpg.net`, `opinionatedgamers.com`), separate from the official `fantasyflightgames.com` rules authority.
