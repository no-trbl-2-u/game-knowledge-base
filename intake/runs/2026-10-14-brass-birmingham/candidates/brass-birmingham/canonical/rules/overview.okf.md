## Overview findings

- Claim: The game runs through a Canal Era from 1770–1830 and a Rail Era from 1830–1870.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The rulebook's goal section names the two eras and gives those year ranges.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Players expand by establishing canals and rails and by building or developing cotton mills, coal mines, iron works, manufacturers, potteries, and breweries.
  Source: src-001, src-002
  Evidence: Roxley lists the game as network-building and economic; the rulebook introduction lists the transport and industry types.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The winner is the player with the most Victory Points after Rail Era scoring.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The rulebook goal and winning sections state that VPs are scored at the end of each era and the highest total after the Rail Era wins.
  Confidence: high
