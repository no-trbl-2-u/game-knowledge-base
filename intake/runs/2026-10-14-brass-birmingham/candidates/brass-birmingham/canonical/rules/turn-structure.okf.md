## Turn structure

- Claim: On a normal turn, a player performs two actions and may choose the same action twice.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The rulebook states, “On your turn, perform a total of 2 actions,” and the actions list permits the same action twice.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: During the first round of the Canal Era, each player performs only one action.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The player-turn exception says the first Canal Era round is limited to one action per player.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Each action requires discarding a card, with Wild cards returned to their Card Draw Areas rather than placed in a discard pile.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The player-turn rules require one discard per action and give the Wild-card exception.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Players spend money on their Character tiles, and the next round's turn order runs from least money spent to most money spent, preserving relative order on ties.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The spending-money and end-of-round procedures use money on Character tiles to rearrange the Turn Order Track from least to most spent.
  Confidence: high
