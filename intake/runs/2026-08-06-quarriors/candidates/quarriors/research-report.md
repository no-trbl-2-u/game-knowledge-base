# Quarriors! reconnaissance

Selected from the first unchecked physical tabletop wishlist entry. Scope is the released WizKids Quarriors! base game (product 70939; BGG 91536), not expansions, Dice Masters, or Quultimate Quedition.

The official WizKids product page was retrieved HTTP 200 and identifies the 2011 release, 2–4 players, 30–60 minutes, ages 14+, and dice-building mechanism. It links the English rulebook. The current linked PDF endpoint returned HTTP 500, so the exact official URL was retrieved through its 2013 Internet Archive capture (HTTP 200, 12 pages, SHA-256 in evidence.json). The packet publishes no FAQ/errata-dependent claim.

Independent reception was retrieved in full from Tabletop Bellhop and Meeple Mountain. Four meaningful visuals derive from the official rulebook and official WizKids product media; derivatives and hashes are recorded in the visual packet.
