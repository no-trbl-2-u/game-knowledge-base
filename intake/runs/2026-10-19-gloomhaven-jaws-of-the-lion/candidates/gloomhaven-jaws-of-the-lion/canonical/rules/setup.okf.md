## Summary

Setup is scenario-specific and is staged by the scenario book, with player materials, map pages, monsters, tokens, and tutorial rules added progressively.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Each player sets out a character mat, reference card, six starting ability cards, attack-modifier deck, hit-point dial, and initiative-order token for the first scenario.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF pp. 3-4 list the character materials and scenario-one play-area setup.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Scenario-one monster setup uses a stat card in a six-section envelope, randomized standees, and a shared monster attack-modifier deck.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF pp. 4-5 describe the Vermling Raider stat envelope, standees, and monster modifier deck.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Later tutorial scenarios add money, treasure, traps, doors, item purchasing, battle goals, the element board, and full monster ability decks.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF pp. 14, 19, and 25 introduce these setup changes in sequence.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The scenario book is both map and setup authority for the tutorial. Doors reveal rooms, and later scenarios introduce a supplemental scenario book and additional boards.

## Player friction

Setup grows as the tutorial advances; the packet does not claim a complete component count or every scenario's setup recipe.
