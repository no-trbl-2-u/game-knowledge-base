## Summary

Each scenario round is driven by simultaneous card selection, initiative ordering, character and monster turns, and end-of-round maintenance.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Players privately and simultaneously choose two ability cards, using one card's initiative value to order the round.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF pp. 6-7 describe face-down two-card selection, limited communication, initiative values, and low-to-high ordering.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A character turn performs one top action and one bottom action from the two selected cards, in either order.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF pp. 6-7 state that a character performs the top of one card and bottom of the other, with no two-top or two-bottom turn.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A round ends after every figure acts; attack-modifier decks with shuffle symbols are shuffled then, and cards can be recovered by resting.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF p. 12 describes end-of-round shuffle timing and short-rest card recovery.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Initiative is a commitment device: players coordinate without exposing exact card values, then resolve the revealed order against monster behavior.

## Player friction

The review independently describes the hidden initiative discussion as a source of miscommunication and ruined plans, while the rules source establishes the underlying timing.
