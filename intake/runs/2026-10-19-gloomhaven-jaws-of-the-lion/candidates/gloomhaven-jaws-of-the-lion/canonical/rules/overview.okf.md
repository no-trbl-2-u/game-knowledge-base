## Summary

The Learn to Play Guide teaches a cooperative tactical campaign through a five-scenario tutorial.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Players work together through a campaign of scenarios and advance after successfully completing scenarios.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF p. 1 says the game is fully cooperative, can be saved between scenarios, and teaches the first five scenarios step by step.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The game begins with four available mercenary characters and supports up to four players; solo play controls two characters.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF p. 3 lists four characters and says one player controls two characters when playing alone.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A scenario ends successfully when its goal is completed and is lost when all characters become exhausted.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: PDF pp. 12-13 describe exhaustion and the success/failure end of the scenario.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The tutorial introduces characters, scenario-book maps, monster stat envelopes, ability cards, attack-modifier decks, conditions, loot, and campaign persistence in stages.
