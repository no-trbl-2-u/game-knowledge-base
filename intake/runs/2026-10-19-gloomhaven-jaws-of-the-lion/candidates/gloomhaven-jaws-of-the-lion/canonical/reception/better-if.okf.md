## Summary

The available evidence points to improvement opportunities around onboarding, setup, cognitive load, failure recovery, and campaign pacing. These are interpretations grounded in one review plus the publisher's tutorial framing, not final mechanics verdicts.

## Better-if opportunities

- Claim: Keep the staged tutorial and make its information ramp explicit in any derivative teaching aid.
  Source: src-001, src-003
  Evidence: Cephalofair describes a five-scenario step-by-step tutorial; Board Game Quest praises the tutorial for easing players into the system.
  Confidence: high
  Label: onboarding

- Claim: Preserve the scenario-book setup shortcut because setup reduction is a concrete part of the game's appeal.
  Source: src-001, src-003
  Evidence: The publisher says the scenario books make setup easier; the review calls the faster setup a strength.
  Confidence: high
  Label: setup-teardown

- Claim: Reduce the penalty of a late failed scenario or provide clearer recovery scaffolding for players who lose after a long session.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The reviewer says a 90-minute loss followed by a required retry is not usually enjoyable.
  Confidence: medium
  Label: turn-pacing

- Claim: Surface the tactical cost of reserving powerful cards so players can understand the hand-exhaustion trade before committing.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The guide makes card loss and exhaustion core rules; the review says players must save their best abilities and that this creates mental overhead.
  Confidence: medium
  Label: strategic-depth

- Claim: Keep character asymmetry legible while reducing the cognitive burden of tracking hand, discard, lost, active, and item states.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The review praises interesting characters but notes that each player has many simultaneous state areas to track.
  Confidence: medium
  Label: combat-resolution

## Unknowns

- No population-level evidence establishes which friction is most common.
- No comparative playtest evidence supports a particular redesign.
