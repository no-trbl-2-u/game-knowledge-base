# Setup

## Solo setup

- Claim: Solo setup shuffles only the 76 basic-game cards, identified as cards without expansion symbols.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 6, Rules for 1 Player — Set up, defines the deck and separately labels expansion symbols.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The opening hand is five Location cards; any Door or Dream drawn during opening setup goes to Limbo and is replaced until five Locations are held, after which Limbo is reshuffled into the deck.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 6 gives the complete opening-hand replacement sequence and example.
  Confidence: high

## Two-player setup

- Claim: Two-player setup reveals eight Location cards, then alternates drafting until each player has three Personal resources and two face-up Shared resources remain.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 10, Rules for 2 Players — Set up, describes replacement of Door or Dream cards and the draft.
  Confidence: high
