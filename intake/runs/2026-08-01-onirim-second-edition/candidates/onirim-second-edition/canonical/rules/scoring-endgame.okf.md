# Scoring and endgame

## Solo

- Claim: Solo victory occurs immediately whenever all eight Door cards are simultaneously in play, without refilling the hand.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 10, End of the game — Victory, states the timing and hand exception.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Solo defeat occurs when a card must be drawn to fill the hand but the deck is empty.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 10, End of the game — Defeat, states the exact loss trigger.
  Confidence: high

## Two players

- Claim: Cooperative victory requires each player to hold four Doors, one of every color, at the same time.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 12, End of the game — Victory, defines the shared condition.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: A player who obtains a fourth Door continues taking turns until the partner also satisfies the common victory condition.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 12 explicitly says the first completed player keeps playing.
  Confidence: high
