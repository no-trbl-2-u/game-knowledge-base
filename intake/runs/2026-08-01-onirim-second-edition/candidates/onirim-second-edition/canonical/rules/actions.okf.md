# Actions

## Play a Location

- Claim: A played Location may not share its Sun, Moon, or Key symbol with the previously played Location.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 7 calls this the golden rule and names the three symbols.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Playing a third consecutive Location of one color lets the player search the deck for a Door of that color, put it into play, and shuffle the deck.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 7, Discovering a Door card, gives the trigger and resolution.
  Confidence: high

## Discard and Prophecy

- Claim: Discarding a Key triggers Prophecy: inspect the top five deck cards, discard exactly one, and return the other four in any order.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 8, Prophecy, defines the mandatory discard and reorder.
  Confidence: high

## Resolve draws

- Claim: A drawn Door may be claimed by discarding a same-color Key; otherwise that Door enters Limbo.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 8, Fill your hand, describes both Door outcomes.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: A drawn Nightmare forces one of four penalties: discard a Key, return an acquired Door to Limbo, discard the top five non-Door/non-Dream cards, or discard and rebuild the whole hand.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Official rules printed page 9, Resolve a Dream card, enumerates the four options and protected cards.
  Confidence: high
