# Independent reception

## Praised design

- Claim: The reviewer describes Dominion as a foundational deck-builder whose short playtime supports trying different strategies without a long failed-game penalty.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Board Game Quest’s review dated Feb 26, 2013 calls Dominion the “grand daddy” of deck builders, describes roughly 30-minute play, and says the short games make experimenting easier.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The reviewer praises the game’s strategic variety, quick turns, accessibility, and the uncertainty created because Victory Point cards remain in players’ decks until scoring.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Final Thoughts says players can pursue different strategies, turns are quick, the game is accessible, and players do not know who is ahead until the end.
  Confidence: high

## Player friction

- Claim: The review identifies lower player interaction and a tacked-on theme as minor flaws rather than rejecting the core play loop.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The Final Thoughts section lists “tacked on theme” and “lower player interaction” among minor flaws while rating the overall experience 4.5 stars.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The reviewer presents the game as accessible to non-gamers but not as a low-complexity filler for every group.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The review pairs accessibility and easy-to-understand play with discussion of strategic choices and a roughly 30-minute session.
  Confidence: medium
