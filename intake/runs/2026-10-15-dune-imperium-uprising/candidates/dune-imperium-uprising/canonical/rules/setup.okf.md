## Rules / Mechanics

Players choose a Leader, take the shared starting deck, prepare the conflict clock, then place Agents, faction cubes, troops, and objectives. One or two player modes use the separate supplement; this packet does not restate that supplement.

## Player friction

The setup has several card stacks and player-specific pieces plus an objective distribution step; the rulebook itself separates special player-count setup into supplements.
