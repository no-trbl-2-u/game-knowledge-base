## Summary

Combat converts deployed units and revealed swords into a ranked reward contest, with sandworms changing the value of rewards.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Each troop in the Conflict is worth 2 strength, each sandworm is worth 3 strength, and each revealed sword is worth 1 strength.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 12–13 define the strength total.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The highest strength takes the first reward, second-highest takes the second reward, and a player with zero strength receives no reward.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 14 states the ranking and zero-strength rule.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: If a player has a sandworm in the Conflict, most rewards are doubled, subject to the listed exceptions.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 14 describes Sandworm Reward Doubling.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The winner may gain a Victory Point from matching battle icons on face-up Conflict or Objective cards.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 14 describes the three battle icons and matching pairs.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Reveal effects provide Persuasion for acquiring cards and swords for Combat. After Combat, Makers add spice to empty Maker spaces, then Recall returns Agents unless the endgame has triggered.

## Player friction

Combat invites late commitment and hidden Intrigue-card surprises, while sandworm rewards make the decision to invest in Maker Hooks materially different from ordinary troop strength.
