## Summary

The standalone rules contain several restrictions that prevent the new combat tools from being unconditional.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A sandworm cannot be summoned to a Conflict protected by the Shield Wall.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 10 states that the Shield Wall protects the three critical locations from sandworms.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Sandworm summoning normally requires a Maker Hooks token on the player's garrison.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 10 and 17 identify the Maker Hooks requirement.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Spy Infiltrate and Gather Intelligence are separate choices, and the same Spy cannot be recalled for both in one turn.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 11 states the two effects and the restriction.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: In the CHOAM module, contracts are taken from face-up contracts and completed by specified future actions.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 16–17 define taking and completing contracts.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The rulebook's clarifications treat timing and prerequisites as first-class rules. The packet therefore keeps the Shield Wall, Maker Hooks, Spy recall, and CHOAM contract boundaries explicit rather than collapsing them into a general “special powers” summary.

## Player friction

The restrictions are meaningful counterweights, but they are also the likely places where first-play questions accumulate.
