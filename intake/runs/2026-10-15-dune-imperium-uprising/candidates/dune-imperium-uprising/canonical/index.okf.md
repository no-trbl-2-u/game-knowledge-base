## Summary

Dune: Imperium — Uprising is a 2023 standalone deck-building worker-placement game from Dire Wolf. It retains the series' card-gated agent placement and conflict race while adding spies, sandworms, a Shield Wall, and a six-player team mode.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Uprising is a standalone tabletop game from Dire Wolf and builds on the Dune: Imperium deck-building/worker-placement formula.
  Source: src-001, src-003
  Evidence: The publisher describes Uprising as standalone and built on the blend; Dune News Net independently calls it a new standalone tabletop game.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The game uses a 10-VP race and ends after a round when a player reaches 10 or more Victory Points or the Conflict Deck is empty.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 6 and 15 state the objective and the endgame trigger.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Cards serve two linked jobs: Agent icons unlock board spaces, while Reveal boxes generate persuasion and other effects. The round alternates Agent and Reveal decisions, then resolves a public Conflict with hidden Intrigue-card uncertainty.

## Player friction

The system layers spies, sandworm prerequisites, Shield Wall timing, contracts, faction influence, and combat rewards onto the base card/worker-placement grammar. The packet does not claim an exhaustive component or card inventory.

## Praised design

Independent reviewers praise spies as a solution to blocked spaces and describe the standalone ruleset as a polished entry point, especially for solo and two-player play.
