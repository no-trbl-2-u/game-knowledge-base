## Summary

Twilight Struggle is a two-player card-driven Cold War game in which US and USSR influence competes across connected countries and regions. This packet is scoped to the base game represented by GMT's 2nd Edition Deluxe rulebook; expansions and digital implementations are excluded.

## Rules / Mechanics

The packet's central mechanism is card-driven influence competition: cards can be used as events or operations, while operations can place influence, trigger realignment rolls, attempt coups, or advance the Space Race. Regional scoring converts the map position into victory points, and DEFCON constrains the most aggressive actions.
