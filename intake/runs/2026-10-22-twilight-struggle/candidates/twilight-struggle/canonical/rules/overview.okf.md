## Summary

Twilight Struggle models a two-player Cold War contest through influence, cards, regional scoring, DEFCON pressure, and a ten-turn structure.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The game is a two-player simulation of the forty-five-year contest between the US and USSR.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 2, section 1.0, describes the game as a two-player forty-five-year contest between the two superpowers.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The map is divided into six regions and connected country spaces, with control determined by influence and stability.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 2–3, sections 2.1.1–2.1.7, describe the regions, connections, country spaces, stability numbers, and control.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The game is not a generic territory race: the map, card deck, military track, DEFCON track, Space Race, and victory-point track continually change the value and danger of a placement decision.
