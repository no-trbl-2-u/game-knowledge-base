## Summary

The core turn has a DEFCON improvement and deal, simultaneous hidden Headline cards, alternating Action Rounds, a military-operations check, end-of-turn cleanup, and turn advancement.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The game is organized into ten turns with Headline and Action Round phases.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 4–5, sections 4.0–4.1, lists ten turns and the Headline, Action Round, military check, cleanup, and final-scoring sequence.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Players secretly select Headline cards and then alternate Action Rounds while the other player responds.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 4 describes simultaneous secret Headline selection; p. 5 continues with alternating Action Rounds.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The cadence forces players to evaluate both the immediate action and the timing of a card's event, because a scoring card or DEFCON change can matter before the next alternating round.
