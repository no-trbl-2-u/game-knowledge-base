## Summary

Victory comes primarily from regional influence scoring, card events, military-operation penalties, and final scoring after turn ten; the VP track also permits an automatic victory at its endpoints.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Regional scoring evaluates presence, domination, and control through influence in the six regions.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 10, sections 10.0–10.1, defines regional scoring and the regional influence terms.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Final scoring occurs after turn ten and the VP track has automatic-victory endpoints.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 4–5 lists final scoring after turn 10; p. 10 describes the US-20 and USSR-20 automatic-victory range.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

Scoring is a timing problem as much as a counting problem: the same card can be weak early, decisive before a region scores, or dangerous when the opponent's influence makes the scoring card reciprocal.
