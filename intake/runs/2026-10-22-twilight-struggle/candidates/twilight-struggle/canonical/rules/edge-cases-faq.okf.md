## Summary

Several restrictions are load-bearing: DEFCON limits coup and realignment access by region, scoring cards must be played in the turn drawn, and the Space Race can absorb a card without resolving its event.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: DEFCON 4 or lower restricts coup and realignment attempts in progressively more regions, and DEFCON 1 ends the game.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 9, section 8.1.5, lists the regional restrictions and the DEFCON 1 game-over result.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Scoring cards must be played during the turn in which they are drawn, and they cannot be held by an event effect.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 3–5, sections 2.2.1 and 4.1, identify scoring-card timing and the hold restriction.
  Confidence: high
