## Summary

Cards are played as events or operations. Operations support influence placement, realignment rolls, coup attempts, and Space Race attempts; events may alter the board, tracks, or later card play.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A card can be played as an Event or for its Operations points.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 5, section 5.1, states that cards may be played in one of two ways: as Events or Operations.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Operations points must be committed to one of four operation types when a card is played for operations.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 6, section 6.0, lists marker placement, realignment rolls, coup attempts, and Space Race attempts.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A coup in a battleground country degrades DEFCON by one level.
  Source: src-001
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 9, section 8.1.4, states the battleground coup DEFCON consequence.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The event-versus-operations choice is the principal pressure valve: an apparently harmful opponent event may still be the best source of operations, while the Space Race offers a way to discard some dangerous cards.
