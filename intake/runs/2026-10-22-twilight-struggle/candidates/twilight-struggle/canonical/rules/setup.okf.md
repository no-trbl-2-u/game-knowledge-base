## Summary

Setup gives the USSR and US asymmetric starting influence, places both Space Race markers, establishes the DEFCON and military-operation tracks, and deals the opening Early War hands.

- Claim: Each player begins with a different hand size and starting influence deployment.

  Confidence: high

  Source: src-001
