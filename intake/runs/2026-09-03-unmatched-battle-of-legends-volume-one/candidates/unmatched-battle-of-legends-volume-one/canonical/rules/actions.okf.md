## Action findings

- Claim: A maneuver may move each fighter up to the move value, one fighter at a time, through adjacent spaces.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The movement procedure uses the character card’s move value and requires each entered space to be adjacent to the previous space.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A scheme plays a scheme card for its effect and requires the player to declare the active fighter.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The scheme procedure says to choose a scheme card, play it face up, declare which fighter plays it, resolve its effect, and discard it.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A player cannot attack without an attack card available to a fighter and a valid target.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The attack procedure states both conditions before an attack action can be taken.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A ranged fighter can target an adjacent fighter or a fighter anywhere in the same zone, while a melee fighter targets an adjacent fighter.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The declare-target rules distinguish the melee adjacent-space condition from the ranged same-zone option.
  Confidence: high
