## Better-if opportunities

- Claim: Team play could be better if its spatial load were made easier to manage as additional fighters enter the same battlefield.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The independent review reports that four-player play tends to bog down as fighters muddy into and around each other.
  Confidence: medium
  Better-if: downtime, player-interaction

- Claim: Onboarding could preserve the under-five-minute teach while making each fighter’s distinct tactical identity explicit before the first duel.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The reviewer says the system takes fewer than five minutes to teach, while also treating the character roster and rules-bendy asymmetry as its strongest feature.
  Confidence: medium
  Better-if: onboarding, strategic-depth

- Claim: Combat presentation should protect the attacker/defender bluff and hand-pressure decisions that the reviewer identifies as the intelligent part of attacks.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The review describes attacks as a mix of brute force and light bluffing, with players deciding when to invest cards and when to hold them.
  Confidence: medium
  Better-if: combat-resolution, strategic-depth

## Boundary

These are design opportunities inferred from independent reception and the official combat structure. They are not proposed implementation specifications or mechanics verdicts.
