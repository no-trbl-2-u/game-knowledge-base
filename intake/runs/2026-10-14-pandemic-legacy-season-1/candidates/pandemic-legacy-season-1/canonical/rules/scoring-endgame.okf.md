## Scoring and endgame

- Claim: A game ends immediately when the group meets the required number of objectives.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 13 says the team wins as soon as it meets the necessary number of objectives, even during a turn or outbreak sequence.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The team loses when the Outbreaks marker reaches its last space, when required disease cubes cannot be placed, or when a player cannot draw two Player cards after actions.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 13 lists those three loss conditions.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: After every game, win or lose, the group chooses two upgrades and clears the board and City cards for the next game; Legacy cards are not returned to the deck.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 14 states the two-upgrade choice and the reset instructions.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A first-time monthly loss is replayed, while a second loss in that month advances the campaign to the next month; funding increases after a loss and decreases after a win.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 14 gives the win/loss month transition and funding changes.
  Confidence: high
