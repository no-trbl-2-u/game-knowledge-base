## Setup findings

- Claim: When starting a month, players first draw Legacy Deck cards until a STOP instruction, read the mission briefing, and integrate any instructed additions.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 6, setup step 1, directs players to draw until STOP and integrate new cards when playing a month for the first time.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The setup includes the board, nearby structures, disease supplies, research stations, outbreak and cure markers, objectives, funded events, and dealt Player cards.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 6–7 list those setup groups in order and state that later games may add other pieces.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The first player is selected blindly from the pawns and play then proceeds clockwise.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 7 says, “Take all the pawns and blindly select one. That player goes first, and play proceeds clockwise.”
  Confidence: high
