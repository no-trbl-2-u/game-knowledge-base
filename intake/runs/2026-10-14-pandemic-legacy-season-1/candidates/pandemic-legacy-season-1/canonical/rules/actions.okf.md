## Action findings

- Claim: The movement actions are Drive/Ferry, Direct Flight, Charter Flight, and Shuttle Flight, each with the location and card restrictions defined by the board state.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 8–9 lists the four movement actions and their discard, adjacency, and research-station requirements.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Treat Disease removes one disease cube from the active character's city, while Discover a Cure requires five same-color City cards at a research station.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 8–9 defines Treat Disease and the five-card Discover a Cure action.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Share Knowledge permits an agreed City-card transfer between players in the matching city, with a Researcher exception for taking any City card in the same city.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook p. 8 defines both directions of Share Knowledge and the Researcher exception.
  Confidence: high
