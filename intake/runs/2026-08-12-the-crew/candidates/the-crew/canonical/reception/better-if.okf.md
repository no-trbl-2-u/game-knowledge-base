# Better-if opportunities

## Source-backed improvement inferences

- Claim: The Crew would be easier to onboard if the first-session guide made the handoff from ordinary trick-taking to mission-task reasoning more explicit.
  Source: src-002, src-004
  Evidence: The manual teaches the framework across several sections, while The Family Gamers says the early missions do onboarding work; this supports a design opportunity, not a claim that the rules are defective.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Any communication aid should preserve the game's pressure from scarce information rather than turn the token into unrestricted table talk.
  Source: src-002, src-003
  Evidence: The manual limits normal communication to one token per player per mission, and Board Game Quest identifies the constrained communication as part of the game's tension.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Mission progression benefits from visibly signaling difficulty and completion without exposing the hidden decision problem.
  Source: src-002, src-004
  Evidence: The manual organizes 50 missions and includes a difficulty section; The Family Gamers describes increasing complexity across scenarios.
  Confidence: medium
- Claim: Accessibility aids should preserve the color-symbol correspondence already present in the official rules.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: The manual supplies symbols for color distinction, so an improvement should extend that aid rather than replace it with an unverified convention.
  Confidence: high
