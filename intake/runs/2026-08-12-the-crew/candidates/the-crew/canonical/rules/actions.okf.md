# Card play and communication

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Players must follow the led color when they can, while a rocket may be played when the player lacks the led color.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Manual printed pages 3–4 states the follow-suit restriction and the condition for playing a different suit, including rockets.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Rockets are the trump suit and can win a trick when legally played.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Manual printed Game Overview explains the five suits and states that rockets are trump.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Each player normally has one radio communication token per mission and may reveal a color card as highest, lowest, or only card of that color.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Manual printed page 5, Communication, describes the token, the revealed card, and its three positions.
  Confidence: high
