# Turn structure

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A trick consists of one face-up card played by each player in turn order.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Manual printed page 3 defines the trick as one card from each crew member played in turn order.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The first card played sets the suit that must be followed when possible.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Manual printed pages 3–4 explain that following suit means playing the same card type as the first card.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: The winner of the trick leads the next trick, and the mission ends when its tasks are completed or a task is failed.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Manual printed pages 4 and 8–9 describe trick-winning succession and the mission success/failure sequence.
  Confidence: high
