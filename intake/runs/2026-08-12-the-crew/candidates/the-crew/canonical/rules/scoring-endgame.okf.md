# Mission completion and campaign progression

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The base game uses mission objectives rather than a normal competitive score track.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Manual printed page 3 describes unique mission win conditions, and pages 8–12 describe completing or failing those conditions.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: A successful mission is recorded by the players before moving to the next mission in the logbook.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Manual mission-sequence text instructs players to record the number of tries and continue through the logbook.
  Confidence: high
- Claim: Distress signals can modify a mission when the crew needs help, at the cost described by the mission framework.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Manual contents and printed pages 12–13 include Degree of Difficulty & Distress Signals as a bounded rules section.
  Confidence: medium
