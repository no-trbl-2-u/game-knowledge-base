## Scoring and endgame

- Claim: The first player to reach 10 victory points wins immediately.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 12 states that the game immediately ends when one player has gained 10 victory points.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Players primarily gain victory points by scoring public and secret objectives during the status phase.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play pp. 11–12 lists scoring objectives as the first status-phase step and describes public and secret objective scoring.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Some objectives can be scored during the action phase when their timing specifies that phase.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 12 explains the action-phase timing exception for objectives.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The status phase also reveals a public objective, which changes the available scoring landscape for later rounds.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 11 lists revealing a public objective as a status-phase step.
  Confidence: high
