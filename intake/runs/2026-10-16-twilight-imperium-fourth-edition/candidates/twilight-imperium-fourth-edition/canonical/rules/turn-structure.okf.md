## Turn structure

- Claim: In the strategy phase, players choose one strategy card in speaker order, and a chosen card is unavailable to other players that round.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 8 says each player chooses one strategy card and that a chosen card is not available to other players during the round.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The action phase proceeds in initiative order and repeats until all players have passed.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play pp. 8–12 explains that players take turns in initiative order and the action phase continues until each player has passed.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A strategy card supplies a primary ability to its picker and a secondary ability that other players may resolve.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play pp. 8–10 describes the primary and secondary abilities and the opportunity for other players to resolve the secondary ability.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: The status phase includes scoring objectives, revealing a public objective, drawing action cards, repairing units, and returning strategy cards.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 11 lists those status-phase steps in order.
  Confidence: high
