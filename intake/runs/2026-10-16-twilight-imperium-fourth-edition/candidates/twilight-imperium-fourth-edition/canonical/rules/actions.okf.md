## Actions

- Claim: A tactical action activates a system, permits movement, resolves combat when opposing units meet, permits invasion, and can produce units.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play pp. 9 and 13–16 list activation, movement, combat, invasion, and production as the tactical-action sequence.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: A strategic action uses the primary ability of the chosen strategy card, after which other players may resolve that card's secondary ability.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play pp. 8–10 defines the strategic action and its primary/secondary resolution.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Component actions are granted by faction sheets, technologies, or action cards.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play p. 24's quick reference identifies component actions as actions granted by those components.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Space combat and ground combat use the units' combat values and dice, with hits assigned through the combat procedure.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Learn to Play pp. 14–16 describes combat resolution and the use of combat rolls for ships and ground forces.
  Confidence: high
