## Summary

War of the Ring: Second Edition is a 2012 Ares Games physical tabletop game for the asymmetric struggle between the Free Peoples and the Shadow. It combines dice-driven action selection, area movement and control, hidden Fellowship movement, hand management, and card-driven conflict.

## Rules / Mechanics

Players roll action dice, alternate resolving available action icons, move armies across a Middle-earth map, play event cards, and manage the hidden Fellowship track. The Shadow hunts the Fellowship while both sides pursue military and Ring-related victory routes.

## Player friction
