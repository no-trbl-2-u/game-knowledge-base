## Summary

A round proceeds through recovery and card draw, the Fellowship phase, Hunt allocation, action-die rolling, alternating action resolution, and a victory check.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The round has six operational phases before the next round begins.
  Source: src-001, src-002
  Evidence: The official turn sequence and the independent review both enumerate recovery/card draw, Fellowship, Hunt allocation, action roll, action resolution, and victory check.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Action resolution alternates between players rather than resolving one player's complete turn in isolation.
  Source: src-001, src-002
  Evidence: The official action procedure and review describe players taking turns activating available dice during the action-resolution phase.
  Confidence: high
