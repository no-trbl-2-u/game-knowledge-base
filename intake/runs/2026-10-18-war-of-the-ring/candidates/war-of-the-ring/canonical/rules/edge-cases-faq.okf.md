## Summary

The Fellowship is represented by a hidden progress track until it is revealed. Moving it increases the risk of a Hunt, while Ring corruption creates a separate loss route. The full exception matrix remains in the official rulebook.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The Free Peoples can move the Fellowship without moving its visible figure one space at a time; revelation later resolves the hidden progress.
  Source: src-014, src-002
  Evidence: The official Fellowship page image and Meeple Mountain review describe a hidden Fellowship track that is converted into visible movement when revealed.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Hunt results can create Fellowship damage or corruption, and corruption is dangerous because it can immediately give the Shadow the Ring victory.
  Source: src-015, src-016, src-017, src-002
  Evidence: The official Hunt and Corruption page images and independent review describe Hunt draws, damage mitigation, and corruption as the Ring-bearers' loss pressure.
  Confidence: high
