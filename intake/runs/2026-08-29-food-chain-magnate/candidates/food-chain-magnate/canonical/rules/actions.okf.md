## Summary

Employee cards provide the productive actions: cooks make food, buyers gather drinks, managers place restaurants, developers add houses or gardens, and marketeers create demand.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: Kitchen staff and buyers collect food and drinks into a shared supply available to the player's restaurants.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Page 10 says earned food and drinks remain in front of the player and are available to all restaurants in that chain.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Restaurant placement and movement depend on manager cards and can open or immediately activate locations.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Page 10 distinguishes a local manager's range-limited “COMING SOON” placement from a regional manager's immediate placement or movement.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Marketing campaigns advertise a selected good, run for a stated duration, and create demand counters on houses they reach.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Pages 9 and 12 describe campaign types, duration, advertised goods, busy marketeers, and demand-token placement.
  Confidence: high
