## Summary

Money earned at dinnertime is the scoring currency. Sales depend on satisfying complete house demand, while tips, bonuses, prices, gardens, and CFO effects modify income. The second bank break ends the game.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A house can buy from a chain only when that chain can provide the complete food-and-drink demand and has a connected restaurant.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Page 11 requires a connected chain to provide all requested goods before competition compares price and distance.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Waitresses provide cash after house processing, and a CFO can add a 50 percent bonus to cash earned that turn.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Page 11 states that each waitress yields cash and that a CFO adds a 50 percent bonus to cash including waitress tips.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: When the bank breaks a second time, the game ends at the end of dinnertime and the richest player wins.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Pages 4 and 11 state the two-break end condition and richest-player win condition.
  Confidence: high
