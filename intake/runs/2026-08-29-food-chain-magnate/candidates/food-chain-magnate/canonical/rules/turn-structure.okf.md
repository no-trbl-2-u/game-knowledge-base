## Summary

A turn consists of restructuring, recruiting, getting food and drinks, dinnertime, payday, marketing campaigns, and cleanup. The next turn begins after cleanup.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: In restructuring, players simultaneously choose which employees are at work and then place those cards into a company structure.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Page 7 says employees are selected face down, revealed simultaneously, and placed into a pyramid in later turns.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Dinnertime processes houses by printed number and resolves which chain can fulfill their complete demand.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Page 11 says dinnertime starts with the lowest house number and checks whether a connected chain can deliver all food and drinks wanted.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Cleanup returns company and beach cards to hand, flips new restaurants active, and starts the next turn.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: Page 12 lists the cleanup returns, restaurant activation, and next-turn transition.
  Confidence: high
