## Summary

Terraforming Mars is a 2016 competitive engine-building tableau game in which corporations develop projects while collectively advancing Mars's temperature, oxygen, and ocean parameters. It is a physical base game from FryxGames, with a solo mode and a published 1–5 player range.

## Rules / Mechanics

Players draft and buy project cards, pay to add them to personal tableaux, place tiles on the shared Mars map, and convert resources through card effects. The shared parameter track makes the players cooperate on the planet's transformation while the scoring system remains competitive.
