## Summary

Independent reception presents Terraforming Mars as a strong engine-building and tableau game with substantial card variety, but it also identifies learning and pacing costs.

## Player friction

- Claim: A full five-player game can drag, according to the independent review discussion.
  Source: src-003
  Evidence: The review's multiplayer exchange calls larger counts, especially five players, a considerable downside because the game can drag.
  Confidence: high

## Better-if opportunities

- onboarding: make the first generation teach the card economy and global tracks in a more staged order.
- downtime: offer a planning aid for tableau-heavy turns at larger counts.
- strategic-depth: preserve card variety while making strategic signals easier to read.
