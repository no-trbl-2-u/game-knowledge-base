## Summary

On a turn, a player may perform one or two actions, including playing a project, using an action on a blue card, claiming a milestone, funding an award, placing a greenery or ocean tile, or converting resources through permitted effects.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: A player may take one or two actions on their turn and may pass after taking one action.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 7–10 define the one-or-two-action player turn and list the action families.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Project cards carry costs and effects that can alter production, resources, tiles, parameters, or points.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 6–10 explain project-card payment, tags, effects, and the action types that use them.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The two-action turn creates a compact action queue: a player can combine a board action with a card action, or spend both actions on a stronger sequence if the rules permit. Blue-card actions and resource conversion make tableau growth operational rather than merely decorative.
