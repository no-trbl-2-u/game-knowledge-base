## Summary

The game ends when temperature, oxygen, and ocean coverage all reach their final levels. Players then total points from several public and private sources.

## Source-backed facts

- Claim: The shared terraforming objective is complete only when all three global parameters reach their endpoint.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 1–2 and 11 state that temperature, oxygen, and oceans must all be completed before the game ends.
  Confidence: high

- Claim: Final scoring includes the player track, greenery and city placement, cards, milestones, awards, and remaining resources according to the rulebook's scoring procedure.
  Source: src-002
  Evidence: Rulebook pp. 11–13 provide the end-game scoring sequence and the point sources it counts.
  Confidence: high

## Rules / Mechanics

The global tracks act as a shared race clock, while milestones and awards add contested scoring routes before final tableau and map scoring. That structure lets a player advance the common objective without making every point a direct cooperation reward.
